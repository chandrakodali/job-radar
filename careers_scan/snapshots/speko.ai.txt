01 — the problem
Why this matters
Voice AI is exploding. New models ship every week, and no provider is best at everything — especially outside English. So every team picks a provider, hopes it works, and starts over next quarter.
Every provider says their model is the answer. We don't. Speko stays neutral: we send each call to whatever actually works, and we prove it in public. Every company building voice agents will need this. We want to be it.

03 — operating system
How we work
These aren't posters. This is how we hire, review, and ship.
Own the result.
"Done" doesn't mean "I finished my part." It means it works for the customer, and you checked. If it breaks later, it's still yours.
Make it work for real.
A demo that works once means almost nothing. Real voice has to handle accents, noise, slow networks, and provider outages. We don't celebrate a demo until we know what breaks in production.
Truth over ego.
What's true matters more than who's right. Use data, not opinions. Fix your own bug before you blame the provider. Share bad news early — hiding a problem is worse than the problem.
Learn fast.
New things ship every week, and you won't know all of them. That's fine. We care how fast you learn and how much you care — not how long your resume is.
Behavior is everything.
Skills can be taught. How you show up cannot. We hire people who ship, own their work, stay curious, and fix things without being asked.
