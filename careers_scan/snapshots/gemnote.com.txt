Just a few reasons why we’re a great place to work:
(01) The more backgrounds, the merrier
Diverse and global team
Diversity is in our DNA and a cornerstone of our culture. Our global team spans over 10 countries (and counting)!
(02) We are anti-burnout and always encourage PTO
Flexible time off
We are anti-burnout and pro-PTO. We actively encourage our employees to take time off and return rejuvenated. Because honestly, you can’t pour from an empty cup, now, can you?
(03) Because health is wealth
Health coverage
We thoroughly support all our full-time employees with comprehensive health, dental and vision insurance options.
The goal is simple: make healthcare as stress-free as possible.
(04) A stipend to set you up at home
Home office credit
Since our workforce is remote, we prioritize making our team feel comfortable and equipped for success. Cue: a generous stipend to create your ideal home office setup!
(05) Save for your future
401(k) options
Adulting is hard but saving shouldn’t be. We offer 401(K) options, so you can invest in your future now. If it’s important to you, it’s important to us.
(06) Very handy during the holidays
Employee discounts
Our employees save up to 50% off manufacturers’ suggested retail prices on trending and high-quality items. Trust us — all those deals, deals, (and, well, some more) deals come in super handy during the holidays.
(07) You bet we do that right
Company merch
This feels natural. We give away the best merch on the block because it's our M.O. Basically, we set the example, so we've got to do it right.
(08) That actually build lasting bonds
Remote team activities
Remote by design. Connected by purpose. Our team spans different cities, time zones, and backgrounds, but we’re united by our mission. Being remote doesn’t mean being distant it means we can move fast, stay flexible, and bring the right people to the table no matter where they’re based.
(09) Roll up your sleeves
Proactive learning & development
Learning is at the heart of our culture. We regularly invite experts to help us continuously build and grow our skills. Around here, if there’s room for improvement, we eagerly roll up our sleeves and get to work.
