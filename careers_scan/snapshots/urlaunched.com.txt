FAQ
How long will it take to hear back from a recruiter after applying?
A recruiter should contact you within 3 business days after applying. If you don’t hear from us, unfortunately, we don’t have any open relevant positions at this time.
What are the mandatory stages of the hiring process?
Screening Interview with a recruiter, Culture Fit stage with a recruiter, and a Technical interview with the hiring manager.
Where can I expect to be contacted after submitting my resume?
If you’ve provided a Telegram handle or phone number, we’ll reach out there. Additionally, we may contact you via Email and LinkedIn.
How long does the hiring process take?
The recruiter will provide feedback within a week after the first and second stages, and within 2 weeks after the technical interview.
Where is the company office located?
The company currently operates in a fully remote work format.
