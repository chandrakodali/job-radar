How we work
Four things that are true here.
Customers in the room
Engineers sit on sandbox calls and see the process a client is trying to automate. Product decisions come from that, not from a roadmap written in isolation.
Ship every week
The platform releases in one-week cycles tracked in the open; the October 2026 release notes are a good picture of the pace.
Honest by default
We publish the limits of the product on its own pages. The same applies inside: say what is not working, early.
Small team, real ownership
A few people own whole workstreams — AI Workflows, documents, infrastructure, Studio. Whoever sees the problem owns the fix.
