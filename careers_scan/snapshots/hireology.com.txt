The Heart of Hireology: Our Core Values
Core Value
Create Wow Moments
We believe in going beyond the baseline to create meaningful, lasting impressions. By exceeding expectations—for customers, teammates, or partners—we forge stronger relationships built on trust, respect, and mutual success. We aim to leave a positive impact every step of the way.
Eager to Improve
No one knows everything about their role, and that’s okay. At Hireology, we value learning and growth, actively seeking feedback from our customers and teammates. An open mind and a willingness to grow are what drive us forward together.
Own the Result
We take pride in being accountable to ourselves, our team, and our customers. By owning our results, we celebrate wins together and take responsibility for setbacks, using them as opportunities to grow. This mindset fosters trust, collaboration, and success.
No Assholes
We value trust and respect in every interaction. Disrespectful behavior has no place at Hireology, and we maintain a zero-tolerance approach to ensure a positive and supportive environment for everyone. Our focus remains on innovation, collaboration, and achieving our shared goals in a healthy and supportive atmosphere.
Pathological Optimism
Relentless optimism drives success. We understand the challenges of building from the ground up, from competitors to inefficiencies. Yet, we stay focused, adapt, and persevere with an unwavering commitment to achieving our goals.
You’ll Fit Right In
At Hireology, we know that great teams are built by bringing together unique perspectives, backgrounds, and personalities. When you join us, you’ll be welcomed just as you are—supported by collaborative colleagues who care. We’re passionate about helping each other grow and making work feel meaningful (and fun!) every day.
