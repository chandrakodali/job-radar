How it works
From one sentence to a running automation
1. Describe it
Tell Modern the job the way you would tell a new hire. It picks the trigger, the steps and the apps, and you can change any of it in plain English.
2. Review it
Run a test with every outside write held back. Choose what needs your sign-off, like anything over $10,000 or any new supplier.
3. It runs
Modern runs the job on schedule or when the trigger fires. Every run has a full history, so your team and your auditors can see what happened.
