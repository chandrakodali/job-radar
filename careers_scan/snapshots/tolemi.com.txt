Working at Tolemi
Trust & Autonomy
We work hard to maintain a high level of trust and autonomy. Because we trust one another, we can be flexible about how, when, and where the work gets done.
Room to Grow
We empower everyone to grow in the directions they're interested in and energized by, and we often promote from within.
Quarterly Onsites
Four times a year, we fly the whole company to Boston for three days together in the office to collaborate, set goals, review the quarter, and have fun together.
A Sustainable Pace
We're highly focused and we work hard, and we make sure everyone gets restorative time so they can live and work sustainably.
Benefits & Perks
Our engineering team at breakfast before work in Barcelona
Work From Anywhere
Our team works from across the country and sometimes the world. Get your work done in whatever environment suits you and on your own schedule, as long as you're available during Eastern hours. We've met up to work in Spain, and some of us spend extended stretches working from places we're visiting.
A Downtown Boston Office
For those in the Boston area, our well-appointed downtown office has snacks, workstations, and meeting rooms. Those of us on site often head out to lunch together.
Health & Wellness
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage, plus gym membership reimbursement.
Unlimited PTO
Take the time you need to rest and recharge. We trust our team to balance great work with real time off.
Home Office Stipend
A yearly stipend to set up a home workspace that works for you.
Your Choice of Workstation
Every employee picks their own machine: Mac, Windows, or Linux.

Summer Internships
Our Internship Program
Each summer we host highly motivated rising seniors with experience and interest in housing issues.
Data Engineering
Join the Data team that keeps city data flowing into Tolemi's products. Use SQL to map large datasets to a central schema, maintain the scripts that pull data from websites and APIs, and contribute to our data standardization work.
Data Science
Join our small Insights & Data Science team to develop new cross-city insights. Explore large datasets, build rule systems or machine learning models, and own an insight project from start to finish over the summer.
Software Engineering
Work alongside our engineers on the software local governments use every day, writing and testing production code and shipping real improvements over the summer.
Marketing
Help tell the story of how local governments use data to tackle housing issues, from customer spotlights and case studies to content for our website.
Some of our interns have come back to work with us, while others have gone on to academic research or work in government.
