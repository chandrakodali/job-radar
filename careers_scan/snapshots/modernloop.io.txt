High-volume hiring comes in two shapes
Volume means something different in a store than in a sales team. Before you compare high-volume recruiting tools, decide which shape your hiring takes, or whether you have both.
Corporate hiring at volume
Typical roles
Sales, customer service, account management and other salaried roles, often hired in waves
The interviews
A recruiter screen, then interviews with hiring managers and sometimes a panel
What breaks first
Hiring-manager calendars, uneven interviewer load and reschedules
How candidates book
Self-scheduling links into recruiter and hiring-manager availability, email and calendar invites, and a branded Candidate Portal
What to buy
Interview scheduling software connected to your ATS, such as ModernLoop
Hourly and frontline hiring
Store, restaurant, warehouse and shift roles, hired continuously
Often one short conversation with a store or shift manager
Speed to first contact, and candidates who drop out before anyone replies
Usually a chat or text conversation that screens and books in one flow
A conversational hiring tool built for hourly roles
Where conversational hourly tools fit
Searching for Paradox interview scheduling or Paradox alternatives? Start with the shape of your hiring, not a vendor list.
Conversational recruiting tools are designed around a chat with the candidate, which suits hourly roles; Paradox is one of the best-known names in that category. ModernLoop is designed around interview calendars, which suits roles where candidates meet recruiters, hiring managers and panels.
Mostly hourly hiring? If most of your hires are store, shift or warehouse roles with one short interview each, a conversational hourly tool is built for that job.
Mostly corporate hiring? If candidates meet recruiters, hiring managers and panels , the bottleneck is interview scheduling. That’s where ModernLoop fits.
Both? Run two tools. Send frontline roles through the conversational tool, and anything with a hiring manager or a panel through ModernLoop. Keep your ATS as the system of record for both.
AI-led first interviews. Taylor AI , ModernLoop’s AI recruiter, interviews candidates 24/7, at any volume. It’s a separate product from ModernLoop’s interview scheduling.
