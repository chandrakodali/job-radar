Working at Checkly
Most of our team works remotely from 16+ countries, with flexible working hours. We’ve got a sales team in our NYC office, who are great at balancing sync working with each other and customers, and async working with the rest of the team.
You won't be happy working here if...
…you need meetings to communicate and make decisions. We write proposals on Notion, keep track of our work in tickets, and share updates with the team in Slack.
…you're not up for a challenge. We need people with focus, speed and adaptability to join us in changing the monitoring and observability world with our as-code approach.
…you say "that's not my job". We are a relatively small team and pride ourselves helping each other to reach our goals. We win together.
…you're uncomfortable with transparency. We believe that good collaboration, alignment across teams, and achieving goals comes from a high level of transparency.
…you don't assume good intentions. We come from many different countries, cultures and backgrounds. For most of us, English is not our native language, and a lot can get lost in written communication. Assuming good intentions is essential to good collaboration.
