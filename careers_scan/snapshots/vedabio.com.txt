Operations
Operations Manager, Consumables & Reagents
San Diego, CA
Apply Now
VedaBio is developing an amplification-free molecular detection platform for point-of-care diagnostics: a fully automated system with an instrument, single-use consumables, and customer-facing software. As we move from prototyping into development and commercialization, manufacturing is a core capability and key to our success.
The Operations Manager, Consumables & Reagents owns day-to-day reagent and component operations across sourcing, incoming QC, stability, inventory, and cartridge assembly. In this hands-on role you will translate proof-of-concept R&D builds into reliable, scalable operations, ensure on-time material readiness, and serve as the operational interface with CDMOs and manufacturing partners, working closely with Manufacturing Engineering, Quality, and R&D. As demand grows, you will hire and develop a small operations team. This is an on-site role in San Diego.
Base salary range: $135,000 to $170,000 per year.

Engineering
Staff / Senior Staff Engineer, Manufacturing & Automation
VedaBio is developing an amplification-free molecular detection platform for point-of-care diagnostics: a fully automated system with an instrument, single-use consumables, and customer-facing software. As we move from R&D into development and commercialization, manufacturing is a core capability and key to our success.
We are looking for a hands-on, senior individual-contributor engineer to own our growing network of international automation suppliers and the associated manufacturing processes. You will write the equipment requirements, challenge supplier designs, run the FAT, debug the line, and drive the improvements that get yield, throughput, and cost where they need to be as we scale consumable production from a few thousand to millions of parts per year. Level will be determined by depth of experience. This is an on-site role in San Diego.
Base salary range: $165,000 to $200,000 per year.
