How to join us
Send application
Upload your CV, done! No cover letter needed. We’ll have a look and get back to you as soon as possible. Please send us your CV in English if the job advertisement is also in English.
First Step
Time to introduce yourself! Answer 3 short questions via video (using our Cammio tool). Depending on the job, a brief call with HR may be arranged instead.
Technical interview(s)
You get to know the department and HR; we learn more about your skills and motivation. You may be asked to complete a small task.
Optional: Meet the team
Meet colleagues from your future team and see if you’re a good fit.
Is it a match?
If both sides agree, you will receive the job offer, the contract and all the information you need for onboarding. Welcome!
EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW
BEFORE YOU START
What application documents do I need to submit?
A detailed CV with an overview of your previous areas of responsibility is sufficient for us. We are an international team, so please send us your CV in English if the job advertisement is also in English.
Depending on the position (for example, in the Editorial department), we may also request work samples – you will find this information in the job advertisement. You can send any application documents you may have forgotten to include to [email protected] .
When can I expect a response to my application?
We will do our best to review your documents as quickly as possible. As we receive a number of applications and carefully review each one, this can sometimes take a while, but usually no longer than 14 days. Thank you for your patience!
No confirmation of receipt (within 30 minutes)? Please check that your e-mail address is spelled correctly and that your attached files are not too large (max. 10 MB). Then just try again!
Radio silence after confirmation of receipt? Just send us a quick message at [email protected] if you haven’t heard anything for more than 14 days.
How does the pre-recorded video interview work?
This step helps us to get to know you a little better before the actual job interview as well as get a first impression of your personality and skills. For some tech positions, we conduct a telephone interview instead.
Here’s how it works:
You answer three questions – wherever you want, as chill as you want – using your smartphone or laptop.
The video interview is asynchronous. This means you can record your answers whenever it suits you best – in the morning with your first cup of coffee or in the evening after work. You set the pace!
More information
How do I prepare for the application process?
Get an overview of our company and our Company Principles to see who we are and how we work.
Read the job description carefully again beforehand. First and foremost, think about how you can contribute to our team and your position, and what you would like to tell us about yourself. Above all, be yourself. We want to get to know you as you really are. The rest will follow naturally.
Please feel free to put together all your questions in advance. We will discuss them during the application process.
Will the interview be conducted virtually or on site?
All our processes are designed for a remote setting, including our job interviews. Only interviews for the warehouse take place on site.
We use Google Meet for our virtual calls. Ensure that you are in a quiet environment for our chat. Please check in advance that your audio, microphone and webcam are working.
How can I be notified about recently posted jobs?
Simply sign up for our Job-Alert . This will notify you as soon as a new job is published in your selected field.
Can and should I apply for several jobs?
If you find several suitable job vacancies, you have the option of applying for more than one. Please note that our HR team can see in the system how many jobs you have applied for. If you send in loads of applications, people might wonder if you’re really serious. We review each application individually; therefore, you may receive feedback from different HR contacts.
Kaufland e-commerce, Kaufland Global Marketplace and the Kaufland online marketplaces explained. What is the story behind them and how are the terms related?
Kaufland e-commerce – this refers to us as a company and employer.
Kaufland Global Marketplace is our B2B brand, which is aimed directly at online sellers, manufacturers, brands and partners. KGM is the comprehensive all-in-one solution that enables our partners to sell on all our international Kaufland marketplaces with just one registration.
The Kaufland marketplaces are not just another brand, but the product itself – the international marketplaces where our customers shop.
For projects such as the Kaufland app and the Kaufland Card, we work closely with our colleagues at Kaufland (the brick-and-mortar locations) , but as an independent tech company, we have our own processes and working methods.
What happens after the contract is signed?
Before you start, our HR team will send you all the important information you need to order your hardware and prepare for your first day. This begins with our digital Welcome Day for all newbies starting in the respective month. There you will find out everything you need to know about our culture, history, policies and much more.
Afterwards, you will meet your team and the buddy assigned to you.
