Hub Deploy / Hub Labs
Data, operators and robots, in the real world.
Robot foundation models learn physical behavior from human demonstration, and real-world data is the bottleneck. Through Hub Deploy, we provide that data to AI and robotics companies, drawing on 7,300+ verified commercial sites across 100+ industries, more than 860K KYC'd unique operators and 680+ catalogued tasks. We also train operators and deploy robots on real sites, from warehouses and kitchens to retail floors and workshops.
Hub Labs studies which data, and which deployments, make robots learn fastest. We run collection, processing, QA and annotation in-house, on platforms we build ourselves. We are backed by Y Combinator (Spring 26) and headquartered in San Francisco.

The team
The people behind Hub.
Our field operators, reviewers, engineers and researchers work across San Francisco, France and LATAM. The core team is small by design, and you work directly with the founders. Compensation, location and language requirements differ by role and are listed on each job. Some roles are remote-friendly, while others require in-person work in San Francisco or Toulouse.
