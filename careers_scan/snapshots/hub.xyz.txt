Capturing physical experience for embodied AI
Our mission is to bring embodied AGI closer through real-world data and research. We provide training data to frontier AI labs and robotics companies: egocentric RGB-D, teleoperation, hand-tracking and tactile datasets, captured, enriched, annotated and quality-checked in-house, end to end.
Our global network includes 150,000+ active contributors and 730+ commercial sites across 150 countries, running on platforms we build ourselves rather than assembled SaaS. We are based in San Francisco, backed by Y Combinator, and our team is spread across SF and Europe.
We keep our core team small by design. You will work directly with the founders and own systems, deals or operations end to end. Compensation, location and language requirements differ by role and are listed on each job.
