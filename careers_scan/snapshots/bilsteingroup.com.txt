We owe our success to the special commitment of our globally active employees.
We attach great importance to diversity and difference in origin, thinking and action.
Working at the bilstein group
I joined the bilstein group in 2009.
First as a cutting machine operator, later as an industrial foreman. In 2017 I took over the function of team leader in production control. As Head of Production, I am responsible for the manufacturing area of bilstein group Engineering.
What makes the bilstein group so special for me as an employee? Above all, the openness for investments and the associated constant growth. The company has always offered me exciting prospects for the future and career opportunities.
Kamil Domagala
I joined the bilstein group in 2010. Due to my technical training I started as a Technical Consultant. After that I was a Sales Developer in the Middle East. Since 2014, I have been a Sales Manager responsible for: Iraq, Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Egypt, Libya and Sudan.
At the bilstein group I particularly appreciate the family cohesion that cannot be taken for granted in such a large company.
Sameh Eskander
I have been with bilstein group Engineering for 20 years.
I started in the production area. After successful further training I changed to CNC programming, and shortly afterwards to production control.
Since 2015 I have been working in the area of production planning and control (PPS) and I am also in charge of the logistics area of bilstein group Engineering.
The bilstein group is a company that does not stand still. This has always offered me new, exciting perspectives for my professional development.
Claus Hoffmann
I have been working in the bilstein group's logistics department for about two years now. I started out in packaging as a commercial production assistant. After about a year I got the chance to switch to the commercial area of logistics. I am currently the logistical contact for parts lists and work plan changes.
For me, the company distinguishes itself by offering its employees very good opportunities to prove their own potential and to constantly develop themselves further.
Lay An Joy Mordeno
