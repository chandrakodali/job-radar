About Vector Legal
Vector Legal serves startups from formation through Series B and beyond. Most work is billed hourly, at about half the cost of Big Law, because the firm's software takes the first pass and attorneys spend their time on judgment. Outcome-based pricing is available where it fits, and fast-moving teams can retain the firm monthly as fractional general counsel. Vector Legal is backed by Y Combinator, Base10, Palm Drive Capital, Amino Capital, and Transpose Platform. Headquartered in San Francisco, California.
Who provides the legal services
Legal services are provided by Vector Legal, P.C., a licensed California law firm. The VectorOS platform is built by Vector Systems, PBC. Software-generated drafts and findings are reviewed by licensed attorneys and are not legal advice on their own.
