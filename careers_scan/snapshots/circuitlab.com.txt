Careers at CircuitLab
CircuitLab is a unique blend of web startup and EDA software developer:
a mix of a fresh, fast development atmosphere within an enormous and
deeply technical engineering field. We're working to change the way
the $2+ trillion consumer and industrial electronics industries design
and manufacture the electronic assemblies that are increasingly
intertwined in our lives.
Our engineers are equally comfortable with
JavaScript and semiconductor device models, HTML5 and Verilog. Our team
works from Silicon Valley, California — the epicenter of the electronics
industry as well as the internet industry — bridging together the
hardware and software generations of Silicon Valley.
