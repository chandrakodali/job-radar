How we work
The principles
that run the company.
Eight principles that shape how we hire, promote, and ship.
Automation-first
We're capable of 10× more by automating where possible.
In practice — If you've done it twice by hand, the third time it's a script.
Measure what matters
We steer by KPIs and experiments — not gut feel or the loudest voice in the room.
In practice — Any meaningful claim — about users, performance, or pricing — arrives with a number, or it doesn't ship.
Results over optics
We value impact to the exclusion of optics and politics.
In practice — The person who fixed the problem owns the credit, not the one who wrote the update doc.
Users first
Do what's right for the user, and everything else follows.
In practice — When a feature request conflicts with simplicity for the user, simplicity wins.
Transparent by default
We share and communicate openly unless there's a specific reason not to.
In practice — Roadmap, board decks, and customer escalations are visible internally by default.
Scrappy
We do more with less wherever possible.
In practice — We'd rather ship the cheap version this week than the polished version next quarter.
Climate leaders by example
A greentech company must walk its own talk.
In practice — We optimize our own footprint — flights, hosting, hardware lifecycle — not just our customers'.
Allergic to bureaucracy
Process exists to serve outcomes, not the other way around.
In practice — If a meeting, doc, or approval step isn't earning its keep, we kill it.
You belong at HeyCharge by embodying these values — not by where you come from, how you look, or when you were born.
