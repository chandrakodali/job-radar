Draftbit is an app builder that runs in your browser. You describe the app you want, and an AI agent (Claude Code or OpenAI Codex) builds a first version in real code while you watch. Questions and answers
Do I have to buy AI credits?
No. Most AI app builders make their money by selling you the AI through credits. In Draftbit you can sign in with the Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already pay for, on every plan, so the agent runs on that instead of on credits. We sell credits too, for anyone who'd rather not connect an account, but you don't have to buy them.
Is Draftbit a drag and drop app builder?
Yes, when you want it to be. The agent is usually the fastest way to build a whole screen or set up sign-in, but for a small, precise change it's often quicker to do it yourself. So you can click the exact element you want to change in the live preview and adjust its layout, colors, or text, or drag a new component into place. The visual editor reads and writes your app's real code, which means the agent sees every change you make and you see every change it makes.
Are the mobile apps native?
Yes. All of the code that Draftbit produces for mobile apps is based on React Native and Expo, so one codebase gives you an iOS app and an Android app built from the phone's native interface components. It isn't a website wrapped in an app shell, which is why your app can use the camera, location, push notifications, and other device features.
Who is Draftbit for?
We built it for people who have an idea and don't have a development team: startup founders, business owners who want an app for their customers or their team, and anyone who wants to build something of their own. We're not the right fit for every project. If you're an experienced developer who wants your own local setup, if your app has to be written separately in Swift and Kotlin, or if you need a desktop app, you'll be better off starting somewhere else.
How does Draftbit compare with other ways to build an app?
It depends on what you'd use instead. Most AI app builders charge for the AI itself, and in Draftbit you can use the Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already have. Visual builders that keep your app on their platform let you drag and drop, and you can do that in Draftbit too, but the app is ordinary code that belongs to you, so you can export it and keep working on it anywhere. And if you were going to hire an agency or a developer, you can try building it yourself first, for free, and have a working first version in minutes.
Can someone help if I need it?
Yes. AI isn't perfect, and sometimes a bug is stubborn or a launch gets messy. When that happens you can talk to Draftbit Experts, the designers and developers on our own team, who can fix the one thing that's in your way or take over the whole build. A real person reads what you send and replies with a real next step.
