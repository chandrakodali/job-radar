A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS
Before your first test.
Discuss your workflow
Why verify before the interview?
Verification at application time gives recruiters evidence before they invest in interviews. Start with one or two role-relevant claims, such as a required qualification or a period of employment, then review the results alongside the application.
Can we verify candidates in different countries?
Reclaim can retrieve evidence from supported source accounts across countries. Coverage depends on the specific employer, institution, or platform and the fields it exposes. Bring the sources your candidates use so we can confirm the checks you need.
Is early verification affordable at application volume?
Automated source checks and volume pricing let you design a low-cost screening step around specific claims. Discuss your application volume, sources, and required fields with us to price the workflow.
Does this replace all background checks?
Reclaim supplies evidence for specific claims. Your team decides which additional identity, reference, assessment, or background checks a role needs. Candidates who cannot complete a check should have a way to provide alternative evidence.
