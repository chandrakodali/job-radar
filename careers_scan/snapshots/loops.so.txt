What we work on
Loops is the email platform for software companies. Chris and Adam started it in 2022, and Framer, Linear, Perplexity and Granola are among its customers today.
A few recent projects: we moved production to AWS over six months with zero downtime, paused deploys over Black Friday so customer campaigns went out on time, and worked out why Gmail flags youtu.be links in tracked emails.
How we work
Small and remote
Eleven people, fully remote. We hire 1 to 3 people a year.
Ship daily
We ship to production throughout the week, Fridays included. Engineers get their own pull requests tested, approved and deployed.
Async by default
We communicate asynchronously and trust each other to manage time and attention.
Write it down
We write things down, so context is easy to find later. Even our Black Friday code freeze started as a written RFC.
Close to customers
Everyone stays close to customers. Framer, Cosmos and Runable each have a shared Slack channel with the team.
Keep it simple
We keep the product simple. Cosmos says Loops has “everything we need without the bloat.”
