Our Story
We're a team of engineers, legal professionals, and technologists supported by a network of clinical and legal experts, with decades of experience.
Our Mission
Every life leaves a record. Thousands of pages, written by many hands and read in full by almost no one. The decisions that rest on it are among the heaviest a person faces: whether they were harmed, what they are owed, what should happen next.
Elithia exists so that no such decision is made on a page nobody read.
We read the whole record. We put it in order. We show where every fact came from, so the people who decide can check our work and stand behind their own.
We began with the hardest cases. We do not intend to stop there.
