Our values
At HyperionDev, our mission is simple: to help people start meaningful careers in tech without spending
years or thousands of dollars. We’re building a team that’s passionate about making that possible,
experts
who love what they do and do it well. With the right people and a great work environment, we empower
learners to become job-ready in just three to six months through our online and on-site coding bootcamps
and
1-on-1 mentorship.
Purpose first
We put purpose first and build our integrity on it.
What we do is greater than ourselves. We are paving new roads to bring new technology into
the
world.
We change lives.
Progress always
We always progress towards mastery.
We are always learning, improving, collaborating, and setting new standards. We write code
right.
Problem solving
We solve problems, even when constrained.
We are scrappy and maximise our resources. We are entrepreneurial and innovative under
pressure. We do not break or give up.
People forward
We respect people and their potential.
People are the heart of what we do and why we do it. We talk as much as we listen, and know
the value in every person above technology.
