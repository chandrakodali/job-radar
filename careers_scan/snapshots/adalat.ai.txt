Build the OS for Justice Systems
India's courts carry over 50 million pending cases . Adalat AI is solving this by reimagining judicial productivity from the ground up.
We're building specialized AI models that understand legal nuances, jargon, and dialects down to the district level. We're creating workflows that streamline and accelerate entire court processes, making every judge and clerk radically more productive.
Our mission is ambitious but clear: Timely justice for all.
If you're serious about using your technical depth—whether you're a designer, engineer, researcher or generalist—to tackle one of society's most pressing problems, we want to build this with you. explore our open roles →
Why Work With Us?
A Mission That Matters
Every commit chips away at the world’s largest case backlog. Your day job directly delivers timely justice.
Real-World Impact
Features go live in active courtrooms—under fluorescent lights, shaky 2G networks, and all. You’ll watch metrics (and lives) move.
Unmatched Learning & Growth
Engineers publish papers, researchers ship code, and judges join design reviews. There’s no faster way to level up.
Build a Network That Counts
Work hand-in-hand with domain experts—senior judges, linguists, policy makers—and open-source your wins to a global AI community.
Work Where You Thrive
We’re 100 % remote inside India. Live anywhere, keep flexible hours, and regroup each quarter for roadmap jams, demos, and real chai.
Build What Doesn't Exist
Your daily work pushes boundaries—speech models trained for Indian courtrooms, UX crafted specifically for judicial workflows, and open-source tools that set global benchmarks. You’ll invent, not just iterate.
No equity, no sushi bar. Our perk is ownership of a problem that actually matters.
When Lines Disappear, Good Things Happen
Code ↔ Courtrooms ↔ Community
Our work doesn’t end at merge. Engineers ship features that go live in real courtrooms — then sit in trainings to see how they’re used. When we solve a hard problem for one state, we often open-source it so others can build on it. We write code with one eye on the judge’s screen, the other on the wider ecosystem.
Product Is a Team Sport
Judges scroll through Figma. Clerks give workflow feedback. Engineers and lawyers brainstorm side-by-side. We don’t build in isolation — we co-design with the justice system. If you join Adalat AI, you’re not just writing code or content — you’re helping shape how justice works. Product isn’t someone else’s job. It’s everyone’s job.
Room to Grow, Space to Contribute
At Adalat AI, people grow by doing. Back-end engineers have led product discussions. Front-end devs have gone deep into backend. Some have published papers, others shaped field deployments. You don’t have to do everything — but if you want to, there’s space. We value what you bring today, and invest in who you’re becoming tomorrow. While you build Adalat AI, it builds you.
