Who we're looking for
Three kinds of people build Nyx.
We don't post a long list of reqs. If one of these is you, show us something you broke or built. It tells us more than a resume.
AI researchers
You understand model architecture and the current frontier deeply enough to reason about where a model will break before you run a single attack — where the training objective, the alignment tuning, and the tool scaffolding leave seams. You use that to formulate the attacks that matter and the defenses that would actually hold.
Product engineers
You're a serious engineer with real infrastructure depth and taste — you know the difference between code that works and code that's right. You're AI-native, working at the edge of what today's tools can do and wiring them into how you work every day, so a small team ships like a large one.
Forward-deployed engineers
You're who we put in front of a customer's security team. You aim Nyx at their hardest agents, turn what it finds into fixes their engineers can ship, and stay technical enough that a red team believes you when you say something's broken.
