At MyDecisive we focus on excellence in hiring people who are great at what they do and creating an environment where talent can thrive. Being part of our team means you'll have a choice of offerings. Because when you feel covered in life, you can focus on doing your best work.
At MDAI we want to empower you to take care of yourself. A bit on our culture and benefits below.
Culture
Remote first
We trust you get your job done and provide the tools that make the logistics work. We also understand life happens. Do what you need to do to take care of you and yours. Prioritize work-life balance in the way that works best for you.
Growth opportunity
You get better outcomes when employees have the information and freedom to make decisions for themselves. We hire unusually responsible people who thrive on this openness and freedom.
Humans first
We're all in this together. Our relationships are grounded in trust, respect, and camaraderie. While every member has different skills, we look for our common strengths that make us better together.
Benefits
Employee stock options
We offer competitive compensation and benefits. As a member of the MyDecisive team you also have the opportunity to benefit from our long term success as our stock price appreciates.
Healthcare
We offer healthcare benefits to manage you and your family health and well-being no matter where you reside. Because medical benefits work differently based on location options that include employer matching funds.
Vacation / Sick time
We are not a 9-5 workday, so we do not have prescribed time off policies either. Observe what is important to you and take time when ill, including when your mind and body need a break.
