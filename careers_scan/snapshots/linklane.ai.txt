What carriers ask us
How do registration and approval work?
Apply on this page with your USDOT or MC number. We read your company from FMCSA, so you only confirm it. We check your authority, insurance and safety record, then you sign our onboarding packet in Highway: the Broker-Carrier Agreement, our payment terms and your W-9. When Highway confirms it, we approve you and email you, and a person here calls to get to know you.
What loads and lanes do you have?
You tell us where you’ll go (anywhere in the lower 48, your region, or set lanes), any states you won’t go to, and when your drivers go home. When we have a load that fits, we send you an offer with the cities, the dates, the freight, the trailer and the all-in rate. You take it or pass.
What equipment do you need?
We book dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck, conestoga, power only, box truck and hotshot loads. Each offer names the trailer and anything special, such as a temperature or team drivers. We don’t move hazmat. The load moves on your own equipment, under your own authority.
What insurance do you require?
Active operating authority with FMCSA, at least a year old, and a certificate of insurance that names LinkLane as holder: $1,000,000 auto liability and $100,000 cargo.
How and when do you pay?
Net 30 from when we receive your invoice with the signed BOL and POD. Send invoices to billing@linklane.ai with the load number on them. If you factor, tell us your factoring company when you set up. Detention: 2 hours free at each stop, then $50 an hour , with in and out times on the BOL.
Are there registration or monthly fees?
No. Applying, the carrier portal and our offers cost you nothing. We pay you the rate on the rate confirmation.
