Researcher
Research Full-time In person · San Francisco $180K – $300K
MadeThis is only as good as the decisions it makes for a business it has never met. You will research how AI agents can plan, act and learn safely over long-running work, and turn that into measurable gains in what MadeThis can do.
Design evaluations for long-running, real-world business tasks, from closing a lead to resolving a dispute
Improve planning, memory and tool use across our agents
Study where people need to stay in the loop and how MadeThis earns their trust
Run experiments quickly and ship what works into the product
Share what you learn with the team and, where it makes sense, with the wider research community
A track record in machine learning, agents, human-computer interaction or a related field, through publications, products or both
Strong experimental design and the engineering skills to run experiments yourself
Curiosity about how small businesses actually operate
A PhD is welcome but not required. What you have built matters more.
