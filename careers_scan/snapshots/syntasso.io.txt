We believe in creating a sustainable work environment so we have a 35 hour week
Our office is based in Leathermarket near London Bridge station and is available to use every day if you choose, but we have a hybrid working policy:
Office Days : Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday
Remote Days : Monday and Friday by default
In practice, how often some are in office varies from once a week to once a month but you need to available and willing to be in the office up to 3 times a week
Fortnightly Team Office Day : Every other Wednesday
We're expecting big things when it comes to the growth of our company and we want everyone to benefit from the growth in value by offering share options
You are entitled to 25 days of annual leave , plus UK public holidays. Additionally, you'll earn one extra day of holiday for each year worked, up to a maximum of 30 days. You can carry forward up to 5 days into the next calendar year and take up to 5 days of unpaid leave if needed
We dedicate every other Friday as a research day for experimentation and skill development to keep Syntasso thriving
We organise one official company social per quarter , with past events including darts and ping pong. Unofficially, team members often gather for drinks after the monthly team office days
Save for your retirement via a workplace pension , with the option to use salary sacrifice; we offer 8% (employer 3%, employee 5%) with the option to increase
Private medical insurance through AXA, with the option to add dependents through salary sacrifice
Childcare can be expensive so we offer workplace nursery benefits which operates on a salary sacrifice scheme
We have a team of avid cyclers and if you are too, you can use the Cycle to Work scheme to save up to 42% on a bike and accessories
