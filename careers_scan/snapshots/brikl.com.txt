Frequently Asked Questions
What is Brikl?
Brikl is the on-demand platform that promotional product distributors run their branded merch on. One login for sourcing, bulk orders, on-demand storefronts, artwork and payment. Source 8,000+ decorated products at wholesale, place bulk orders or launch stores in minutes, and let production and shipping run automatically, with no inventory and no minimums.
What does Brikl cost?
Nothing upfront — no setup fee, no monthly subscription, no per-seat charge. Brikl takes a 3.5% commission on each transaction. Full details on the pricing page.
Can I place bulk orders without building a store?
Yes. Brikl's Order Builder takes bulk orders directly — per-size grids and order sheets on per-unit pricing, with decoration set at the item level and live margin. You don't need to launch a store first.
Do I need to hold inventory?
No. Every product on Brikl is produced on demand. When an end customer places an order, it routes automatically to a decorator for production and direct shipping. You never touch inventory, never manage stock levels, never deal with leftover product.
What happens when a customer places an order?
Everything after checkout runs without you. Brikl captures the payment, turns the logo into production-ready artwork, and routes the order to the right fulfillment partner automatically. The item is decorated and shipped straight to your customer, and you get a tracking number and a net payout — no emails, no POs, no handoffs to chase.
Who handles payments, sales tax, and chargebacks?
For stores on the Launch Plan, Brikl acts as the Merchant of Record. We collect payments, calculate and remit sales tax across all U.S. states, and assume chargeback risk. You receive a single net payout, with no invoices to reconcile.
For stores on the Enterprise Plan, you remain the Merchant of Record and are responsible for collecting payments and managing sales tax compliance.
See the Pricing page for full details.
How fast can I get a store live for a client?
A branded store can go live the same day — and with Turbo Store, a stocked, correctly priced store takes about ten seconds. Production turnaround is typically 7–10 business days from order to delivery, depending on the fulfillment partner and decoration method.
Can I keep my existing storefront?
Yes. Build your catalogs on Brikl and run the storefront on Base44, Framer, Lovable, Shopify, Squarespace or Webflow. Linking takes a click inside the platform; products, stock and orders keep syncing with Brikl.
