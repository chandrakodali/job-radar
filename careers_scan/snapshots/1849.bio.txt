HOW IT WORKS
Sample & Sequence
We analyze your ore samples to map their unique microbiome and mineral composition.
Engineer & Optimize
Using our advanced bioengineering platform, we enhance naturally occurring microbes with specific traits to boost maximum extraction rate on a specific ore.
Test & Validate
Our high-throughput screening technology rapidly tests optimized strains in parallel, ensuring peak performance for your specific ore composition.
Integrate & Deploy
Your custom microbial solution is then deployed through your existing leaching infrastructure.
