Our Values
Our public benefit mission is central to everything we do. We are committed to building a team that shares our values and is passionate about our mission.
Curious
We're scientists at heart. We love exploring new ideas, pursuing challenging problems, and pushing the boundaries of what's possible. We believe that great products begin with great technology, and we're committed to building a team that is offered the freedom to explore and innovate in areas that excite them.
Mission-Driven
Our mission is to redefine “zero-trust” through innovative applied post-quantum cryptography research. We believe our technology has the potential to massively improve the general security and privacy posture of the modern web. As a public benefit company, this mission drives us to pursue excellence in everything we do.
Transparent
We're strong believers in open-source software. We don't believe in security through obscurity, but instead prioritize clear and open communication about our capabilities. Most importantly, we hold ourselves accountable when we make mistakes, and we do everything we can to learn from them.
