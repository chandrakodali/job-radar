Our values
Our public benefit mission is central to everything we do
We are committed to building a team that shares our values and our mission.
Curious
We’re scientists at heart. We love exploring new ideas and pursuing challenging problems. We believe that great products begin with great technology, and our team is free to innovate in areas that excite them.
Mission-driven
Our mission is to redefine “zero-trust” through applied post-quantum cryptography research. As a public benefit company, we believe our technology can improve the security and privacy of the modern web.
Transparent
We’re strong believers in open-source software. We don’t believe in security through obscurity, but in clear and open communication. We hold ourselves accountable for our mistakes, and we learn from them.
Our perks
We value our team members
Our perks keep our team happy, healthy and fulfilled inside and outside of work, and empower them to do their best work every day.
Competitive compensation
We offer a very competitive total package, where every employee is a major owner of our stock.
Unlimited PTO
Employees are trusted to take paid time off for personal or sick leave, without prescribed limits.
Wellbeing perks
We encourage a healthy work-life balance, and can reimburse employees’ reasonable health expenses.
Professional development
We encourage self-improvement, and can reimburse employees for professional development expenses.
Commuter benefits
In-person employees can be reimbursed for transportation, and pay other qualified costs pre-tax.
Retirement savings
We offer employer-sponsored retirement accounts to help employees save in a tax-advantaged setting.
