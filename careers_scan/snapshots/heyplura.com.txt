About Plura
Plura is the social app for edge-of-culture communities that helps you meet people organically through shared events. We collaborate with event organizers whose events facilitate connection and community. These groups are often at the boundary of culture, exploring new ways to connect authentically, approaching life with a learning mindset, and creating a more community-minded future. In Plura’s app, you can discover events, become Buds with people, and join community conversations. Plura is a queer woman-led company known for its strong consent culture and inclusive approach. Join us to explore, learn, and grow with like-minded people and create a more liberated and open world. You are welcome here.
Open Roles
UGC Creator (User-Generated Content)
Plura is hiring UGC creators to create content about Plura and the communities it serves: queer/LGBTQ+, non-monogamous, and other groups.
You’ll produce 5–7 videos per week, with guidance from Plura’s Social Media Manager. You won’t be starting from scratch: our team will share videos and formats that have already gone viral so you can put your own spin on proven formats.
This role requires you to:
Create short-form UGC
Produce 5–7 videos per week
Create videos based on examples, scripts, or sample videos provided by the UGC manager
Develop your own video ideas for approval by the UGC manager
Post and organize content
Post each video across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels
Use accounts created and managed by Plura
Store video content in a shared Dropbox
All video content will be owned by Plura going forward
Participate in the creator community
Check the creator group chat daily
Like and comment on other creators’ content to help boost it
Contribute ideas for new videos to test
Meet the two-week review requirement
Have at least two videos reach 10,000 views within your first two weeks to continue in the program
Compensation
The base rate is $30 per video , covering the video posted across all four channels.
Additional bonuses are based on the highest view count reached on a single channel:
10,000 views: $30 bonus
25,000 views: $75 bonus
50,000 views: $150 bonus
100,000+ views: $300 bonus (cap)
