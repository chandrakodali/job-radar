Principles we work by
High bar
Potential over pedigree
We care more about how fast you learn than where you have been. Recent graduate, former founder or someone who has done great work elsewhere, you get the same fair shot. We take our time hiring, so the people beside you are people you will learn from.
Real ownership
Your area is yours
There is no chain of managers or product owners between you and the work. You own your area, you are trusted to make the calls, and your voice counts when something should change. Our CEO holds himself to the same standard every week.
Close to customers
Hear it from customers yourself
Everyone at Pango talks to the e-commerce brands we work with, engineers included. You hear their problems first hand, in their own words, and then you get to build the fix. Nothing gets lost on its way to you.
Built with agents
Work alongside AI agents
Our whole company is a bet that AI models keep getting better. You will use agents every day to multiply what you can get done, with full access and the tokens to use them properly, not a locked-down trial.
People we hire
We don’t care about standard job titles. We care about impact.
The niche specialist, whose skills are so sharp that they can bend the trajectory of our company. The one with founder energy, who has built something from nothing before and doesn’t need a manager to tell them what needs fixing today. The e-commerce veteran, who knows the ugly truth about the industry and is tired of legacy software failing to fix it. The recent graduate, who learns faster than anyone expects and wants real responsibility from the first day.
If you are reading this and thinking “that’s me,” we want to get to know you.
