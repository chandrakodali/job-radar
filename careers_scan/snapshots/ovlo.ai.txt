Questions
What does it cost? +
One monthly fee for each role we're searching. No placement fee, unlimited hires. A fraction of what an agency charges for one hire.
What roles do you hire for? +
Any role, technical or not, at any level.
How is Ovlo different from a recruiting agency? +
Agencies search by hand and recycle the same candidates between clients. Our search engine reads the whole market for every role. Agencies sit between you and the candidate. We reach out in your name, so candidates talk to you. Agencies leave you the admin. We run the whole top of funnel in your Slack and your ATS. Agencies charge 20 to 30% of the salary per hire. We charge one monthly fee per role, unlimited hires.
Where do candidates come from? +
Mostly people who weren't looking. Our agents search LinkedIn, GitHub, company sites, talks, posts, social media and the wider web, and we approach the people who fit directly.
Do we have to use any software? +
No. Our team runs it. Candidates arrive in your Slack, we handle replies, scheduling and follow-ups, and your part is the interviews.
Do you work with in-house recruiters? +
Yes. We add search and outreach capacity to your team and handle sourcing, outreach, screening, scheduling and ATS updates, so your recruiters spend their time on interviews and offers.
