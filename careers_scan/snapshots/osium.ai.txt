Origin of Osium
Osmium
The densest metal on earth. This rare and precious metal has exceptional characteristics and precisely represents the values of Osium: robustness, performance and authenticity.

Originally the essence or substance. Osium AI sparks breakthroughs in fundamental research. Hence, Osium perfectly derives from Ousia, where materials are modified at their essence.
