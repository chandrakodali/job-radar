Career Opportunities
Technology Development Scientist
Responsibilities
Develop diagnostic methods to detect epigenetic signatures on circulating tumor DNA in human plasma samples
Lead team to plan, design, and execute key technology development and testing
Support in designing and executing guardbanding experiments for protocol formulation
Lead data analysis and technical troubleshootings in internal meetings
Author technical reports, SOPs, and technology transfer documents
Contribute to scientific documentation of patent applications and subsequent internal and external presentation and/or publication of new technologies
Train and oversee junior colleagues with respect to lab activities
Qualifications
Ph.D in molecular biology, biochemistry, biotechnology, or related fields
Previous experience with PCR assay development and molecular diagnostics
Previous experience with DNA methylation analysis techniques such as qMSP, EM-Seq, and WGBS
Excellent communication, presentation, and data analysis skills
Strong desire to learn new techniques, willingness to work collaboratively as part of a team, and be productive in a fast-paced start-up environment
Recommended
PhD with 1+ years of industry or relevant experience in process development
Experience with NGS assay protocols including library preparation, target enrichment, and sequencing
Experience with whole genome amplification (WGA) and isothermal amplification techniques
Knowledge of statistical design of experiments, as well as data analysis using JMP or other statistical package is desired
