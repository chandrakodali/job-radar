What is Peoplebox.ai?
Peoplebox.ai (YC-backed) is an AI-native talent platform used by 500+ companies to hire, manage, and grow their people.
Nova, world’s most human-like digital twin of your best interviewer and recruiter, runs two-way voice/video pre-screens calls, first-round interviews, check-ins, and assessments, with org context built in.
The platform brings together goals, performance, career growth, calibration, and compensation into one unified system, helping companies align, assess, and grow talent faster.
