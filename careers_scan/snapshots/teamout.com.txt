05 FAQ
Questions, answered
Is there a base salary?
It's commission only, and that's the point: every deal you sign pays you, with no cap on what you can earn in a year. The more you build, the more it pays.
When do I get paid?
At signature. Your first payout comes the moment the company signs the contract. TeamOut handles billing and collects the commission for you, so you never chase an invoice.
Will TeamOut give me leads?
You find your own accounts, and that's what makes the role yours: your market, your network, your pitch. What we bring is everything around it: founder-led training, the playbooks we use ourselves, the venue sourcing, contracts and billing once a deal is signed, and a team that answers on Slack when a deal gets complicated.
Do I need sales experience?
It helps, and most of us had already sold something before joining: hotel rooms, event packages, sponsorships, travel. If you've done that, you'll recognize the work. We train the method and the playbooks; you bring the appetite and the relationships.
Do I need hospitality experience?
Yes, and it's your edge. Hotels, venues, events, travel: any role on that side of the table counts, because you already know how a group booking works and what a planner is worried about. That's exactly what our clients are buying.
Where can I be based?
Anywhere in the US, Canada, Mexico or the Caribbean. The role is fully remote, from home, a hotel lobby or the road, as long as you work on US time zones, where our clients are.
