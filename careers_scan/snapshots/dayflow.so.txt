How we work
We’re a small team with a high bar. We’d rather get one thing genuinely right than ship five things that are just fine.
We use Dayflow to build Dayflow. Being our own users keeps our iteration loop tight - we feel every rough edge and fix it fast - and the timeline it builds keeps us focused on the work that actually matters.
We work closely, give honest feedback, and see our work through end to end. If that sounds like you, we’d like to hear from you.
