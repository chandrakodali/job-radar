How We Work
Nothing great is made alone.
Design isn’t just a team, it is a process we’re all a part of.
Joy is a design-driven product. That means that the entire team values and participates in an open and collaborative design process, from the start of a feature all the way through to testing and release. Because getting to the best solution takes everyone working together.
We work in pods, forming close relationships across disciplines and strengths.
Pods at Joy are like sports teams. They have their own ways of working and cultures, and they’re always evolving and iterating on their products and their process. We encourage experimentation and evolution because the best way of working is always a moving target.
“Everyone is so supportive and you have the freedom to explore what you want to explore.
Everyone collaborates so well together. All ideas are heard, talked about, and sometimes even executed. It’s a very collaborative experience.”
Courtney Cross, Product Designer
