About Omnistrate
Omnistrate is pioneering a fundamentally new category of defining next-gen abstractions for building and operating cloud services in the Cloud.
As we all know, an increasing number of organizations are adopting the cloud with more and more service offerings moving to Cloud at scale. At Omnistrate, we believe that the complexity of the underlying cloud platform is growing with the increasing number of cloud offerings, multi/poly cloud strategies, regulatory requirements, globalization, and so on.
At Omnistrate, we're redefining the abstractions of Cloud Computing. Our cloud-native offering is designed to abstract away all the complexities making it simple, fast, and cost-effective for building managed services. With Omnistrate, organizations can create build and manage services and run everywhere in minutes.
We are building a team of self-motivated people who crave a challenge and feel energized to roll up their sleeves and help realize Omnistrate's enormous potential. Chart your own path and take healthy risks as we solve big problems together. We value having diverse teams and want you to grow as we grow—whether you're just starting out in your career or managing a large team, you'll be amazed at the magnitude of your impact.
