Our values
Discover a diverse community of thinkers, doers, and dreamers who make up the fabric of our company culture.
Be an owner
Everyone in the company acts like an owner. Because we are. Every customer is your customer, their problem is your problem. We are always thinking about what is in the best interest of the company as a whole, rather than individual gratification.
Rise to the challenge
Building a start-up is hard. We always rise to the challenge of everything that comes with it. Every day will present a new challenge, and we do not shy away from it. We discuss it openly, challenge the status quo, take risks, plot a course and fearlessly pursue it. We do the hard things first and improve every day.
Work together
We sacrifice our own individual comfort for the better of the team. The whole of the team is greater than the sum of its parts and we achieve amazing things because we help each other out, we think and act as one.
Relentlessness
Throwing our arms up is not something we do. We eagerly suss out problems, keep difficult conversations going and we do not take no for an answer. We embrace the pain that comes with startup life and wear it like a badge of honor. We attack problems.
Move fast, break things
Mistakes are made when we go fast, but mistakes mean we are learning. We move fast, we change things every day, and test new ways of doing everything. Nothing is permanent. Everything is an ongoing experiment; nothing stays the same.
