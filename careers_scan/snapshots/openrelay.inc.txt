Designed for failure.
Centralized clouds treat a failed node as an outage. We treat it as a Tuesday. The network expects nodes to drop: and routes around them before you notice.

Detect
Health checks flag an unreachable node within milliseconds: no waiting on timeouts.
Reroute
In-flight and new requests shift to the nearest healthy GPU automatically.
Heal
The failed node drains and rejoins when it recovers. No manual intervention.
The principles that guide everything we build.
Fault Tolerance First
We design for failure. When a backend degrades, hosted inference routes new requests to healthy capacity without manual intervention.
Distributed by Design
No single datacenter, no single vendor bottleneck. Capacity spans our own servers, partner data centers, and vetted providers.
Open Participation
Approved providers enroll supported NVIDIA, AMD, or CPU nodes with one command and earn on verified utilization.
Simple Abstractions
Call hosted models through an OpenAI-compatible API, or launch GPU VMs and pods from the same account.
