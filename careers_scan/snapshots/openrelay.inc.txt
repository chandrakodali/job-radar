Designed for failure.
Centralized clouds treat a failed node as an outage. We treat it as a Tuesday. The network expects nodes to drop: and routes around them before you notice.

Detect
Health checks flag an unreachable node within milliseconds: no waiting on timeouts.
Reroute
In-flight and new requests shift to the nearest healthy GPU automatically.
Heal
The failed node drains and rejoins when it recovers. No manual intervention.
The principles that guide everything we build.
Fault Tolerance First
We design for failure. Nodes go down, networks partition: your workloads keep running. Resilience is the default, not a feature.
Decentralized by Design
No single datacenter, no single point of failure. Our overlay spans the globe.
Open Participation
Anyone can join the network. Have a 4090 sitting idle? Contribute compute and earn.
Simple Abstractions
Deploy inference workloads with one API call. We handle routing, failover, and load balancing.
