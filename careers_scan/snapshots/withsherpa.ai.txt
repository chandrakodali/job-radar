A day at Sherpa
8:00 am Gym
Most of us start here. Clears your head before the day gets loud.
9:00 am Standup
Every day we all meet, figure out the highest priority right now, and go knock it out.
10:00 am Babysitting agents
You've probably got a few agents running by now. Check their work, unblock them, send them back out.
11:00 am Voicing into your agents
Talking to your agents. We do a lot of things here out loud.
12:00 pm Team lunch
Hang out and catch up.
2:00 pm Live with a customer
Watch a customer use Sherpa and improve whatever they need.
4:00 pm Ping pong
Trust me, these get taken more seriously than they should.
6:30 pm Product review
Show off what you shipped, get feedback, talk about what's next.
7:00 pm Team dinner
Still talking about the product, usually.
8:00 pm Deep work
Headphones on, no meetings. Some of the best stuff ships after dinner.
10:00 pm Last agents of the day
Wrap up your work and send your agents off on long running tasks, so the work keeps shipping while you sleep.
