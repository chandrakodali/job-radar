Our mission
Make finance operations run themselves
Finance teams spend their days moving documents between tools, chasing approvals, and reconciling numbers that already exist somewhere. Software should handle that work so people can step in only where judgment is genuinely needed.
That's what we're building: automation that fits how a business actually operates, in the tools teams already use, with a full audit trail behind every action.
