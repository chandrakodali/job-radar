Work at the frontier of visual intelligence
We're building the foundational layer for visual intelligence: multimodal models trained jointly on images, video, audio, and action to see, generate, and understand the world.
Our founding team pioneered latent diffusion and Stable Diffusion, the breakthroughs that made generative AI accessible to millions. With FLUX, we're pushing into generative media at scale, machines that act in the physical world, and emerging applications in computer use.
Our FLUX models top public benchmarks and run in production in some of the world's biggest platforms. We're a small, fast growing research lab competing with the largest companies in the world. From our labs in Freiburg to San Francisco, we're building what comes next.

Our values
Everything we do is grounded in four values.
Obsessed
We are a frontier research lab. The science has to be right, the understanding deep, the product beautiful.
Low ego
The work speaks. The best idea wins, no matter who said it. Credit is shared. Nobody is above any task.
Bold
We take the ambitious bet. We ship, we do not wait for conditions to be perfect.
Kind
People over politics. We treat each other with genuine warmth. Agency without empathy creates chaos.
How we work
Execute, do not wait.
The right decision is the best decision. The wrong decision is the next best. The worst decision is no decision.
Work in the open.
Channels, not DMs. Share early and widely, even when it is rough.
Debate, then go all in.
Challenge ideas, not people. Once the call is made, execute like it was yours.
Embrace discomfort.
Ambiguity is normal. Plans will change. If we feel comfortable, we are probably not pushing hard enough.
Write it down.
Decisions, context, and rationale go in docs. Meetings are for decisions, not context downloads.
Raise the bar.
Every project, every hire, every product, every decision should make us better than yesterday.
Talk across the pond.
Freiburg and SF are 9 hours apart. This only works if both sides over-communicate.

Our offices
Freiburg
Freiburg is home to our headquarters and where Black Forest Labs was founded. Nestled on the edge of the Black Forest, the city is known for its beautiful old town, vibrant university community, and easy access to the outdoors. It's a place where great work and a high quality of life go hand in hand.
San Francisco
Our San Francisco office is located in SoMa, a block from Caltrain and a short walk to Oracle Park. The office itself is built for how we actually work, whether that's heads-down focus, collaboration, or a team dinner, and the neighborhood is packed with great coffee and food spots.
