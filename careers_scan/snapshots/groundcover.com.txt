Company Values
At groundcover, we move fast, think big, and build bold. We believe in products that simply work, out-of-the-box, effortlessly.
We’re a team of imperfect perfectionists, every person lives and breathes groundcover, bringing a passion and dedication to the product that’s rare to find. We collaborate as one team, catalyze change, and apply genius technology in unlikely places, always paying attention to the smallest of details.
Life at groundcover
Our offices, nestled in the heart of Tel Aviv, are a true city gem. With stunning rooftops perfect for meetups, company parties, or a peaceful afternoon yoga session, the space blends work and play effortlessly. Start your day with amazing breakfasts, enjoy a dog-friendly environment, and join us for Thursday happy hours as you soak in the vibrant urban energy that keeps our team inspired and connected.
Beyond Tel Aviv, our growing US team brings their own energy, perspective, and expertise, collaborating seamlessly across time zones to build products that make a real impact.
Coffee
At groundcover, coffee isn’t just a drink - it’s part of who we are. We take pride in our amazing coffee machine and even hold coffee sessions to learn how to make the best brew. The first thing we ask when you walk in isn’t about your experience it’s, “How do you take your coffee?”
Because for us, coffee fuels conversations, sparks ideas, and brings people together.
