Our Values
These commitments shape how we operate and who we hire.
Custodianship
Space belongs to everyone. We build technology that helps people understand and use it responsibly. We care about every object we observe, natural or artificial. We work to keep space useful for the people who come next.
Sustainability
We believe better information means better use of what is already in orbit. We help operators get more from existing spacecraft, plan missions efficiently, and reduce unnecessary activity in space.
Trust
Our customers rely on us to tell them what is actually happening. That starts with reliable, repeatable, transparent work. We show our reasoning and stand behind the evidence. People should know why they can trust the answer.
