Water is a human right. But, the United States has a water affordability problem.
In places like Detroit, Baltimore, and other cities,
families unable to afford their water bills have their water shut off. When this happens,
kids can't bathe, people can't wash dishes, and grandparents can't flush their toilets. The Human Utility is non-profit organization providing
help to families and makes sure they always have running water at home.
