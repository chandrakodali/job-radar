Frequently asked questions
What is Female Invest and how does it work?
Female Invest is a financial learning platform built specifically for women - because the traditional world of finance wasn’t. Inside the app, you set your personal goal (like investing, budgeting, or growing your wealth), and we create a tailored learning path to get you there.
You’ll get expert-led courses, videos, articles, and interactive exercises that break down complex topics into simple, practical steps. And with Playvest, our virtual trading simulator using real market data (but no real money), you’ll get hands-on practice before you ever invest a cent.
You'll also get access to budgeting templates, finance worksheets, curated daily news, and a vibrant global community where you can ask questions and get guidance from our money experts. It's everything you need to understand money, all in one place - no previous knowledge required.
How does the free trial work?
Your free trial gives you full, unlimited access to everything Female Invest offers - no strings attached. You can explore all the learning paths, take as many courses as you want, try the trading simulator, join the community, and use every budgeting tool and worksheet.
It’s the best way to see if Female Invest is right for you, without paying anything upfront. No locked content. No surprises. Just a chance to experience the full value risk-free.
Will I be reminded before my trial ends?
Yes. We’ll send you a clear reminder 2 days before your trial ends, so you’ll have time to decide if you want to continue.
There’s no pressure and no hidden charges - cancel any time in just a few taps. You stay in full control of your membership.
I’ve never invested before. Is this for me?
Yes - in fact, Female Invest was built with beginners in mind. If you’ve ever felt like investing is confusing, intimidating, or “not for people like you,” this is your safe place to learn.
We break everything down into easy-to-follow lessons, with no jargon and no judgment. You'll learn the basics of investing, how to get started safely, and how to make your money work for you - at your own pace, with full support along the way.
I already invest. Is this for me?
Definitely. Even if you’ve already started investing, Female Invest can help you take your knowledge to the next level. You’ll get deeper insights into strategy, risk management, diversification, and market analysis.
Our expert-led content is constantly updated with new lessons and tools to help you stay sharp. Plus, with daily curated financial news, interactive tools, and a global community of female investors, you’ll stay informed and inspired as you grow your portfolio.
How much time do I need to commit?
Female Invest is built to fit into your life, no matter how busy you are. You can make meaningful progress in as little as 5 minutes a day.
The app lets you learn at your own pace - whether that’s one lesson per day or a deep-dive weekend session. There’s no pressure or deadline, just consistent, practical steps toward your financial goals.
Can I talk to money experts?
Yes. Inside the app, you can ask questions and get direct answers from Female Invest’s team of money experts. You’ll also benefit from seeing answers to questions asked by other members - many of whom are probably wondering the same things you are.
It’s expert guidance without the pressure or price tag of a private advisor.
Is it relevant in my country?
Yes. Female Invest has members in over 125 countries, and our core financial education is designed to work across borders.
The lessons focus on universal money principles - like how to budget, save, invest, and build wealth - which apply no matter where you live. While some specifics may vary country to country (like tax rules or investment platforms), the foundational knowledge remains globally relevant.
