Our story
Stirling PDF is an open core PDF editor. It started as a 24-hour experiment over one weekend: one engineer, four features, posted to Reddit. Today it has more than 44 million downloads and runs inside 55,000 companies. While we serve everyone, we focus on people and organizations in regulated industries.

How we work
The core stays open
The core code is open source under the MIT licence on GitHub. Revenue from teams and enterprises pays to keep improving Stirling.
What's open source in Stirling PDF
Privacy comes first
We focus on people and organizations in regulated industries. User security and data privacy shape what we build.
Built with the community
Stirling PDF began as a weekend project shared on Reddit. We still work in the open on GitHub and look to hire people who already contribute.
Own it end to end
We are a small, fully remote team. Each person owns their work from development through to QA.
