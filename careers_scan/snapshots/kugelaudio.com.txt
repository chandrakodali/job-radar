We train the model. We do not wrap somebody else’s API.
We think voice is the most natural interface a person has, so we are building the layer underneath every voice application: the model, the framework, the APIs. Almost every voice-AI job posting you will read this month is a wrapper around an API. Here is what is actually ours.
The model is ours
We train it ourselves, on our own hardware, end to end. When quality regresses we bisect a training run, not a vendor’s changelog. Almost nobody at our size does this, and it is what makes everything else here possible.
The latency
Thirty-nine milliseconds to first audio, and every millisecond in the path belongs to us: text normalisation, the model, the serving loop, the transport. There is no layer where the answer is “that’s the provider’s problem”.
The hard part nobody demos
IBANs. Email addresses. Product names. Bavarian German, Northern Irish English, Yemeni Arabic. This company exists because enterprises told us the incumbents mispronounce their own product names, and their legal team would not sign the DPA anyway. Both halves of that sentence are a roadmap.
Shipping into someone else’s cluster
Our model runs on-premise, in customers’ own Kubernetes, where we cannot log in to fix it. That constraint is why regulated European buyers pick us, and it makes every engineering decision harder and more interesting.

Questions
Do I need to speak German?
We work in English, and most roles do not need German at all. Content and go-to-market roles are the exception, because a good part of our founder content is in German: those postings say so, and they mean C2 level, written and spoken.
Do you take working students?
No. At four people every role has to own something end to end, and that is not a fair thing to ask of someone who is also studying full-time. If that changes we will post the role here rather than take speculative applications for it.
Do I need speech or audio ML experience?
No, for most roles. We need people who are good at systems and fast at learning a domain. Tell us in your application what you would need to learn.
I applied before. Can I apply again?
Yes. Tell us what changed.
What happens to my application?
A founder reads it, not a filter. It is stored in our applicant tracking system, kept for six months and then deleted. Email us any time and we will delete it sooner, no reason needed.
We will never ask you for money
We only write from @kugelaudio.com, never ask for payment, equipment or bank details while hiring, and never interview over text-only chat. If something claiming to be us looks off, email security@kugelaudio.com.
Need an adjustment to the process?
Email jobs@kugelaudio.com and we will arrange it. It has no bearing on the decision.
Your data
We keep applications for six months, then delete them. You can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.
