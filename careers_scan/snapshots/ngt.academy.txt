IT Infrastructure Jobs Keep Growing
Four Reasons IT Infrastructure Jobs Keep Growing
AI Needs Networks
Every AI application runs on networks that need to be designed, deployed, and maintained. The more AI scales, the more network engineers are needed.
AI Creates New Attack Surfaces
AI systems introduce new security risks — model theft, prompt injection, data poisoning. Cybersecurity professionals are on the front lines of protecting AI, not being replaced by it.
AI Demands More Data Centers
The AI boom is driving the largest build-out of data center capacity in history. That build-out needs people who understand networks, systems, and security.
Foundational Skills Are More Valuable, Not Less
As AI changes how work gets done, the people with strong technical fundamentals — networks, systems, security — become the ones companies trust to deploy and maintain AI-driven infrastructure.
