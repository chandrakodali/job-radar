Backend Software Engineer
Remote, Europe or Latin America or United States
Full-time
Submit an application
Own the backend behind OllyGarden: our public REST API, webhooks, the pipeline that discovers services and stores insights, and the Postgres schema they share.
What We’re Looking For
Senior backend engineering experience, including Go services you shipped to production and kept running
Strong Postgres skills: schema design, online migrations, indexes, transactions, and row-level security
Experience with event-driven systems: streams or queues, at-least-once delivery, idempotency, and retries
Experience designing public APIs that customers build on: authentication, pagination, versioning, and backward compatibility
A habit of instrumenting what you build, and of using traces, metrics, and logs to debug production
AI-native development, knowing how software is developed in the companies of the future, coordinating multiple agents and using AI-related engineering skills to get quality work from them. Your main IDE nowadays is Claude Code, Codex, Amp, or Conductor.
Based in Europe, or Latin America, or USA
What You’ll Do
Own our public REST API end to end: new endpoints, versioning and deprecation, latency, and the per-tenant limits that keep one customer from affecting another
Own our database schema and its migration pipeline: evolve it as features need, review every change, test it in CI against a fresh database, and apply it through automation
Keep tenant isolation enforced in the database as the schema and the number of services that use it grow
Keep webhook delivery trustworthy: filtering, retries, idempotency, and a delivery history customers can rely on
Keep service discovery and insight persistence correct and fast as telemetry volume grows: deduplication, consumer backpressure, and caching
Run what you build in production: dashboards, alerts, and incident follow-ups for the services you own
Work with our frontend and Rose engineers on the data and APIs their features need
Bonus Points
Experience instrumenting services with OpenTelemetry, or working on observability tooling
Production experience with NATS JetStream, or similar
Experience designing and operating multi-tenant PostgreSQL schemas, including row-level security, roles and grants, production migrations, and query performance
Experience building APIs designed for AI agents
Experience maintaining an open source project, especially in the OpenTelemetry or Prometheus ecosystems
Kubernetes on AWS, Flux, or Terraform
Enthusiasm for sharing your work in public, from blog posts to conference talks
