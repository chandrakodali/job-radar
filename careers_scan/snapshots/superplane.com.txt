Building SuperPlane
An open-source software factory. Built by people who care about the work.
We’re building a way for engineering teams to hand routine work to AI agents with the rules, checks and visibility they need to trust the result.
Our founders spent more than two decades building developer tools together, including Semaphore. Today, we’re a small team working in the open, learning alongside developers and bringing that experience to SuperPlane.
