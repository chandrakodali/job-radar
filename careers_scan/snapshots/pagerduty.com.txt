The PagerDuty Story
It all started with three Amazon developers in 2009, frustrated by their own on-call experiences. That spark of inspiration became PagerDuty. Fast forward to today: we're a vibrant global community of 1,200 innovators spread across five continents, tackling some of technology's toughest problems. Over half of the Fortune 500 and nearly 70% of the Fortune 100 trust our AI-driven Operations Cloud to keep their critical systems running smoothly.
At PagerDuty, every role has purpose. Our engineers push technological boundaries. Our customer teams build lasting partnerships. Our designers craft experiences that delight. And our business teams chart the course forward. We're a community of bright minds who believe in flexibility, celebrate innovative thinking, and unite around our core values – led by our commitment to Champion the Customer in everything we do.
