About Work at Prezly
We’re looking for curious, hardworking people to join our team, to help us do the best work of our lives. Together we’ll create a product that helps people, organizations and brands share their stories with the world.
Prezly is a profitable, bootstrapped company fully owned by its founders . We’re a tight-knit team of doers that's growing fast, and we’re looking for smart people around the globe to help us build trailblazing software.
The Prezly team is fully remote, spread across 15 different cities around the world. Our office is in Leuven, Belgium, but everyone at Prezly is free to live and work wherever they want.
