Our values
Start with the product
Understand the hardware, the constraints, and what a better chip needs to achieve. Build around the customer’s system rather than a catalog part.
Reason from the fundamentals
Make architecture decisions with a clear understanding of timing, area, power, and verification. Follow the details until the design makes sense.

Bring design, verification, software, and manufacturing into the same conversation. Share what you learn and make room for better ideas.
Build work that holds up
Write clear RTL, build rigorous verification, and make engineering decisions that carry through to real silicon. Quality is part of the process.

FAQ
How do I apply?
Apply directly from either open role , use the digital role’s YC link, or contact founders@visiblsemi.com . Keep responses and project examples non-confidential. We do not expect proprietary code or design details from previous employers.
What work do the roles involve?
The digital role covers RTL, verification and synthesis handoff. The layout role covers analog/mixed-signal physical design, DRC/LVS/PEX and integration handoff. Each listing describes its own experience requirements and responsibilities.
Where is the role based?
This is a remote opportunity. This is a US-based role with regular overlap with our Pacific-time engineering team. Canadian citizens are welcome to apply, and we can provide employer documentation to support TN status for eligible candidates working in the US. See each role for its engagement and eligibility details.
Is this contract or full-time, and how is compensation handled?
This is a paid remote contract opportunity, with scope, weekly commitment, and compensation agreed before starting. We’re also open to full-time employment. For the digital role, the displayed $180,000–$250,000 USD range is annual base salary for the full-time option only. For the layout role, the $160,000–$220,000 USD range is annual base salary for the full-time option only. Contract compensation is agreed separately for each assignment.
What is the team’s background?
Our technical leaders bring chip-design experience from Microsoft, Intel, and Arm. We combine hardware engineering with software and automation to make custom silicon more accessible. We value open communication, mutual support, and a positive contribution to how the team works together.
