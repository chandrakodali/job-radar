What we’re building
A shared foundation.
A broader possibility.
Clinical development depends on work that happens between systems and teams: reconciling a data delivery, following up on a monitoring finding, or establishing the evidence behind a quality decision.
Panoptive connects study data, documents, and operational history so agents can help carry out that work. We’re building the foundation for connected systems across clinical data management, clinical operations, and quality assurance—with checks and human review built in.
External data operations
Connect vendor deliveries to the study, from mapping and quality checks to reconciliation and review.
Trial monitoring oversight
Bring monitoring evidence, sponsor decisions, and the work that follows into one connected case history.
QA automation
Assess study records against protocols and SOPs, with evidence and gaps ready for quality teams to review.
