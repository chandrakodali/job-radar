Diversity, Equity and Inclusion
DEI is in our DNA at Dayforce
We succeed when all our people thrive. That’s why we believe and invest in diversity and inclusion and hold equity as a core value. We actively support the advancement of people from underrepresented groups within our workforce through our unique Achieving Corporate Equity (ACE) differentiated leadership program and our Employee Resource Groups program which builds an inclusive environment where every employee is welcome to connect with, learn from, and grow with one another across regions, functional areas, and cultures.
Opening access to tools, strategies, and information is a critical part of our approach that empowers everyone in the organization to take part in Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion. By putting equity into action, we’ve been recognized in Seramount’s Global Inclusion Index, Bloomberg's Gender Equality Index list, and Forbes Canada’s Best Employers for Diversity.
Dayforce is an equal opportunity global employer. We consider and embrace all individuals and what makes them unique. We are committed to equal employment opportunity regardless of race, color, religion, gender, national origin or ancestry, age, disability, marital or family status, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, veteran status, genetic information, or other applicable legally protected characteristics.
View the EEO Know Your Rights poster and Dayforce’s Pay Transparency Non-Discrimination Provision for more information.
Dayforce Cares
At Dayforce, we’ve always been dedicated to giving back where our people live and work. Daymakers around the world contribute in meaningful ways year-round. With a global volunteer month, a full day’s worth of paid volunteer time, and an annual giving campaign, our teams across five continents enjoy having a positive impact in our communities.
Additionally, by creating our own employee-driven charity, Dayforce Cares, we’re able to further that commitment in a more direct and meaningful way. Our employee-driven charity, Dayforce Cares, provides basic needs grants to families and individuals in our communities. Dayforce Cares has provided over $6.5 million in grants to nearly 5,000 individuals in North America since the charity’s founding.

Life at Dayforce
Benefits and perks
Think work life should be better? We do too.
The Dayforce brand promise, Makes Work Life Better™, is our guide in creating engaging, inclusive, and rewarding employee experiences. By listening to our people, we offer the benefits, perks, and programs that matter most to employees.
Grow your career
· Experience a culture of learning that encourages your skills development and a tuition and certification program that supports learning outside of work.
· Grow, don’t go – our expansive internal mobility program offers plenty of opportunities for career growth within Dayforce.
· Get involved in leadership development opportunities, including mentorship and sponsorship from senior leaders.
· Experiment with future career paths by trying out new roles with job shadowing and rotations.
Stay healthy
· Keep yourself and your family healthy with our comprehensive medical, dental, and vision plans.
· Address life’s everyday concerns with access to our Employee Assistance Program for you and your family.
· Grow your family with support from our infertility assistance program.
· Be proactive with your health and enjoy friendly competition with company-sponsored challenges.
Be financially fit
· Save for your future with our progressive retirement plans with company matches.
· Know that pay equity is a priority at Dayforce – as an EDGE certified organization, we provide competitive and fair pay for all.
· Make every day payday with our on-demand pay solution, Dayforce Wallet.
Take time for you
· Relax, recharge, and reconnect with family and friends through our unlimited Time Away From Work program.
· Have peace of mind as you start or add to your family with generous parental leave and adoption assistance.
· Keep your life moving with our Take 2 program, which allows you to take two hours off work whenever you need.
· Rest and recover when you’re sick, with ample sick leave and supportive teammates covering for you.
Give where you live
· Use your annual volunteer day for events and causes you care about in your community.
· Experience our culture of giving through Dayforce-matched employee contributions.
· Engage with local charities and nonprofits with events throughout the year.
· Donate to Dayforce Cares, our own charity that gives 100% of employee contributions to people in need.
