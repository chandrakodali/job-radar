Our Mission
Artifact is the fastest way to design electrical systems and harnesses across an organization - accelerating every step in the product lifecycle:
Design → Manufacturing → Operations
We raised $3.5M to build a real-time, collaborative platform that reflects how hardware actually gets built. Every artifact system view is auto-generated and traceable to a single-source of truth.
We believe hardware engineering should be rigorous, deliberate, and system-aware. We work directly with customers solving the world's hardest problems to modernize how hardware is engineered.
