Frequently Asked Questions
Where your burning questions meet our cool, collected answers. From deciphering our hiring process to uncovering what it’s like to work with us, we’ve compiled everything you need to know. Consider this your co-pilot for the job search journey—though we won’t actually fly you anywhere. Ready for take-off? Let’s dive in.
What does the recruitment process look like?
We have designed a 4-step recruitment process to ensure we find the right fit for both the role and our company culture:
Initial Call – A welcoming conversation to get to know you and share insights into who we are. This step also explores initial alignment with our company’s values and culture.
Hiring Manager Interview – A focused discussion about your technical expertise, professional experiences, approach to work, and collaboration style.
Panel Interview – An opportunity to meet 1–2 team members, during which we’ll discuss real-world scenarios and how we collaborate as a team.
Final Conversation – A meaningful exchange with Eloa, our CEO. Beyond exploring our core values, this step also centres on your motivations and aspirations to ensure the role aligns with your career goals. On occasion, these discussions lead to tailoring the role to better match your ambitions.
This process ensures we hire not only for technical skills but for shared purpose and cultural fit, while making the experience as enriching and engaging for candidates as it is for us.
What is the best way to prepare for the interview?
Be yourself! However, preparation is essential. Here is what we recommend:
Research Our Company : Take the time to understand our mission, values, and the work we do. Familiarise yourself with our culture and what we stand for.
Reflect on Your Experience : Consider how your skills and achievements align with the role you’re applying for. Be prepared to discuss concrete examples.
Think About Fit : Reflect on what motivates you, your working style, and how our values resonate with you. Anticipate questions that explore both your expertise and your alignment with our culture.
Be Curious : Prepare thoughtful questions about the role, team, or company. An interview is as much about you getting to know us as it is about us getting to know you.
Approaching the conversation with authenticity and enthusiasm will make for a rewarding experience for both sides!
What can I expect during my first few weeks on the job?
Your first few weeks at Beyond are all about diving into our world, getting to know the team, and feeling part of the adventure.
It starts with a written pre-onboarding to give you a sneak peek into our market and ecosystem—enough to spark your excitement while helping you ease into the journey ahead.
Your official welcome day is where you’ll meet your team and start finding your rhythm. Then come the legendary Takeoff Days—two packed days that bring the Beyond experience to life. You’ll get the scoop on our vision, strategy, team, culture, areas of expertise, and all the cool projects we’re working on. It’s the ultimate intro to what makes Beyond, well, Beyond.
From there, your job-specific onboarding gets you set up with the tools you need, a clear focus on what to tackle during your trial period, and a Buddy who’s got your back as you navigate the day-to-day. It’s all designed to make sure you hit the ground running—and enjoy the ride while you’re at it!
What makes the company culture unique?
At Beyond, culture isn’t just what we stand for; it’s how we show up every day.It is shaped by our core values—Caring, Raising the Bar, Pragmatism, and Higher Purpose—which guide everything we do.
If you want to work in a challenging environment, develop cutting-edge technologies surrounded by experts and enthusiasts, join a welcoming and friendly international team and contribute to the future of aviation, you're in the right place !
How does the company support employee development?
At Beyond, we believe that personal growth and career development are integral to both individual fulfillment and organizational success. Our Career Path and Leveling Framework provides clear, actionable guidance to support your professional journey. It is designed to help you understand the expectations at different levels within your career and map out the steps to progress, whether your focus is technical expertise, or Leadership.
Are there team events or social gatherings?
At Beyond, there’s always something exciting happening, with team events and social gatherings that vary each week. Start your week with Monday breakfasts, stay active with tennis and sport sessions, and enjoy a rotating mix of afterworks, cooking sessions, and board game evenings.
We also organize inspiring masterclasses featuring renowned top athletes, adventurers, and other exceptional individuals, offering unique insights to fuel your inspiration. Every quarter, the whole team comes together for dedicated team-building activities, capped off with a party to celebrate our successes. These events aren’t just about having fun—they’re about creating a dynamic and connected team culture.
How is individual performance managed?
At Beyond Aero, we assess performance in a global way. It is based both on technical skills, which are essential for tackling our technological challenges, and on the commitment to embody and uphold our core values. During Monthly, Quarterly and Annual Reviews, you will have the opportunity to measure and evaluate your strengths and areas for improvement with your manager using a shared Performance Framework. Technical skills and the embodiment of values carry equal weight in our recognition policy.
Are there opportunities for remote work or flexible hours?
Yes, we believe that flexibility empowers our team to perform at their best, so we offer both flexible working hours and remote work options. Our Beyonders can adjust their schedules within core business hours to balance personal and professional commitments, supporting a healthy work-life balance.
