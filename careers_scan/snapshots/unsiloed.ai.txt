[ ARCHITECTURE DEEPDIVE ]
Built for your most complex documents.
Attention-guided Heatmaps
Reads pages like a human, breaking them into typed regions: tables, figures, signatures, handwriting. Attention-guided heatmaps focus compute on pivot zones: numerical columns, merged cells, section headers. Multi-page tables stay whole, split rows rejoin, and clauses keep their hierarchy.
Handwritten text
Field notes with corrections, ticked boxes, circled readings and margin arrows come back as structured, typed fields.
Tables
Merged multi-level headers, indented sub-rows, footnotes and bracketed negatives come back as a clean table with the same structure.
Charts
Crossing multi-series lines, legends and callouts read back into the series behind them, so the numbers are ready for your pipeline.
