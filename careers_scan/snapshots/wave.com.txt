Working at Wave
Our hiring philosophy is rooted in culture and values as much as capability.
We generate offers from a transparent formula so your pay is based on your skill and experience, not your ability to negotiate.
We have a flexible vacation policy, with most folks taking between 21-30 days off, exclusive of statutory holidays.
We offer employees a subsidized health care plan that covers their dependents.
We support working parents by offering generous parental leave policies.
We care about impact, not optics - work however you work best.
Core Values
We’re egolessly mission driven.
We never forget why we’re in it—to make life better for everyone that touches our products.
The people that use Wave to do their jobs, send money to loved ones or save their day’s wages—they’re
our real bosses.
We don’t wait—we live our fullest lives now.
Collectively, we take care of our users first, but individually we take time offline to live a full life,
whether that means choosing our working hours carefully, turning off notifications, or taking long
vacations.
We’re embarrassingly honest.
At most companies, admitting your mistakes is seen as weakness, and direct communication about hard things is taboo. At Wave, being vulnerable and honest about your mistakes is a sign of strength, and giving honest feedback to teammates is an act of generosity.
We maximize our rate of learning.
We’re always iterating on our personal goals and habits to become more effective and happier. Most importantly, we do this fast. The faster we run these loops—as a company and individuals—the faster we’ll achieve our mission.
