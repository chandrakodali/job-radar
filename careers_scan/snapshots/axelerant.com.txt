What we value Operating rules
Ownership, in the open, done with care.
Excellence
You are measured on what you ship and what it moves, not on effort or hours. If it did not move the metric you own, it did not count.
Kindness
Relationships come first, with clients and with each other. You are trusted to look after the whole person, including yourself. Most firms will not put this on the wall because it sounds soft. That is exactly why it is ours.
Openness
You see what the system sees. Trust runs on information, not authority, which means you are expected to work in the open and share what you know.
Three values, and each one keeps the others honest. Ownership is not a fourth word on the wall; it is how we work: one name accountable for every outcome, never a committee.

Somewhere with a well-defined scope and a manager who hands you tasks. Here, you own the whole loop, ambiguity included.
