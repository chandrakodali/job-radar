About Tracer
Your AI product calls a frontier LLM thousands of times a day.
Most of those calls don't need a frontier model.
Tracer trains a lightweight ML on your own production LLM traces,
then handles the repeated calls for near-zero cost. Same prompts,
same providers, up to 95% lower inference bill.
It's open-source, parity-gated so quality never silently drops,
and already running in production at multiple companies.
See how.
