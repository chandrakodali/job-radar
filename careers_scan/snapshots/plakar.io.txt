Plakar Open Source Community Code of Conduct
The Plakar community is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone.
We value contributions from all members of the community and are dedicated to ensuring that every participant can collaborate openly and effectively.
This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for behavior and guides our commitment to an open, collaborative, and respectful space.
You can read the full, detailed version of this Code of Conduct on our
GitHub repository
Our Standards
Be Respectful: Treat others with kindness, empathy, and respect.
Be Constructive: Provide helpful feedback, avoid personal attacks.
Focus on Technical Merit: Evaluate based on project goals, not bias.
Communicate Clearly: Use clear language, disclose conflicts of interest.
Foster a Welcoming Environment: Include and support newcomers.
Respect Personal Boundaries: Avoid unwelcome or inappropriate behavior.
Keep It Professional: No offensive or discriminatory language or imagery.
Accept Responsibility: Apologize sincerely and learn from mistakes.
Unacceptable Behavior
Harassment, bullying, or discrimination.
Insults, threats, or personal attacks.
Unsolicited sexual attention or advances.
Trolling, spamming, or disruptive behavior.
Publishing private information without consent.
Making false accusations or misleading statements.
Reporting & Enforcement
Report violations confidentially to conduct@plakar.io .
Consequences may include warnings, temporary or permanent suspension, or bans from all project spaces.
Scope
This Code applies to all project spaces, both online and offline, including issue trackers, mailing lists, code repositories, and events.

Community and Control Plane
Sustaining that work over the long term, developing, maintaining, and improving the standard and the tools around it,
takes a viable business. Here is how the open-source project and our commercial offering fit together.
Plakar Community Edition is free and open source for everyone.
There are no restrictions on who can use it or what you can use it for, and it will always be
actively developed and maintained. It gives you everything you need to back up and restore your data,
and it is especially well suited to:
Individuals protecting personal data
Organizations with straightforward backup and restore needs
Plakar Control Plane is our commercial edition. It adds capabilities for running Plakar at scale:
multi‑Kloset management with fine‑grained access rights, integration with KMS (Key Management Systems),
consolidated reports and statistics on backup and restore operations, compliance tools,
and collaborative backup management for multiple users within the same organization.
We also offer the Control Plane free of charge to organizations we want to support:
Non-profit organizations
Startups under 3 years old and pre‑Series A
Organizations operating in war zones that require assistance
The core components, Ptar , Plakar agent , and Kloset ,
are and always will be licensed under the ISC license .
The Plakar UI ships with the agent and is free to use for managing a single Kloset.
Our commitment is simple: keep the core open, free, and accessible to everyone, while building a
sustainable business around the advanced capabilities that larger organizations need.
