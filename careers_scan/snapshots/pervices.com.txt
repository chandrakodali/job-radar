Careers
Per Vices develops high-performance software defined radio (SDR) platforms for commercial, research, and mission-critical applications. We pride ourselves on an open and collaborative workplace and are always looking for talented and motivated individuals interested in solving challenging RF, FPGA, embedded, and hardware engineering problems.
Current Opportunities
Hardware Engineers
Digital/FPGA Engineers
Embedded Engineers
PCBA Manufacturing Assistant
To apply, please send your resume to careers@pervices.com highlighting your relevant experience. We are particularly interested in candidates with experience in RF, FPGA, embedded systems, hardware design, software development, or electronics manufacturing. If your experience does not align exactly with one of the opportunities listed above but you think you would be a good fit for Per Vices, we would still like to hear from you.
