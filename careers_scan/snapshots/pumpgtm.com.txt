Three steps, then you read the replies.
Pick the roles
The jobs that mean they need you: SDRs for a sales tool, a Head of PR for a PR agency, accountants for a staffing firm.
Set how fresh
Job posts from the last 7, 30 or 90 days, plus the company size and location you sell to.
Reach the owner of the hire
PumpGTM finds the manager, writes a note that mentions the role, and follows up until they answer.
Questions before you start.
The short answers, before you connect anything.
Where does the hiring data come from?
Live public job posts. You can also pull the same signal into your own tools through the Signals API.
Who does it contact, the recruiter or the manager?
Whoever you choose. Most teams pick the manager the role reports to, since they own the problem.
Will it get my LinkedIn account flagged?
PumpGTM sends from your own account at a human pace, inside working hours, under a daily cap you set of up to 30 connection requests a day. You approve the people and the messages before anything goes out.
Which channels does it send on?
LinkedIn, email and X, from accounts you connect yourself. One lead list sits behind every channel, so nobody gets contacted twice, and follow-ups stop the moment someone replies.
