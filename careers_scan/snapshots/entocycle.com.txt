Our Values
Be Responsible
Take responsibility and try to leave things better than when you find them – whether that’s the planet, our office environment or a conversation with a teammate.
Listen More
Don’t react too quickly or make assumptions. Listen first, then respond – whether to teammates or customers. Be open with one another and honest with feedback.
Find A Better Way
Try new approaches and strive to continuously improve outcomes. Better to try and fail (and learn) than not to try at all.
No Egos
No matter your role, no one is too busy or important to pick up a broom when needed. Treat everyone with respect – the way you would want to be treated.
Hunt As A Pack
Ruthlessly focus as a team on the company’s objectives. Avoid working in silos, branch out for help and support. We’re one team, we eat and win together.
Life at Entocycle
Why work at Entocycle? We’re a passionate group of individuals who all care deeply about Entocycle’s mission and vision.
Our collective expertise, technical depth and commercial focus underpins everything we do, enabling us to deliver impactful solutions that are pushing the alternative protein sector forward.
As the saying goes - we work hard, but we certainly play hard too. With regular sporting events and socials in our calendar, we’re a close-knit bunch who are not only a pleasure to work with, but genuinely lovely people. We’re also pretty good at karaoke.
