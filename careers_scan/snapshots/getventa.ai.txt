Features
From an open role to a first message
Venta AI screens job postings daily, reads what they actually say and qualifies each company against your own criteria - then hands you the decision-makers and a reason to write to them.
Search for the exact roles you target
Instead of picking an industry and hoping, you name the positions that signal a fit for you - an SDR, a plant manager, a data engineer. Venta AI looks for companies advertising exactly those roles, and you narrow it further by location, publication date, remote setup and your own keywords.
Combine hiring with your own criteria
Hiring is rarely the only thing that matters. In the same campaign you describe the rest of your ideal customer - size, region, what the company does - and Venta AI qualifies every company against all of it. Whether they are filling one position or building a whole team is part of the picture you get.
Turn a job post into a conversation
The posting is more than a trigger, it is the material for your first message. Venta AI uses what the ad actually says as the hook, adds the decision-makers matching the buying persona you defined, and writes to each of them individually - so your message refers to something the company published itself.
FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Venta AI turns open positions into campaigns with a reason behind them - so your team spends its time in conversations instead of on job boards.
Where does Venta AI find job postings?
Venta AI currently reads job postings published on LinkedIn. New ads are picked up every 24 hours, so a campaign keeps filling itself without anyone browsing job boards.
How current is the data?
Postings are screened daily and matching companies enter your campaign shortly after an ad appears. That timing is what makes the signal worth acting on - a role that has been open for months says far less than one posted last week.
Can I combine job postings with other criteria?
Yes. A job post campaign takes the same ideal customer profile as a normal company search: industry, size, region and your own AI research criteria. Hiring becomes one qualification criterion alongside the others rather than the only one.
Can I search for my own wording inside an ad?
Yes. The text of the posting is evaluated, not only the job title, so you can search for a technology, a tool, a responsibility or any phrasing that signals a fit. You can also filter by location, publication date, activity status and remote setup.
What happens once a matching company is found?
It goes straight into the campaign. Venta AI adds the decision-makers matching the buying persona you defined and drafts the outreach, using the content of the posting as the hook. You can review every message before it goes out or let the campaign send automatically.
