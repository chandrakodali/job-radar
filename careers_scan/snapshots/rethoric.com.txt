Why Work at Rethoric
Rethoric is a Y Combinator-backed (W21) content agency that helps founders of high-growth startups build authority on LinkedIn. We work with Series A through Series C founders and executives at companies like ClickUp, Checkr, and groWrk, helping them turn their expertise into content that generates millions of impressions.
We're a small, remote-first team based in San Diego, CA. Every person on the team directly impacts client outcomes. There are no layers of bureaucracy. You'll work directly with founders, learn their businesses, and see the results of your work within weeks, not quarters.
What We Value
Founder empathy
Our clients are busy building companies. We succeed when we can capture their voice, ideas, and expertise without taking more than 30 minutes of their time per week. Understanding how founders think is the core skill at Rethoric.
Quality over volume
We'd rather publish 3 exceptional posts per week than 7 mediocre ones. Every piece of content carries our client's name and reputation. We hold ourselves to a high bar.
Data-driven iteration
We track impressions, engagement rates, follower growth, and inbound leads. Content strategy decisions are made on performance data, not gut feelings.
Ownership and autonomy
Everyone at Rethoric owns their client relationships end-to-end. You'll have the autonomy to make strategic decisions and the responsibility to deliver results.
