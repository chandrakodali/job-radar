How we work
What it means to be
on this team
Ship real things
Our software drives real forklifts and tow trucks in live warehouses and factories. We don't spend months polishing simulations - we deploy, observe, fix, and redeploy. Everyone here can point to something real they built.
Own the outcome
Small team means high ownership. You don't hand things off - you see them through. When a deployment goes live, you're proud of it. When something breaks, you fix it. No silos, no blame.
Move with speed
We're a startup competing with companies that have 10× our headcount. Our advantage is speed and obsession. We make decisions with incomplete information, we learn fast, and we don't wait for permission.
Safety isn't a checkbox
Our machines share floor space with real people. We don't treat safety as simply a compliance requirement - it's a design constraint from day one, in the sensor stack, the code, and the deployment process.
Go to the floor
The best insights come from being in the warehouse, not in a meeting room. We talk to operators, watch how the machines behave, and build for what's actually needed - not what we assumed in a spec doc. One size does not fit all.
Raise the bar
We hold the work to a high standard because our machines operate in real environments around real people. Good enough isn't good enough. If something can be more reliable, safer, or faster - we make it so.
