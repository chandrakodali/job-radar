How It Works
Schedule in Minutes
Individual drivers or contractors use our online form to choose a time and add vehicle details. Fleets can bulk-schedule inspections for multiple vehicles and drivers.
Join Your Inspection
At your scheduled time, the driver joins the video call on their phone using the link we send. Our inspector will expertly guide them through the inspection steps.
Get Instant Results
We will email the driver the signed document after the inspection is complete. Company accounts can also access results stored in their web portal.
