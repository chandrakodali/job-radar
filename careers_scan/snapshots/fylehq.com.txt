What working at Sage Expense Management (formerly Fyle) feels like
Everything we do is centered around how you feel. In order to feel great, one must experience a sense of belonging and accomplishment, constantly. You will experience certain behavior traits that make up the overall culture at the company. The only thing that’s unique is that we know these traits at work is hard work.
Empathy
We’re more than just colleagues - we treat one-another like family. We’re generous with our gratitude, support and love.
Customer first
Everything we do is with the customer in mind. We’re obsessed with the details, and prioritize responsiveness.
Respectful
Whether we’re dealing with our colleagues, customers or even competitors, respect comes first.
Trustworthy
We deliver what we promise, we communicate effectively, and fix what we break. The team functions on trust.
Self-motivated
We do everything in our power to get the job done, while assuming ownership and high standards.
Curious
Always start with why. Why do customers care about this? Why is this feature our focus? The more curious we are, the better we get.
Open & Transparent
There's no sugar-coating. We believe in being upfront, direct and genuine. Everyone on the team has access to every bit of information.
High performance
The better our performance, the better product we build. We punch above our weight, move fast, get feedback, and iterate faster.
A place where everyone belongs
From PHD holders to college dropouts, all of us come from diverse backgrounds. And we love it because there’s heterogeneity in perspectives. Right from the first day, we make sure you get on board the right way, because we understand how important it is to feel a sense of belonging.
We believe in an open culture
We bet big on feedback, and we’re not just talking customer feedback. Everyone is encouraged to express their ideas, with ample space to disagree. We believe that everything can be better and it is our collective responsibility to make that happen. Feedback and honest communication is at the center of it. The more the input, the better the outcome.
Making work life (balance) better in a hybrid environment
As the working world has evolved, work has become a point of stress, frustration and anxiety. Improving people’s experience at work is our priority. We work hard, and put in tons of effort; but we also know how to prioritize family, friends and everything else that’s important to us. We have a hybrid work setup, which means more flexibility.
Everything you need and more
We want to help you be at your best. Our compensation and benefits focus on your well-being, happiness and prosperity. Now that we’re remote, we make sure that your transition to working remotely is really smooth.
Flexible hours & leaves
Enjoy flexible work hours and the freedom to take time off when you need it - so you can work in a way that’s best for you.
Health & Fitness
Get an annual stipend to power your fitness, gym and workout regimen. From equipment to classes, whatever helps you.
Mental wellness
Health doesn’t stop at physical fitness. We know how imperative mental wellness is. That’s why we offer resources and counselling for mental health management.
Health Insurance
Safety comes first. We offer you and your family (upto 5 members) health insurance, and personal accident cover. This is Independent of your salary.
Internet Allowance
For the days you work from home, your internet is on us - so you can stay connected and work seamlessly, wherever you are.
