Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about cutting edge hiring software
What is Employment Hero Jobs?
Employment Hero Jobs is a job search platform that connects jobseekers with companies across Australia. It gives you access to roles that match your skills and ambitions, all in one place.
How do I find jobs on the platform?
Sign up and create your profile in just a few minutes. From there, you can browse roles, save the ones you like, and come back to them when you’re ready.
How do I apply?
Apply directly once your profile is set up. Applications are streamlined, so you can put your best foot forward without unnecessary steps.
Do I need to be actively job hunting to use it?
Not at all. Whether you’re casually exploring new opportunities or ready to apply today, it’s a simple way to find your dream job on your own timeline.
Can I search for jobs Australia wide?
Absolutely. Employment Hero Jobs connects you with opportunities in every state and territory, from major cities to regional and remote communities. Whether you’re looking for a role in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Hobart, Darwin, or anywhere in between, you’ll find it here.
What types of jobs are available?
You’ll find a wide mix of roles on Employment Hero Jobs, from entry-level and graduate positions through to senior and leadership roles, across many industries. Use the search and filters to narrow things down to the work that suits you, whether that’s full-time, part-time, casual or contract.
How often are jobs updated?
New roles are added as employers post them, so there’s always something fresh to look at. Create an account and set up job alerts, and we’ll let you know when new roles that match your search come up.
Can I save jobs for later?
Yes, you can save jobs for later. Create an account to save jobs and set up job alerts for new opportunities, so you can come back to them when you’re ready.
