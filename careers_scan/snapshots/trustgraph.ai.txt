01 What is TrustGraph?
An context graph and a policy runtime, connected.
TrustGraph is an open-source AI Governance Platform that turns enterprise knowledge, systems, and logic into an ontology-grounded context graph, then uses that graph to govern AI agents at the moment they act. Every proposed action is checked against business meaning, authority and evidence, and is allowed, paused or denied before it executes.
Each decision is stored with the exact evidence and policy version behind it, as W3C PROV-O provenance you can query later.
02 The problem
Agents have credentials. They don't have context or limits.
How the business works is scattered
It lives across documents, systems and people. Turning it into a governed, ontology-grounded graph is slow, manual and stale on arrival.
Nothing governs the moment of action
An agent with API credentials can execute regardless of contract, authority or evidence. Guardrails filter text; they don't decide whether a payment should go out.
Enterprises need both, connected : a living graph of how the business operates, and a runtime that enforces it on every agent action.

09 FAQ
Questions, answered
More in the glossary and guides .
What is TrustGraph?
TrustGraph is an open-source AI Governance Platform: a context graph with a deterministic agent runtime. It builds an ontology-grounded knowledge graph of how a business operates, then checks every action an AI agent proposes against that graph, returning allow, pause or deny with recorded evidence.
What is a context graph?
A context graph connects business entities, relationships, policies and evidence so AI systems act with the full meaning of a request. Thoughtworks uses TrustGraph as its definition of a context graph.
How is this different from AI guardrails?
Guardrails filter what a model says. TrustGraph governs what an agent does: it resolves the business entities an action affects and evaluates authority, scope, risk, uncertainty, time and value before execution.
Which standards does TrustGraph use?
W3C RDF 1.2, OWL, SHACL and PROV-O. Your ontologies, policies and decision records stay portable with no lock-in.
Is TrustGraph open source?
Yes, the core is open source. Enterprise add-ons cover knowledge-based access controls and IAM, an attestation engine, temporal knowledge management and the deterministic agent runtime, with engineering support for production deployments.
