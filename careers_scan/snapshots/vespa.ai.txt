Who We Are
Our culture thrives on collaboration, long-term vision, passion, and continuous improvement. We believe AI-driven search will empower our customers’ success, and we are dedicated to that vision of unlocking their data for profitable outcomes.
At Vespa, every voice matters. We make decisions through open information sharing and seek diverse perspectives. No one gets into trouble for speaking their mind. We empower individuals to drive progress with organizational ownership that focuses on enabling, not hindering, growth. Technical excellence and long-term impact guide us. We are not scared to learn from failure. We take collective responsibility for all we do to ensure customer success.
