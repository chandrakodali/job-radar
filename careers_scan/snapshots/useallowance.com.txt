Founding Engineer
Digital purchases are a $1.5T market. A meaningful share of that will be driven by AI agents. We're building the payments infrastructure for that shift.
Right now there's no good way for an AI to make a purchase. People paste card numbers into chat, create virtual cards with arbitrary limits, or just stop and do it themselves. We're building the right layer: set a limit, lock it to a merchant, your AI handles the rest.
The app is live and people are using it. This is a new primitive for how software transacts. You'd be joining as the first engineer on the team — helping define how we build from here.
What you'll work on
Agent experiences that take a purchase from conversation to checkout without guesswork
Eval and reliability infrastructure that gets smarter as more agents use the product
Multi-channel experiences — push, SMS, and wherever approvals actually need to happen
Integrations with payment networks, cloud browser providers, and merchant systems
You'll work directly with me. No layers.

You have experience building AI products in production — ideally agent workflows, conversational experiences, or evaluation and reliability systems — and are excited to make them trustworthy enough for customers to use with real money.
You're comfortable operating with ambiguity and moving quickly in an early-stage environment.
Payments or fintech experience is a plus, not required.
