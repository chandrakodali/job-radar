About Yoneda Labs
At Yoneda Labs, we use state-of-the-art lab automation to run hundreds of organic chemical reactions a week.
Using this experimental dataset, we plan to create an AI model that will be able to predict high-yielding
reaction conditions for organic reactions without any wet-lab experimentation.
Lab Technician
Full-time Position
Responsibilities
Ensure the day-to-day running of our wet lab
Prepare powders and stock solutions to feed to our robots
Maintain the lab equipment
Analyze HPLC-MS and NMR results using our semi-automatic pipeline
Troubleshoot unexpected challenges that show up in our wet lab (e.g. purify impure compounds that we receive from suppliers)
Help set up a bigger wet lab
Help shape the overall chemistry strategy of Yoneda
About Us & What We Offer
We are an early-stage startup — currently a team of 3 friends in their 20s with backgrounds in Chemistry
and Computer Science — and we hope to build datasets and models that will revolutionize how organic synthesis is done.
Apart from a fast-paced startup environment, we offer an interesting job at the intersection of Chemistry
and Data Science in a well-funded lab. As we are a small team without a strongly defined structure, you
will be able to work on what you're best at without any unnecessary internal meetings or reports, helping
to define the direction and success of the company.
Compensation: Competitive salary and equity, plus full health, medical, and dental insurance.
