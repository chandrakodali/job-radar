Recurring Themes in
the Work we Do
Miden is a mission-driven company, which means we find ourselves returning to a similar set of questions in everything we do. If you already think about a few of these, chances are high you'll love working here.
HOW DO WE MAKE PAYMENT COMPLEXITY A THING OF THE PAST?
For too long, businesses across Africa have had to navigate fragmented, unreliable, over-complicated payments infrastructure. We're rebuilding it from the ground up — with a developer-first, enterprise-grade stack that gives businesses real control, transparency, and scalability. How do we keep making this simpler, faster, and more reliable for every business that depends on us?
HOW DO WE BRING MORE PEOPLE INTO THE FORMAL ECONOMY?
Every business onboarded, every payment processed, every new market opened — these aren't just metrics. They represent real people and real enterprises gaining access to financial systems that can transform their trajectory. How do we accelerate this, and make sure the infrastructure we build serves the broadest possible range of businesses?
WHAT NEW BUSINESS MODELS CAN OUR INFRASTRUCTURE MAKE POSSIBLE?
Embedded banking. Card programmes. Cross-border disbursements. When we built the right infrastructure, entirely new categories of products became possible. Which industries can we help unlock next? What would African businesses build if they had access to truly world-class financial tools?
HOW DO WE FIND AND GROW THE BEST TALENT ON THE CONTINENT?
Some of the most extraordinary people in the world are building and creating across Africa right now. How do we reliably find them? And once they join us, how do we invest in them — so they do the best work of their lives right here at Miden?
