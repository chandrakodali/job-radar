01 Engineering
Senior AI Engineer
Exploratory conversation
Who we’re looking for
We’re looking for an AI engineer who has taken LLM or machine learning systems beyond a demo and into production. You enjoy working with messy technical knowledge, testing what actually works and owning the quality of the answers people rely on.
You’ll be a good fit if you bring
Hands-on experience building and operating retrieval or LLM applications, including their data pipelines and evaluation.
Strong software engineering skills and an ability to weigh answer quality against latency, cost and reliability.
A habit of testing assumptions with real users and explaining technical trade-offs clearly to product colleagues.
What you would work on
Build retrieval and reasoning workflows across technical documentation and maintenance history.
Define evaluations that measure answer quality, traceability and usefulness on the shop floor.
Take AI capabilities from experiment to reliable operation with the product and engineering team.
What to include in your email
Your CV as a PDF, with a LinkedIn or professional profile link if you have one.
A few personal lines on why you want to join Pairio, why this role interests you and what you would contribute.
Include a short example of an AI system you shipped: your contribution, how you evaluated it and what you learned. A public project or GitHub link is welcome, if available.
Apply by email careers@pairio.com
Scope, location, working model and timing are discussed directly with the founders.

Full-Stack Engineer

Experience shipping and maintaining full-stack web applications, from accessible interfaces to backend services.
Confidence with API design, databases, authentication, automated testing and debugging production issues.
The ability to scope an ambiguous problem, communicate trade-offs and improve a feature after customer feedback.
Develop end-to-end workflows for troubleshooting, documentation and knowledge access.
Connect industrial data sources through robust APIs and integrations.
Shape architecture, performance and engineering standards as the product develops.
Tell us about a product or feature you built end to end, the decisions you owned and its outcome. Add a GitHub, live product or project link if you can share one.

A portfolio of shipped products showing your role, design reasoning and what changed for users.
Experience running research, mapping complex workflows and testing prototypes with customers.
Strong interaction and visual design skills, attention to accessibility and close collaboration with engineers.

Good to know

What does Pairio build?
Pairio has its office in Heilbronn, Germany, and develops an AI maintenance assistant for manufacturing and industrial teams. The product supports troubleshooting, technical knowledge access and maintenance documentation.
How do I apply to Pairio?
Email careers@pairio.com with the role title, your CV as a PDF and a few lines on why you want to join Pairio and what you would contribute. Include the relevant work example or portfolio requested in the role profile. No separate cover letter or applicant account is needed.
Where would I work?
Our office is in Heilbronn, Germany. The working model, any remote arrangement, employment terms and start date for a potential role are discussed individually with the founders.
Can I send an open application?
Yes. If your experience could help Pairio in another area, email careers@pairio.com and tell us what you would like to contribute. You can write in German or English.
