Bay Area or LA/OC
Full-time
Growth Lead
Find and prove out new acquisition channels hands-on, then bring in the right resources to scale what works. You’ll own channel strategy, ROI, and sustainable revenue growth.
Reports to: Cofounder / Head of Marketing
Salary range: $120,000–$150,000
About the role
Locale is looking for a Growth Lead to find and prove out new acquisition channels. You’ll scout channels, run them hands-on, and bring in the right resource once one proves it can scale. Success is measured by real, sustainable revenue growth.
What you’ll do
Scout and operate new acquisition channels, from campaign setup and copy to budget management.
Set clear success criteria, analyze performance, and decide what is worth scaling.
Bring in agencies, employees, or contractors to scale what works while retaining ownership of strategy and results.
Track and communicate channel performance and ROI with a focus on revenue.
What we’re looking for
Ideally 3–5 years in growth or performance marketing with hands-on channel management.
A track record of testing and scaling acquisition channels from scratch.
Comfort with ambiguity and a data-driven, revenue-focused approach.
Experience managing agencies, contractors, or channel-specific hires is a plus.
Based in, or willing to relocate within, the SF Bay Area or LA/OC region.
Compensation and logistics
$120,000–$150,000 base salary, depending on experience.
Full-time and remote-first, with occasional in-person office days.
Must live within Locale’s delivery radius in the Bay Area or LA/OC.

Find, evaluate, and build relationships with creators and influencers who are a strong fit for Locale.
Manage creator partnerships, including communication, briefs, timelines, deliverables, and follow-up.
Identify and develop new strategic and company partnership opportunities.
Own partner relationships from outreach and negotiation through launch and ongoing management.
Coordinate with marketing and other internal teams to keep partnerships organized and effective.
Track partnership activity and results, share learnings, and improve programs over time.
A thoughtful relationship builder who communicates clearly and follows through.
Experience in partnerships, creator or influencer programs, business development, or a related role.
Strong organization and the ability to manage several relationships and projects at once.
Comfort moving between hands-on outreach and longer-term partnership strategy.
A collaborative, practical approach and good judgment about brand fit.
$80,000–$100,000 base salary, depending on experience.
Full-time role reporting to Locale’s Cofounder / CMO.
Based in the SF Bay Area or LA/OC region.
