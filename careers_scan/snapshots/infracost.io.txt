Values
Ustomer, not customer
It is all about seeing us and the customer as one. We like to be a part of the user’s team, and help them however we can. If the user is not successful, then we will not be either so we try to walk in their shoes. It’s more than work — we build relationships and community with users and customers.
Open is our core
Put yourself out there. Show your learning. Transparency builds confidence. Encourage sharing the good and the bad. The best decisions are made when everyone has access to all the data. Be straightforward and kind, feedback is about the work, not the individual.
Let's JEDI
Let’s Just Effing Do It! Own it and move fast. A good plan fiercely executed now is better than a perfect plan later. We ask for help and unblock each other. The main thing is to keep the main thing the main thing.

Hiring process
Application review: Submit your application and we’ll aim to respond within 1-2 weeks, depending on the amount of applications.
Intro call: Meet with our recruiter to share more about what you are looking for in your next role and to hear more about our team and how we work
Interviews with the team: We typically have three interviews which are a mix of technical/skills evaluation and culture/behavioral questions to see if our working styles match. Some of these might be 1:1 and others might be with a few people on our team. For some roles, we ask for a take home assignment, in which case we aim to put in as much time reviewing on our end as you put into it.
References: We talk to three people who have worked with you. This helps us get a sense of what it will be like working together.
Offer: Hooray! We are excited to work with you and will share our offer (a combination of salary and equity).
Background check: As part of our security compliance, we use Checkr to perform a background check. If there is anything that might show up on your report, please let us know in advance and we can talk through whether this has an impact on your role.
