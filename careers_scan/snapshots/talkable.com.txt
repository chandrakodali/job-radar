Talkable Values
Pursue Quality in Everything
This is our cornerstone value. We seek excellence in all that we do, recognizing there is always room to grow.
Drive Customer Value
We are lucky that we can measure every time we introduce someone to a cool new product or brand! We exist to help companies grow by rewarding their loyal customers instead of paying big publishers for advertising.
Focus on the Long-Term
We develop long-term partnerships with our customers. We design our products and teams for long term success. We innovate and move fast, but we do not cut corners.
Help Others
Leveraging different perspectives and having mutual respect for one another is paramount. We are team players ready to help our colleagues and customers.
Share Positive Energy
Passion for our work and positive energy allows both our customers and coworkers to enjoy working with us. We respect each other and are enthusiastic about the work we do.
Be Transparent
We are truthful with ourselves and others about the actions we take. We pride ourselves on sharing information, even when it’s uncomfortable to do so.
Act Like An Owner
The cornerstone of our culture and our management philosophy. Talkable celebrates autonomy and encourages everyone to make a difference. We look for extraordinary people and enable them to achieve their potential. There is no bureaucracy and nothing is someone else’s problem. You are free to fly.
