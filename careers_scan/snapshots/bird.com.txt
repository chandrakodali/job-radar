Bring your judgment. Put it to work.
Our Anti-Handbook sets out the freedom and responsibility that come with working here.
01 / Autonomy
Make the call.
Decisions belong close to the problem. Bring context, make a reasoned choice and own the result. Pull people in when a decision is hard to reverse.
02 / Craft
Remove the unnecessary.
Find the essential problem. Challenge the extra step, the inherited process and the code that adds more complexity than it removes.
03 / Tools
Work with agents.
Direct machines to do more of the repeatable work. Use your experience to set direction, ask better questions and judge the outcome.
04 / Ownership
Make your work count.
We work remotely and asynchronously, with clear communication and responsibility for results. Rest matters. Hours online are a poor measure of contribution.
