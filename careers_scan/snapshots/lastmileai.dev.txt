Founding Mission
Building the first Cognitive Computer
Generative AI has unlocked a cognitive era for computing, allowing computers to interact with the world in novel ways through vision, speech and text.
We are building a new kind of computer: one that always has perfect context about a person, team and organization; connects to every tool and service a user authorizes; and anticipates needs while orchestrating tasks with humans and AI alike.
LastMile AI exists to make that cognitive computer useful, reliable and accessible for the people and organizations doing their most important work.
