About Us
Open Source Security, Inc exists to both fill the void left by
underfunded and understaffed attention to security in Linux and advance the state
of the art in system defense. As creators of the technologies behind DEP, ASLR, CFI, and many other
defensive measures adopted in one form or another by all modern operating systems,
Open Source Security, Inc's track record for understanding vulnerabilities and
creating effective defenses is unmatched in the industry.
The company's central office is based in Lancaster, PA USA
with employees operating both locally and remotely across the USA and Europe.
Open Source Security, Inc combines its deep expertise with swift and
detailed support for its customers. It acts as a trusted advisor and an extension
of the security and kernel engineering teams of its customers across several
industries with high security needs that cannot be filled anywhere else.
