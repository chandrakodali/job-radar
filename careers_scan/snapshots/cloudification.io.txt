Senior Ceph Engineer | Kubernetes & Rook (m/f/d, full-time & remote)
We’re looking for an experienced Ceph Engineer to join our growing distributed team and play a key role in the design and operations of the storage infrastructure of our cloud platform – c12n.cloud
About the Role:
As a Ceph Engineer, you’ll be responsible for building, scaling, and maintaining highly available Ceph clusters, integrated with Kubernetes via Rook. You’ll work on real production environments used by customers across industries, ensuring the performance, reliability, and resilience of their data storage.
You’ll collaborate closely with other engineers, DevOps specialists, and architects to deliver secure, scalable, and future-proof infrastructure solutions — all using open-source tools and platforms.
What You’ll Do:
Architect, upgrade and maintain tens of Ceph clusters with Rook.
Automate infrastructure provisioning using Ansible and Helm.
Monitor and troubleshoot performance, availability, and scaling issues.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement best practices in cloud-native infrastructure.
What You Bring:
5 + years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or Infrastructure Engineering
Deep hands-on experience with Ceph in production environments at PB scale
Knowledge of Kubernetes and particularly of Rook operator
Excellent Linux system administration skills and scripting abilities
Familiarity with observability tools like Prometheus and Grafana
Fluency in English and strong communication skills
Bonus: Experience with OpenStack, contributing to open-source projects, or working with cloud-native CI/CD pipelines
What We OFFER:
High degree of autonomy and flexible working hours that adapt to your lifestyle.
100% remote work – live and work from anywhere in the world (with optional business trips to Berlin)
26 paid vacation days per year + Christmas and New Year’s Eve off
Option for a coworking membership in your city.
A MacBook Pro and hardware or software you need to be productive.
Be part of a company that contributes to the open-source ecosystem and is recognized by the OpenInfra Foundation.
