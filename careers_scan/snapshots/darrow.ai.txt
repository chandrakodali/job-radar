How We Work
Darrowers are interdisciplinary to the core. Legal thinkers, AI engineers, data scientists, and intelligence analysts work in close formation - not in silos.
The people who build the systems are in constant dialogue with the people
who understand the legal environments those systems are designed to decode.
We practice intentional communication: listening carefully, speaking clearly,
and welcoming disagreement in the service of better outcomes. Rigorous debate and collaboration aren't in tension here: they are the same thing.
