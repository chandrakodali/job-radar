02 / Our values
How we work, in five lines .
Embrace a growth mindset.
We get better by doing harder things. We treat feedback as a gift, run small experiments, and ship the next version of the work even when this one looks fine.
Genuinely care.
About the people we serve. About each other. About the craft. Care shows up as the small, unglamorous work that compounds: a clearer write-up, a better edge case caught, an honest hand-off.
We’ll figure it out.
Bias to action when the path is unclear. We’d rather try, learn, and adjust than wait for permission. The work is too important to stall on the question of whose lane it sits in.
Say what you mean.
Direct, candid, and kind. We disagree in writing, in meetings, and on Slack, then we commit. Being clear is part of being kind.
Raise the bar.
We’re building the company that proves brain–body therapy can scale. That standard belongs in everything we ship: every program, every email, every line of code, every interview.
03 / Life at MSH
Real impact. Real momentum.
One day, you’re shaping the next trial protocol with researchers at Monash or Baylor. The next, running an A/B test on signup, filming a patient story in our studio, or pushing a model update that personalises every session. The work is wide. The pace is fast.
We celebrate the wins together, including measurably impacting patients every day.
