Riga office benefits
We support your work, well-being, and growth so that you can thrive both professionally and personally.
Flexible Work
Work from the office, remotely, or in a hybrid setup - depending on your role and location. We support whatever best fits your needs.
Health & Well-Being
Comprehensive health coverage and wellness support designed to keep you healthy, focused, and energized throughout your career with us.
Growth & Learning
Develop continuously through certifications, courses, conferences, and real hands-on experience that helps you grow your skills.
Global Experience
Opportunities to collaborate with colleagues and customers worldwide. Many roles include opportunities for travel.
Team Culture & Events
Regular team-building activities, sports, celebrations, and company events that bring people together.
Rewards & Extras
Enjoy performance bonuses, company gifts, branded goodies, and small surprises that recognize your contribution.
Great location & parking
Our office is easily accessible by public transport, car, bicycle, or on foot, with parking available for both cars and bikes - no matter how you choose to get to work, it’s easy!
A modern workspace
A comfortable and modern office environment with spaces for teamwork, focused work, and informal discussions. Our headquarters in Riga brings together many department leads and core teams, placing you close to key product decisions.
Coffee & snacks
You can count on coffee, tea, healthy snacks, and a fridge stocked with goodies to keep you energized and refreshed throughout the working day.
Quiet & relaxation zones
Comfortable lounge and relaxation areas designed for quiet breaks during the day, including space to recharge with a massage chair.
Games & activity areas
Take an active break with table tennis, foosball, and other casual activities to unwind and connect between work sessions.
More than just work
From seasonal celebrations to sports activities and team building events, we create opportunities to connect outside everyday work.
Why work at Zabbix?
Open source first: Join a team that builds one of the world’s leading open source monitoring platforms.
Global reach: Our technology is used by organizations worldwide across multiple industries, including finance, telecom, education, healthcare and more.
Innovation & growth: We empower you to grow your career with real responsibility, ongoing learning, and the chance to shape your future along with our product.
Openness & transparency: We build in the open, communicate honestly, and believe that trust comes from sharing knowledge and working together.
Continuous improvement: We grow every day - improving our products, our skills, and how we work, with a focus on quality and long-term impact.
Built for real impact: Everything we create is designed to solve real problems for real users, helping businesses and teams work more effectively.
Global mindset, local heart: We work across cultures and countries while staying grounded, respectful, and connected to the people and communities around us.
