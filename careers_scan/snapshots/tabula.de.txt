Building Tabula
Join the team building the AI Financial Operating System for SMEs and their Accountants, starting in Germany.
Help us execute on a massive vision
Accounting is the source of truth on what happens in a business. Every decision hinges on knowing the numbers. Every company does accounting.
For centuries, doing the books has taken dozens of hours a month. As a result, small business owners have worked with financial data that is hopelessly out of date, fragmented and unreliable.
AI is the steam engine moment for accounting. For the first time, it's become possible to bring the work needed to understand your business from hours to minutes.
Our mission is to help company owners make better financial decisions , and to empower accountants to focus on their advisory role . We are doing this by building the AI financial operating system that does the work for you.
