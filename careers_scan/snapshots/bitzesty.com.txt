Our values
We believe that by forming strong relationships with colleagues and clients, we can be better than the sum of our parts. Our focus on human-centered design helps us find smart solutions to our clients’ problems. And our commitment to quality means the services and products we create have a positive impact on society.
Work remotely
From the very beginning, we established effective remote-working practices. We believe it gives our people a better quality of life . It opens up opportunities for those who otherwise would not be able to work with us and helps us do our bit in preserving the environment .
Remote working and being in different countries doesn’t stop our team bonding. We always come together virtually to have fun, socialise and bond as a team.
Bit Zesty is one of the top UK companies for remote working At the end of 2020, Resume.io ranked Bit Zesty as the 2nd best UK employer for remote working.
