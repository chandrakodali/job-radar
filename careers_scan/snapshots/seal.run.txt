Same day
Short interview with a founder
If we see a potential fit, we’ll message you the same day to arrange an interview. Do not prepare. The format of the interview is unusual and direct: we are trying to figure out as quickly as possible why it might not be a fit. Offer or work trial
If it’s a yes, we’ll make an offer. Application to decision in under 24 hours, including screening, interview and follow-up questions; if you’re not currently in a role, the decision may be a work trial. Real urgency is our first principle, and we’ll expect you to match it.
