Important Recruitment Scam Alert
Know how to spot and avoid recruitment scams.
At Wells Fargo, candidate safety is important to us. We are aware of fraudulent individuals posing as recruiters and using fake interviews or offer letters to obtain money or personal information. Wells Fargo recruiters always use @wellsfargo.com email addresses, never request payment or sensitive data, and conduct interviews only on approved enterprise platforms. Learn how to recognize recruitment scams on our Job Scams Awareness page , and report suspicious messages to [email protected] .
