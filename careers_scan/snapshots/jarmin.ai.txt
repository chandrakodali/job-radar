What working here is like
A tight-knit team in San Francisco
Our engineering team works in person because the problems are still changing shape weekly and the fastest way through them is the same room. Our sales roles can be remote in the US. You will know everyone you work with.
You define and build the latest technology in AI
We build with techniques before they are widely known. You will read the research, decide what is worth using, and be the one who puts it into production.
Ownership from day one
Whole parts of the product have one name on them. You will decide how your area works and ship it to customers who are running their operations on it, not implement someone else's spec.
Define your own career path
The people who join now shape the roles that come after them. What you own is set by what you are good at and what you take on, not by a ladder someone else drew.
