Our mission
To modernize logistics across the $1T+ North American freight market.
Setting a new standard for North American freight
Nuvocargo combines AI agents with experienced freight operators on NuvoOS to run managed transportation, freight brokerage and customs brokerage from PO to POD.
2019
Nuvocargo started in 2019 with some of the hardest freight in North America: trucks crossing between the US and Mexico. We learned the work by doing it, load after load, with our own people answering for every one.
2024
In early 2024 we put what we had learned into NuvoOS, the system we built to run our own operation.
2025
In 2025 we took NuvoOS across North America. Merge Transportation, a South Carolina team running freight across the US, joined us, and so did Mentum, a Y Combinator team building AI agents.
That same year Fast Company named Nuvocargo #1 in logistics among the World’s 50 Most Innovative Companies.
2026
In 2026 we launched Nuvo AI and put the AI agents that run our own operation in shippers’ hands. Their teams now move every load with them, from the quote to the invoice audit, and experienced freight operators answer for the result.
Today
Tens of thousands of loads later, our promise is the same one we started with.
You send the tender and we run the freight.

Values
How we operate
No matter what team you are on, make sure your work is adding value to our customers and building deeper customer relationships. Listen to our customers, understand their needs and pain points, and focus on what matters to them. Deliver products & services that our customers love and that address real problems for them.
Speed matters. Speed compounds. Getting back to someone today is better than tomorrow. Shipping a simple solution today is better than a perfect one in a week. We push ourselves to make decisions faster. Challenge yourself and your peers to ensure we are all moving together in the same direction, as one team. Be a force for driving velocity, every single day. Amp it up.
Quality and excellence are important in everything we do. Hold yourself and others to the highest standards. Keep raising the bar for yourself and for those around you.
We embrace the highs & lows we will face in pursuit of our mission together. Be adaptable to the constant changes that are normal in a startup. Strive to be a teammate that everyone would want by their side when faced with difficult obstacles and problems. Strive to face adversity with a sense of optimism, determination, and a good attitude.
We creatively capitalize on the resources and data at hand to drive impact for our customers and for our business. We reward small teams that drive significant impact and ROI in an outsized way. We believe this not only makes for a healthier business, but also it creates more room for Nuvos to grow and drive even more efficiencies for our customers.
We are not afraid to push back or challenge ideas. However, we must always keep moving forward as a team. Once we align on a course of action, we respect decisions and embrace them. Disagree & commit.
We represent the company in our actions in front of customers, teammates, and the communities we operate in. Make decisions like an owner, with the highest integrity.

Questions about Nuvocargo?
Nuvocargo is building the first AI-native freight execution engine for North American shippers. It combines AI agents with experienced freight operators on NuvoOS, its proprietary system of record. Nuvocargo helps shippers simplify their supply chains, reduce freight spend, improve service and adopt AI faster.
Leading shippers across North America have run tens of thousands of loads with Nuvocargo. Founded in 2019, Nuvocargo is backed by $75M+ from global investors, including Tiger Global, QED, NFX, Hi Ventures, Y Combinator and the founders of Nubank, Ramp and Deel.
We move FTL and LTL freight across North America: inside the US and cross-border with Mexico and Canada. We started on US–Mexico freight in 2019 and expanded to truckload across North America in 2025.
Nuvocargo has been named to Y Combinator’s Top Companies list and Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies (#1 in Logistics, #30 globally).
