Fern Morrison
CEO & Co-Founder
Fern spent five years in the battery industry, specializing in materials and degradation modeling at Apple and Mitra Chem. With a background in investment banking, she merges financial and battery modeling to develop innovative risk solutions for energy storage.
Gabriele Pozzato
CTO & Co-Founder
Gabriele is a modeling engineer with ten years of experience, formerly a Staff Battery Modeling Engineer at Form Energy and a Research Engineer at Stanford. He models complex systems across robotics, powertrains, electronics, and energy with physics-based, data-driven, and hybrid approaches, and has also published computer-vision algorithms for gesture recognition and imaging-based analysis of material microstructures.
