Research
Founding Robotics Researcher
Design experiments, test them on real robots, and figure out why things fail.
We’re looking for a Founding Robotics Researcher with a deep understanding of robotics and a hands-on approach to research. Someone who likes designing experiments, testing them on real robots, and figuring out why things fail.
At Shiraz AI, our research is driven by deployment. We’re working to shorten the time it takes to get a robot working reliably on a new task, and what we learn in factories shapes what we work on next.
If you’re excited about foundation models, robot learning, and making robots work outside a controlled lab, we’d love to meet you.
I. What you’ll do
Develop robot foundation models and learning methods that shorten the time it takes to deploy a robot on a new task.
Train and evaluate policies in simulation and on real robots, using reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and vision-language-action models.
Design experiments that show why a policy works or fails. Trace failures through the whole system, from model outputs down to control loops and hardware.
Work closely with our deployment and hardware engineers. Turn failures on the factory floor into research questions.
Help set Shiraz AI’s research direction as a founding member of the research team.
II. What we’re looking for
Strong grounding in both robotics fundamentals and modern AI.
A track record of original research in robot learning, foundation models, world models, reinforcement learning, or a related area, with experience taking a research question from initial formulation through experimental validation.
Hands-on experience training policies on real robots, including collecting data, building training pipelines, and debugging failures that only show up on hardware.
Rigorous experimental habits, including strong baselines, targeted ablations, and honest evaluation.
Experience with large-scale model training is a plus.
III. How to apply
Email founders@shiraz.ai with your resume and an example of your work, such as a paper, a repo, or a video of a robot you got working.

Robot Deployment Engineer
Take a robot into a customer’s factory and make it work reliably in everyday production.
We’re looking for a Robot Deployment Engineer who can take a robot into a customer’s factory and make it work reliably in everyday production. Someone with strong robotics and software skills who likes being on the factory floor, owning the result, and figuring out why things break.
At Shiraz AI, our research is driven by deployment, and deployment engineers are where that starts. What you see in factories shapes what our researchers and hardware engineers work on next.
If you’re excited about learning-based robots, complex systems, and making robots work in real manufacturing environments, we’d love to meet you.
Own robot deployments in customer factories, from workcell setup and integration with existing equipment to commissioning and validation under real production conditions.
Work directly with customers and operators to understand their workflows and what the robot needs to do.
Diagnose problems across hardware, software, cameras and perception, and controls to improve task success, throughput, and uptime.
Build tools for testing and monitoring, and write the operating and maintenance procedures that make each deployment faster than the last.
Collect training data and work with researchers and hardware engineers to turn lessons from the floor into lasting improvements.
Excellent debugging skills across hardware and software. You can track down system-level failures that don’t have an obvious cause.
Strong background in robotics, computer science, electrical engineering, or a related field.
Hands-on experience integrating, calibrating, and deploying robotic systems, including cameras and perception systems.
Hands-on electronics skills: wiring, sensors, actuators, and motion control.
Enough mechanical design to build fixtures, mounts, and simple tooling in CAD.
Comfort working directly with customers and operators, and turning what you see on their floor into practical fixes.
You take ownership and work well on your own, including on site.
Experience in factories, or with PLCs, industrial I/O, robot safety systems, or embedded systems, is a plus.
Email founders@shiraz.ai with your resume and an example of your work, such as a system you deployed, a tool you built, or a video of a robot you got working.

Mechanical Engineer

Solid understanding of materials, manufacturing processes, tolerances, and FEA.

A strong sense of ownership.
Experience designing grippers or end effectors is a plus.
