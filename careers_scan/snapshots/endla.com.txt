Our Mission
Endla is building the next generation of software for heavy industries, helping solve simulation and optimisation problems. Our vision is to provide engineers with auto-design and auto-optimisation tools that enable them to focus on understanding and specifying the right problem to solve and allow the software to produce high performing solutions.
Working at Endla
We’re a fast-paced, innovative team working together in person in Brisbane, building tools for engineers in heavy industries.
The work
From engineering questions to operational decisions.
Endla connects engineering data, models and workflows in one operational platform. Our customers work on wells, pipelines and production systems. We help them move from scattered data and manual calculations to decisions they can check and act on.
Understand the problem
Work with the engineers using our software. Learn their constraints, question assumptions and agree what a useful result looks like.
Build it together
Bring software and engineering thinking to the same table. Share what you learn and turn customer insights into a stronger product.
Follow through
Take work through to deployment and everyday use. Check the results, help users succeed and improve what comes next.
