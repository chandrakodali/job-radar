Why this seat
You'd be the first commercial hire, not the fifth.
Mantis builds living, predictive models of individual people — pre-symptomatic
illness detection, decompensation forecasting, adherence prediction — and sells scoped,
consented access to those predictions. The technology works. What it doesn't have yet is
someone who owns the revenue side of it. That's this job, and it comes with the latitude that
being first implies: you'll set the pitch, pick the segments, and build the pipeline from
nothing.
An honest product to sell
We don't sell data — we sell scoped, revocable predictions with the person's consent.
You will never have to talk around what happens to a patient's information.
Evidence in the deck
Every claim traces to peer-reviewed work: 81% of COVID cases flagged before symptom
onset, a measured 93% ceiling on behavioral predictability. You're selling published
results, not vapor.
Uncapped, and paid on it
15% of first-year contract value on everything you close, on top of base. Healthcare
contracts are large. The math works in your favor.
