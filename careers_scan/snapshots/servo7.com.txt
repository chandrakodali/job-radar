About us
Who we are
We're a small team with an office and a mini-factory in Amsterdam, with an actual shipping container inside. Everyone is hands-on: writing code, on the road to the warehouse, helping each other out where needed.
We come from military drones, surgical robots, LLM research, and world models. Now we build robots that do real work in the real world.
What we're building
AI-powered robots for the hardest, most demanding job in logistics: unloading loose-loaded shipping containers at the dock door, the entry point of the entire supply chain.
Our robots work in the field, doing the job for paying customers. For 3 months in a row, we have doubled our customer base. We operate across western Europe, with global growth ahead of us. Now we need more engineers to keep up with demand.
