FAQ
Frequently asked questions.
What is Cake Labs?
Cake Labs (Cake Labs LLC) builds open-source, non-custodial crypto apps for everyday use: Cake Wallet, Monero.com, Cake Pay and Cupcake. Founded in 2017, it makes apps relied on by more than 2 million people.
Who makes Cake Wallet, and which coins does it support?
Cake Labs makes Cake Wallet , its first app, shipped in 2018. It is a non-custodial wallet for Monero, Bitcoin with Lightning, Litecoin, Zcash and more, with built-in swaps, buy and sell, and hardware-wallet support.
Does Cake Labs hold my crypto?
No. None of our products custody your funds. You keep your own keys, and the decisions are always yours.
Are Cake Labs apps open source and free?
Yes. Our apps are free to use, and the code for Cake Wallet, Monero.com and Cupcake is open source at github.com/cake-tech .
Which platforms are the apps available on?
Cake Wallet runs on iOS, Android, macOS and Linux. Monero.com and Cupcake are available on iOS and Android.
What is Cake Pay?
Cake Pay turns crypto into gift cards and prepaid Visa and Mastercard cards for 150,000+ merchants, at face value: a $20 card costs $20. Use it at cakepay.com or right inside Cake Wallet.
Where is Cake Labs based?
Cake Labs is based in Nevis and the USA.
