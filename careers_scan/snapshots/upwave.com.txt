Phishing Scam Notification
We’ve been made aware of a phishing scam involving individuals impersonating recruiters from Upwave. Please know:
We never ask candidates for payment, financial information, or personal documents as part of our hiring process.
All legitimate communication from our team comes solely from @upwave.com email addresses
You can see all legitimate opportunities at upwave.com/careers
If you’ve been contacted by someone posing as a recruiter from Upwave, please do not engage, and report the message to the platform where you received it. We take this seriously and are actively working to stop the misuse of our brand. Thank you for helping us protect the integrity of our candidate experience.
