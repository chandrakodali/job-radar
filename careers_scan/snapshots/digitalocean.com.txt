Interviewing with DigitalOcean
Here's what you can expect to happen after you apply to an open role at DigitalOcean.
Your resume will be reviewed by a real-life human, not a robot (hooray humans!). If selected for an interview, a recruiter will reach out within two to three weeks to schedule a video call via Google Meet.
A member of our recruiting team will conduct an initial interview over Google Meet.
During the Skills Assessment stage of our interview process, candidates participate in role-specific evaluations designed to measure the technical, functional, and behavioral competencies required for success in the position. Our goal is to create a fair and consistent process that allows candidates to demonstrate their abilities while giving the hiring team meaningful insight into their skills and potential.
Candidates get the chance to meet with several other members of the team in a round of interviews. Depending on the role, there will be an assessment included in the process.
We will extend an offer to successful candidates with full compensation and benefits details.
New employees receive IT equipment and go through comprehensive remote onboarding sessions as part of their orientation.
Four ways to prepare for your interview
Get to know DigitalOcean
Visit our company page to learn about our mission, values, and products.
Tell us about your experience
We want to hear about how you’ve solved complex problems, adapted to unexpected changes, driven innovation, and more. Bring your authentic self and some stories — then be prepared to answer questions that start with, “Tell us about a time when…”
Describe what you’re passionate about
What excites you? What kind of impact do you want to make? How do your values align with DigitalOcean’s 7 values? We want to hear about what drives you and why you’d be a great addition to our team.
Ask questions
What questions do you have for us? What did you want to learn about us that you haven’t? Do not leave the interview process with a question unanswered!

I just applied for a job with DigitalOcean! When should I expect to hear from you?
DigitalOcean’s Talent Acquisition team reviews all applications and reaches out to the candidates we would like to move forward with in a timely manner. If your application is not selected for the role you applied to, we also will let you know via email.
Can I apply to multiple jobs?
Candidates are welcome to apply to 3 jobs within a 180 day period. We encourage you to apply to the roles that you believe are the best match for you. This gives the Talent Acquisition team more time to dedicate to each application and helps you focus on the jobs that are best matched to you, uniquely!
I need accommodation for my interview. Can you help?
We would love to help. Please send an email to recops@digitalocean.com and let us know how we can assist with your accommodation request.
Can you share DigitalOcean’s Total Rewards program?
We are happy to! Please click through our program that is uniquely designed for each country. Currently, we are actively hiring in the United States, India and Pakistan.
How does DigitalOcean support its employees and communities?
DigitalOcean enables its employees to do the best work of their careers by investing in the employee experience. Whether that’s building in-person connections at one of our Local Meetups, online in one of our Belonging Spaces, or through motivating our employees through our reward and recognition platform. When you join DO, you’ll join a rich community of Sharks who care for one another and our customers.
I got an offer from someone claiming to be with DigitalOcean. Is this legitimate?
There is an increased number of fraudulent activities taking advantage of DigitalOcean’s job applicants. Please note that our only official domain is @digitalocean.com so if the sender of your interview invite or offer is from anyone other than the official domain, unfortunately, that is not a legitimate request.
