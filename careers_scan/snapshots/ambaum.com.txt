About Ambaum
Ambaum is a fast-growing digital consulting and eCommerce agency and a leader in the Shopify movement. As a Shopify Premier Partner, we help scale digital brands through a mix of strategy, design, development, and growth marketing.
Our focus is on creating nimble, flexible, and cost-effective enterprise commerce solutions using Shopify Plus, the fastest-growing Enterprise eCommerce platform. To do this we pair an exceptional knowledge of the Shopify platform and ecosystem with design and strategy to help our clients fuel their digital growth.
We offer competitive salaries (DOE), full health and dental insurance plus paid vacation and holiday (after 90 days). We are headquartered south of Seattle in Burien, WA, but we offer the ability to work 100% remotely if that suits you.
Benefits:
• Comptitive salaries (DOE)
• Full medical / dental insurance
• Opportunity to grow
• Personal development
• Work from home or office
• Collaborative and creative environment
• Ongoing opportunities for training
