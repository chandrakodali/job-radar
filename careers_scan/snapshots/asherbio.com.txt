AsherBio, along with other biotech companies, has become aware of a surge in email scams targeting prospective job candidates within our industry. Should you receive communication from an individual about a job opportunity claiming to be an AsherBio recruiter with an alternative domain, we kindly request you to report the incident to our team. Please send an email to info@asherbio.com with the details.
Please also be aware that all job postings will be listed on our website at asherbio.com/careers/.
