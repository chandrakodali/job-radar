About Dost Education
(registered in India as
'Dost Learning Foundation')
"Dost" means "friend" in Hindi. Since 2016 we have helped families across low-income and rural India turn everyday talk, care, and play into early learning, on any phone, with no internet or reading required, reinforced by Anganwadi workers inside the public system. Every home a playful learning space. Every child curious, confident, and school-ready.
