Life at Isovalent
Building open source software is the engine of our innovation and the root of our engineering culture. We believe that open source leads to great technology and the highest quality software and we apply its best practices across the board.
Inclusive environment
Inclusive teams and culture are very important to us. Inclusive and diverse teams lead to better decisions, creative innovation, and well-balanced teams. We offer an environment that works for you, from calendar blocks for focus time, to work and adventure balance that best suits your productivity.
Remote-first
We strongly believe in a remote-first approach. Our teams currently collaborate successfully across 27 different countries, and across 12 time zones. For those who prefer to work in offices, Cisco has offices around the world.
