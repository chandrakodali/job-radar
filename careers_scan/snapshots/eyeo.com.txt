Our culture
What we stand for
Working with us means stepping into a culture built on trust, strong accountability, and a deep commitment to collective growth. We are a team of doers focused on building a better tomorrow, and we expect each other to live by actionable principles every day. This isn’t just what we value. It’s how we work.
Our values
How we work together
Care
We actively look after one another, amplify successes, and immediately unblock peers who are struggling. We advise constructively, avoid gatekeeping, and seek teammates who offer support and ensure collaboration flows freely.
Judgement
We value principled decisions informed by data and input from all stakeholders. We own our decisions and hold ourselves accountable. We look for people who challenge the status quo and openly own mistaken decisions with humility.
Transparency
Good decisions require radical transparency about the good, the bad, and the ugly. We share strong opinions loosely held to encourage constructive dissent and seek those who engage openly with uncomfortable facts. We bias toward clear, written communication.
Bravery
To make a real dent in the world, we must operate at the edge of our abilities, which means failing often. We publicly highlight failures to learn and praise those who are bravely innovating and advancing their skills.
Action
We are a team of doers where action always trumps talk; we only build a better tomorrow if we actually build. We value people who deliver the near impossible and actively remove unnecessary blockers for others.
Inclusivity
To build a better world for everyone, we must build with everyone, continually seeking different perspectives. We value those who collaborate broadly for inclusive decision making and actively listen to the quietest voices.
Generalist specialists
We are T-shaped: we can all do everything, and each of us is truly exceptional at something. We intentionally take on work outside our specialist area and seek people who show deep mastery while empowering others through teaching.

Recruitment process
Step 1
Apply
We aim to review applications within 48 hours. If selected, we will reach out shortly after.
Step 2
Recruiter stage
The first interview covers your background, motivation, and key skills. The recruiter will also discuss logistics like hours, location, and salary.
Step 3
Technical, values, and lead stage
The technical interview(s) assess your role-specific skills and may include a take-home assignment or case study, depending on the role. The values interview explores how you contribute to the team through behavioral questions. The lead (and final) interview involves a manager or C-level executive covering any final details not addressed earlier in the process.
* Please note that the number of interviews varies depending on position type and team. The recruiter will let you know how many to expect. All our interviews are held via video call.
Step 4
Decision
We aim to complete the process within four weeks, though it may take longer due to business demands. Once finalized, we’ll share the outcome and provide feedback. If it’s a match, you’ll receive an offer. If not, we’ll provide constructive feedback for future opportunities.
