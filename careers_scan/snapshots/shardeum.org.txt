How RWA Becomes Programmable
Shardeum transforms real-world assets, rights and entitlements into programmable onchain assets through an auditable four-step process powered by EVM-compatible smart contracts.
Tokenize
Represent real-world assets, rights or entitlements as EVM-compatible tokens through smart contracts.
Mint & Record
Issue tokens against approved real-world records and establish an immutable, verifiable onchain ownership history.
Program the Lifecycle
Set the rules to define how assets can be transferred, paused, redeemed, or retired - and let smart contracts execute those rules automatically.
Connect & Settle
Connect with wallets, marketplaces and payment rails for near-instant, low-fee onchain settlement.

Solving the Trilemma
Scalability
Thanks to dynamic state sharding, consensus is reached at the transaction level. This means high speed because of parallel processing, and low fees.
Security
From our combined proof-of-stake and proof-of-quorum approach to consensus, to our auto rotation mechanism we focus on security every step of the way.
Decentralization
Shardeum is permissionless and built for the community to run nodes that increase TPS (Transactions Per Second), and participate regardless of their locations and backgrounds.
