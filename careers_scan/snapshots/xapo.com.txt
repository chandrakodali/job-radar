Why Work with Xapo Bank?
Fully Remote
Xapo Bank was one of the first companies to go fully remote: in fact, it was founded that way. We’ve learned how to do it right, keeping virtual meetings productive and maintaining a genuine sense of connection. Embracing Bitcoin's global ethos, we unite talent from every corner of the world, fostering diverse perspectives and innovation in our fully remote workforce.
Work-Life Balance
We empower our team to take control of their work schedules and enjoy a generous 35 days of paid time off each year. Beyond vacation, we provide comprehensive coverage for sick, special, and parental leave, ensuring you are supported through every stage of life, both personally and professionally.
Opportunities for Growth
Learn from some of the best in the industry and contribute to an innovative product with global reach. Xapo Bank nurtures your career by offering a personalised learning budget for courses, books, and conferences, complemented by annual performance and salary reviews. Together, these ensure you have the tools and recognition to foster continuous growth.
A True Community
Collaboration is our core, driving a dynamic culture where every idea is valued. Join a truly global community where top talent from over 50 different countries comes together to solve complex problems.
