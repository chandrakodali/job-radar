The question
When analysis gets cheap, the scarce resource becomes judgment.
Most enterprise AI conversations focus on what work can be automated. This forum is about what happens next: how leaders get to the truth, where judgment still matters, and which decisions should move closer to machines.
What should leaders know in real time?
Which decisions should still belong to people?
What changes when analysis is always available?
How does management change when information is no longer scarce?
Where does judgment become more valuable, not less?
What will the CEO and COO delegate by 2030?
