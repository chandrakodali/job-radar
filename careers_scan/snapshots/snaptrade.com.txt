Who we are
SnapTrade builds infrastructure for developers building investing products. Our APIs connect apps to brokerage accounts, giving developers access to investment data and trading without building and maintaining each brokerage integration themselves.
We're a small team backed by Y Combinator . We work across engineering, marketing, customer success, and operations to help developers build reliable products.
We value curiosity, clear thinking, and following through. You'll have room to learn, take responsibility, and contribute beyond your job title.
