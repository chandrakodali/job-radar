01 How we work
The field is part of the job
AICE is a small, on-site engineering team. The people who design the system also integrate it, test it, and learn from what happens in the water.
Own the whole system
Work across boundaries. The best answer may be mechanical, electronic, or algorithmic.
Move in days
From CAD or code to a prototype in the water, then back to the bench with evidence.
Stay close to reality
In-water prototype tests, operator feedback, and failure analysis shape the roadmap.
Use every tool
Simulation and AI-assisted engineering accelerate judgment. They do not replace it.
