FAQ
Openlayer is the AI governance and observability platform for enterprises. It gives your organization one place to see, test, and control every AI system — models, agents, and the teams behind them — before and after they ship.
Openlayer focuses on the entire AI quality lifecycle rather than just monitoring models after deployment. While many platforms specialize in observability, Openlayer combines evaluation, testing, monitoring, and governance into a single workflow. The goal is to help teams understand not only what went wrong, but also why it happened and how to prevent similar issues in the future.
Openlayer connects to your AI stack and continuously evaluates, monitors, and governs your systems. Teams use it to test models before deployment, watch performance, cost, and reliability in production, enforce policy with real-time guardrails, and keep a full audit trail for compliance.
Evaluation is becoming a continuous discipline that runs across the whole AI lifecycle instead of a one-off step before launch. As AI systems multiply and regulation catches up, every enterprise will need a trust layer between its teams and the AI they ship. Openlayer is building that layer — the infrastructure that makes AI measurable, accountable, and safe to scale.
You connect your models, agents, and pipelines to Openlayer through our SDKs, APIs, and integrations. From there, Openlayer runs your evaluations, monitors live traffic, applies guardrails in real time, and keeps governance evidence up to date — all from one control point.
Openlayer is agent-agnostic. Because it sits in the request path through the AI Gateway, any agent framework or model provider can be observed, evaluated, and governed the same way — including blocking unsafe inputs and outputs in real time.
