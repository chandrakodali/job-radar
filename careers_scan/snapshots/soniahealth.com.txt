FAQ
What is Sonia?
Sonia is an AI companion designed to provide emotional support, encourage self reflection, and help you navigate everyday challenges. You can talk through stress, relationships, work, confidence, grief, and personal growth in a private, judgment free space.
Why is it called Sonia?
Sonia, often also spelled Sonja or Sonya, is a name rooted in the Greek word for wisdom. The symbol for wisdom is the wisdom knot (see here ). At the center of the wisdom knot, you can spot a star-like shape. Stars have guided us humans for hundreds of thousands of years. And Sonia is your guide going forward.
How does Sonia work?
Sonia uses conversational AI to help you explore your thoughts, process emotions, and reflect on what's happening in your life. Built with input from therapists, psychologists, and mental health professionals, Sonia is designed to provide thoughtful, personalized support that encourages emotional wellbeing and personal growth. You can start a conversation anytime, wherever you are.
What can I talk to Sonia about?
People use Sonia for relationship challenges, anxiety, work stress, burnout, confidence, loneliness, grief, major life transitions, sleep concerns, and personal growth. You can talk about whatever is on your mind.
Can talking to AI help your mental health?
Many people find that talking through their thoughts can improve emotional awareness, reduce stress, and provide clarity. Sonia is designed to offer emotional support and encourage healthy reflection, but it is not a replacement for professional mental health care.
Can AI help with anxiety or stress?
AI can help you process anxious thoughts, organize your feelings, practice coping strategies, and reflect on stressful situations. Sonia is designed to support emotional wellbeing, but it does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.
Is AI emotional support effective?
Yes. Talking through your thoughts with an AI companion can help you process emotions, reduce stress, and gain clarity. Sonia provides immediate, judgment free support whenever you need someone to talk to. While research suggests conversational AI can improve emotional wellbeing for some people, Sonia is not a replacement for a licensed therapist or professional mental health care.
Is Sonia private?
Yes. Sonia is designed with privacy and security in mind and uses HIPAA compliant technology to help protect your information.
Is Sonia confidential?
Your conversations are private and intended to create a judgment free space where you can openly discuss your thoughts and emotions.
Is Sonia safe to use?
Sonia is built with guidance from therapists and mental health professionals and is continuously evaluated through research. It provides emotional support, but is not a replacement for professional medical care or therapy, does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions, and is not intended for emergencies or crisis situations.
