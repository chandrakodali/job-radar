Hiring a Founding Front‑End Engineer at 100x.bot (YC S22)
100x.bot, a Y Combinator S22 startup, is actively hiring a founding front‑end engineer in Bangalore to lead the UI/UX of its next‑generation AI‑driven browser automation platform.
Why This Hiring Announcement Matters
In a wave of startup hiring trends that emphasize AI expertise, 100x.bot’s call for a founding front‑end engineer stands out. The role is not a typical “convert Figma files to React components” job; it is a strategic position that will shape the visual cockpit for autonomous browser agents—technology that could redefine how businesses automate web interactions.
Read the original Hacker News discussion for the full posting and community reactions.
Company Background & YC Roots
Founded by Shardul Lavekar (ex‑Airtel AI, ex‑Ola) and Parth Mudgal (ex‑Flipkart, ex‑Ola), 100x.bot is backed by UBOS homepage , Accel, and Inventus. As a Enterprise AI platform by UBOS partner, the startup leverages UBOS’s low‑code AI infrastructure to accelerate product development.
Graduating from the YC startup batch S22 , 100x.bot has already secured seed funding and is positioned to tackle the “hardest problem in automation”: making browser agents fast, reliable, and cost‑effective.
For founders looking for a similar launchpad, the UBOS for startups page outlines how the platform accelerates AI‑first product builds.
Role Details & Core Responsibilities
What You’ll Build
Agent Visualization Engine : Interactive graph‑based UI that displays real‑time workflow logic, loops, and self‑healing events.
Dynamic MCP Interfaces : Auto‑generated UI components that adapt instantly to new Model Context Protocol (MCP) schemas without redeployment.
High‑Performance Streaming UI : Virtualized rendering (e.g., react-window ) and atomic state management (Zustand or TanStack Query) to keep logs at 60 fps.
Recorder Experience : A Chrome extension overlay built with Shadow DOM that captures user intent cleanly across third‑party sites.

Ideal candidates are React veterans (2+ years), TypeScript purists, and UI “craftspersons” who obsess over micro‑interactions. Experience with high‑frequency data streams, virtualization, and Chrome extension development is a strong plus.
For developers who love building with low‑code AI, the Web app editor on UBOS offers a sandbox to prototype similar interfaces quickly.
Why This Hire Is a Game‑Changer
Front‑end engineering is often the public face of AI products. By securing a founding engineer, 100x.bot ensures that its AI agents are not only powerful under the hood but also intuitive on the surface. This move signals several broader trends:
AI‑first product design : Companies are embedding AI directly into UI layers, demanding engineers who can bridge LLM logic with responsive front‑end performance.
Rapid scaling via low‑code platforms : Leveraging the UBOS platform overview , startups can accelerate feature delivery while maintaining code quality.
Equity‑driven talent acquisition : The YC‑standard equity package aligns engineers’ incentives with long‑term company success, attracting top talent in competitive markets like Bangalore.
From a community perspective, the role also highlights the growing demand for front‑end developers who understand AI workflows . As more startups adopt generative AI, the skill set described above will become a benchmark for hiring across the sector.
