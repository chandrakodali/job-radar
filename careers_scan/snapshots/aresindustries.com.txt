INTRODUCING MAV &
goose
Maverick and Goose are our vision for mass-fire munitions: affordable, mass-manufacturable weapons built to be fielded en masse.
Swarms of Goose can overwhelm defenses and enable maritime area denial, each carrying a ~20 lb payload out to 100 miles. Packs of Maverick extend the fight much deeper, each delivering a ~100 lb payload at ranges up to 1,000 miles.
Both launch from CONEX containers, plugging into existing logistics infrastructure to integrate seamlessly with ground and naval forces. They fly at mid-subsonic speeds and provide strike capability without reliance on costly air platforms.
Built in the hundreds of thousands at five-figure prices, Maverick and Goose define a new class of munition: precision missile capabilities at drone costs.

WE FACE
mass manufacturing
By combining commercially available components with vertical integration of critical subsystems, we control both cost and supply. Simplified tooling and minimized part count enable automotive-level production, reaching hundreds of thousands per year while driving down costs by orders of magnitude.
Containerized Launch
Containerized launch keeps our systems hidden until the moment of fire, with up to 64 Goose vertically launching from a single 20-foot CONEX. Rapidly mobile and forward-deployable, these launchers turn ordinary logistics assets into high-capacity strike batteries.
PACK DYNAMICS
Swarms of smaller, easily producible missiles can accomplish missions that today rely on exquisite, high-cost systems. Our platforms are meant to be launched in masses, with only some providing advanced guidance and others carrying payloads, networked together to overwhelm defenses.
EXPORT-FRIENdLY
Our systems are export-ready from day one, built with modular designs and adaptable architectures. Allies can integrate their own components and leverage local supply chains, enabling faster adoption and scalable production abroad.
