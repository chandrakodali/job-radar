Frequently asked questions
What is Numero?
Numero is an all-in-one donor database and call time app for political campaigns and fundraising teams. It combines your donor lists, call time, texting, and payment tracking in one place, and surfaces each donor's giving history when you're making calls.
How is Numero different from NGP VAN?
Numero is built for the person actually raising the money, not just for compliance staff. It automatically dedupes your list, shows giving history at call time, and includes texting and payment follow-up without the manual exports and VLOOKUPs NGP requires. Many teams switch from NGP VAN to Numero.
Do I have to change how my team makes calls?
No. Numero works the way you already run call time — you open the app, see your call list, and make your calls. The difference is that each donor's giving history is right in front of you.
Does Numero dedupe my donor list?
Yes. When you import from NGP, VAN, ActBlue, or a spreadsheet, Numero cleans and dedupes automatically. Teams often find their real contact count is smaller than they thought because the duplicates are gone.
Does Numero handle texting?
Yes. You can send one-to-one texts with a pre-filled Speedy Give link, as well as text and email broadcasts, from inside the app, so you're not texting donors from your personal phone.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. Numero is month-to-month with 30-day notice, prorated, and no upfront charge.
How much do I need to raise for Numero to be worth it?
Numero typically pays for itself for campaigns raising roughly $30,000 to $40,000 a year and up. Book a demo and the team will walk through the math for your race.
Are there setup fees?
No. Numero has no setup fees. There's also no upfront charge or long-term contract required to get started.
Is Numero FEC-compliant? How does it handle compliance reporting?
Yes. All Numero contribution forms are FEC-compliant, capturing the donor information and disclosures required for federal filings automatically as contributions come in.
Is my donor data secure? Where is it hosted? Who has access?
Numero is hosted on secure, U.S.-based cloud infrastructure, designed to meet the expectations of enterprise political organizations and compliance teams. All sensitive data is protected using modern TLS in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest — the same standard trusted by banks and government agencies for top-secret information.
How does Numero compare to Pingdex?
Pingdex is an add-on tool that sits on top of NGP VAN and costs $1,000 plus your NGP VAN bill — so you're paying for two products. Numero is a complete donor database, call time, texting, and payments platform for a flat $1,080/month with no separate NGP VAN cost underneath it.
How does Numero compare to CallTime.ai?
CallTime.ai focuses on call time specifically and costs $1,100 to $2,200/month depending on your list size — and its engineering team is outsourced overseas. Numero includes call time plus your full donor database, texting, and payment/pledge tracking for a flat $1,080/month at every tier, built entirely by a U.S.-based team.
Does Numero work with ActBlue?
Yes. Numero imports and dedupes donor lists from ActBlue automatically, and you can use ActBlue for payment processing alongside Numero's donor database, call time, and texting tools.
Does Numero work for state/local races, not just federal?
Yes. Numero supports campaigns at every level — federal, state, and local. Numero's winner carousel features state legislature, city council, and mayoral wins alongside federal races.
What's the support response time? How do I get help?
Numero's support team responds in 60 minutes or less. You can reach us directly from inside the platform or by emailing [email protected] .
Who is Numero built for?
Numero is built for political campaigns, PACs, party committees, finance directors, and fundraising staff across federal, state, and local races.
Does Numero support multiple staff?
Yes. Numero includes unlimited users at every pricing tier — no per-seat charges.
Can I import my existing donor list? What formats?
Yes. Numero imports donor data from NGP, VAN, ActBlue, and spreadsheets.
