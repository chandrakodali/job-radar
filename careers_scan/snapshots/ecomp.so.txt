We believe understanding how capabilities form during pretraining and building principled reinforcement learning environments are the path to solving science with AI.
Our beliefs
We need to understand when capabilities form during pretraining and how data affects them. Understanding this will lead to sample-efficient capability learning.
Reinforcement learning is the clear pathway to superintelligence. It is essential to find scalable ways to make high-quality RL environments that models believe are real and that have non-gameable rewards.
Products / active research
Causal biology RL environments where models predict genetic and drug effects graded on the viable grounding of their conclusions
Formally verifying security exploits: attempting to create formally verifiable grounded exploit systems to help models prove vulnerabilities in software
Computed-state use: do models know things they fail to use?
Timing windows: Does when a model learns something determine whether it can retain it?
