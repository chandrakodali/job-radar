A few fair
questions.
What happens if our current page wins?
It stays live. If our version hasn’t beaten the agreed baseline for revenue per visitor by day 45, we keep working at no charge until it does.
We’ve tried CRO before. What changes this time?
We start with the ad that brought the visitor in. Then we test the positioning, copy, design and build of the page against a baseline you approved. You can see what changed and how it performed.
We just redesigned the store. Can we still do this?
Yes. Your existing page stays in place as the control. We build a separate page for paid traffic and test the two side by side.
Who will actually do the work?
Paul leads a small senior team and works on every account. The same team handles strategy, copy, design, Shopify development and analysis.
What access do you need?
Read access to your store, analytics and ad account for the first week. We need Shopify collaborator access to build and launch tests. You approve each launch and can remove access at any time.
What happens after the first test?
We use the result to update a ranked roadmap. The next test could be another landing page, your cart, a best-selling product page or an offer and bundle.
