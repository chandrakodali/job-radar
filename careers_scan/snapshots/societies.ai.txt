Co-founder and CEO
James He
James He is co-founder and CEO of Artificial Societies. At Cambridge, he researched human behaviour using data science and agent-based simulation. In 2023, James led the first peer-reviewed paper on large agent swarms that studied a society of 33,000 agents. He now guides the company’s overall deployment strategy and foundational research.

Our mission
Artificial Societies is building foundation models of human societies to inform decisions that shape the world.
We’re an applied computational social science company that exists to unfold humanness, and help scale humanity’s ability to collaborate and innovate effectively.
As a team, we’re proud of being great partners, great craftspeople, and great scientists. We partner side by side with our clients to apply this impactful technology, craft every moment of interaction, and bring scientific rigour and openness to our models and to every conversation.
Humanity has spent the last ten millennia scaling the ability to collaborate and innovate, but now, our technologies are accelerating faster than our institutions, psychology, or understanding of ourselves can keep up.
This is why we care deeply about pioneering socially intelligent technology, to bridge social science and machine intelligence.
Impact guides our work. We help enterprises and sovereigns test decisions before they’re made, so humanity can move forward with clarity and conviction. We conduct frontier research on building socially-aligned artificial collective intelligence, so that humans and future technologies can collaborate and innovate better, together.
We are Artificial Societies. Thank you for being part of our journey.
