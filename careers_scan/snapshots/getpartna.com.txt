How we work
Six things we hold each other to.
Sweat the details
We take pride in our work, and it shows in the small things: an edge case handled, a clear error message, a reconciled transaction.
Fast, but never sloppy
Speed matters, because our customers move money and can't wait on us. Quality still comes first: we'd rather ship a day later than ship something we're not proud of.
Customers shape the roadmap
We spend time with the people who use Partna, and what we hear decides what we build next.
Care like it's yours
We want people who are obsessed with what they're building, who notice when something is off and want to fix it.
Say it clearly
We're spread across time zones, so we write precisely. A clear message today saves a day of back-and-forth.
Be kind
We're good to each other, we enjoy working together, and we like spending time together outside work too.
