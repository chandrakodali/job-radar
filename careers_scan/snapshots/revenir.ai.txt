🌍 Hybrid Flexibility
We expect the team to be in the office on a Monday, Tuesday and Thursday as we believe this is key to creating the best company culture and key to our work.

Flexible working hours- We trust you to work enough hours to do your job well and get the work done. We do not clock watch at Revenir. However, we do expect you to be online during core working hours.

25 days’ a year, plus bank holidays. You can also carry over a max of 5 days. The holiday year runs from 1st January to 31st December.

A culture that respects work-life balance, access to cycle to work scheme and paid sick leave.
🍕 Team Lunches & Socials
We always celebrate personal and work milestones with team lunches and also have regular pub sessions — we work hard but have fun doing it.
🍼 Parental Leave
Inclusive and flexible parental leave for all new parents — no awkward conversations required. This includes Antenatal and Adoption Leave.
💻 The Right Tools
MacBook, and any tech equipment you need to do your best work from day one- within reason!

We’ll automatically enrol you into the nest pension scheme. We contribute the equivalent of 3% of your base salary. You’ll need to contribute 5% too. You can opt out if you’d prefer not to have a pension at all.
