ABOUT VERVERICA
Ververica is the original creator of Apache Flink® and remains a core contributor to it. Flink is the leading open-source engine for stream and batch processing. It runs mission-critical workloads at Netflix, Alibaba, Amazon and Uber, as well as at banks, telcos and global enterprises.
We build Ververica Unified Streaming Data Platform, an enterprise-grade platform for real-time data on top of Flink. Backed by one of the world's largest tech companies, we combine open-source innovation with long-term stability.
WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS
Our customers run real-time pipelines that cannot stop: fraud detection, payments, telemetry, recommendations. When something breaks, you are the engineer who gets it running again.
As a Technical Support Engineer , you will solve real production problems on Kubernetes, in the cloud and inside Flink itself. You will work side by side with the engineers who build Flink, so every hard ticket is a chance to learn how a distributed system works under the hood.
This role suits someone who enjoys a good puzzle, likes helping people and wants to grow into a stream processing expert.
WHAT YOU WILL DO
Be the first technical point of contact for customers using Ververica Unified Streaming Data Platform.
Diagnose and fix issues across the stack, from Kubernetes and cloud infrastructure to Flink jobs, connectors and SQL.
Reproduce tricky problems, dig through logs and metrics, and find the root cause.
Escalate to Engineering and Product with clear, well-documented cases, and help shape fixes and product improvements.
Explain solutions and best practices clearly to engineers and non-technical stakeholders alike.
Keep accurate records of cases and resolutions in our ticketing system.
Turn what you learn into knowledge base articles, tutorials and runbooks that help customers and teammates.
Keep sharpening your skills in cloud technologies and stream processing, and share them with the team.
WHAT YOU BRING
2+ years in technical support or a similar hands-on role, ideally with cloud-based products.
Solid knowledge of at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure or Google Cloud).
Practical experience with containers and Kubernetes. Our product runs on Kubernetes, so this matters.
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. You enjoy finding out why something fails.
Clear written and spoken English, and the patience to explain technical ideas to any audience.
A customer-first mindset and the ability to stay calm when a customer is under pressure.
Ability to work independently and as part of a distributed team.
Curiosity and a drive to keep learning.
A degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
NICE TO HAVE
No one ticks every box. If you have some of these, tell us.
Experience with stream processing, Apache Flink or Apache Kafka.
Scripting or programming skills in Shell, Python, Java or SQL.
Experience with Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code tools.
Experience with cloud monitoring and observability tools.
Cloud certifications such as AWS Certified Solutions Architect or Azure Administrator.
LOCATION AND WORKING HOURS
This is a remote role for someone based between UTC-4 and UTC-7. That covers US and Canadian Eastern, Central, Mountain and Pacific time, plus Latin American countries in the same range.
Your core hours follow customers' business day in the Americas.
You will work closely with our EMEA support team to give customers follow-the-sun coverage.
WHAT WE OFFER
Competitive compensation: salary, equity and performance bonus
Remote-first culture with flexible hours
Direct access to Flink core developers and a global open-source community
A clear path to grow into senior support, Technical Account Manager or engineering roles
Generous vacation, personal leave and wellness benefits

Senior Product Designer (m/f/d) - Spain | Remote
About us
Ververica builds the real-time data streaming platform from the original creators of Apache Flink. Our customers run mission-critical pipelines on it, so our product is dense, technical and unforgiving of bad UX.
We are AI-first in how we build: our engineers work with coding agents daily, our design system ships its own MCP server, and our guidelines are written to be read by humans and machines alike. We want a designer who works the same way.
The role
We are hiring a Senior Product Designer who designs AI-first products and ships design specs as working code. You own the experience of our data console, for processing and storing data and our customer-facing AI assistant, and you co-own the Stream Design System (SDS) with our frontend engineers.
Your primary deliverable is not a static Figma file. It is a spec in Storybook, built with AI coding tools on top of SDS, that engineers can pick up and ship.
Location : Remote
Reports to : Director of AI Engineering and Design
Start : as early as possible in Q1 2027
What you'll do
Design AI-first experiences
Design agentic and conversational flows where AI acts on the platform: creating deployments, diagnosing failing jobs, searching docs, opening support cases.
Define patterns for AI in a technical product: intent capture, plan previews, human-in-the-loop approval, streaming output, confidence and provenance, undo and recovery from wrong actions.
Prototype with real models early, test with users, and iterate on prompts and system behaviour together with engineers.
Ship specs as code
Turn flows into interactive Storybook stories built on SDS components, using AI coding tools with the SDS MCP server.
Open pull requests, take review from engineers, and keep stories in sync with what ships.
Use Figma for exploration and Code Connect mappings, not as the final source of truth.
Create the content of the SDS guidelines to teach the AI clients how to use it and how to design autonomously.
Own product areas end to end
Catalog and table management, authentication, access control, dashboards and server status.
Complex operational flows such as blue/green deployments, deployment defaults and Flink state introspection.
Partner with backend engineers on VIDs (our design proposals) so UX shapes the API, not the other way around.
Shape requirements with product management and engineering, from discovery to release.
Run lean user research and usability tests with customers, Customer Success and Sales Engineering, and turn findings into design decisions.
Co-own the Stream Design System
Grow SDS: new components (skeleton, segmented control, timeline, charts, breadcrumb), tokens (motion, opacity, content width, control height) and a light theme.
Keep Figma, code and Code Connect aligned, and review component implementations for visual and accessibility quality.
Write and maintain the SDS guidelines that both people and coding agents consume, and close the gaps that agent evaluation runs surface.
What you bring
5+ years of product design on complex B2B, developer or data products, with shipped work you can walk us through.
Hands-on experience designing AI-powered features: copilots, agents or chat interfaces in production, not only concepts.
A working understanding of how LLMs behave: context, tool calling, latency, hallucination, failure modes, and how each shows up in the UI.
You build in code daily with AI assistance. You can set up a Storybook, write stories in HTML/CSS and TypeScript, and use Git and pull requests without hand-holding.
Deep design system experience: tokens, component APIs, variants, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and Figma-to-code mapping.
Strong interaction design for data-dense interfaces: tables, forms, states, errors and long-running operations.
Experience running user research and usability testing, and turning insights into product decisions.
Strong communication with technical and non-technical stakeholders, and comfort in an agile team that iterates fast on feedback.
Clear written English. You write specs, guidelines and rationale that engineers and agents can act on.
