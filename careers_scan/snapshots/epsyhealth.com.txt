Changing epilepsy, one mind at a time.
Founded in 2020, Epsy has already impacted tens of thousands of lives.
Doctors are recommending us during appointments, patients are hailing the app as “life-saving”, and our team is growing to support this demand.
We're on a mission: fast track anyone with epilepsy to their best treatment path. And we are always looking for thinkers and creators to join us on our journey... here's a glimpse of life at Epsy:
All-team collaboration
We pride ourselves on teamwork, as we actively strive for excellence and work together to better the lives of millions. We're bold and optimistic about the future, and our high-energy culture spills into everything we do, and all things around us. It brightens every interaction we have with our users, teammates, and partners — and moves us all forward, together.
Flexible working, solid backing
We're officially based in Paddington, London (pics of our office coming soon), yet we fully embrace remote working. Our team is currently spread over London / Berlin / Krakow / Seoul / Houston! We are a startup, but operate under the umbrella of a multi-billion dollar corporate: LivaNova. At Epsy, we get the best of both worlds: high growth, combined with the safety of our corporate backing - a rare but powerful combination for a start-up.
Plenty of benefits
Users come first in our product, and people come first in our team. Epsy is proud to offer excellent private medical insurance, generous retirement options, mind-easing life assurance and an employee purchase share program. And of course there's always time for fun; pizza-making, pub quizzes and park meet-ups are among the activities we like to do together.
Our story
Epsy was founded in 2020, with all members of our team starting fully remotely. Today we have embraced remote working for the long-term and we keep growing as a company and as a team.
We operate as an independent start-up within the global medical technology company LivaNova. We move fast and drive growth, while we also benefit from the corporate umbrella for excellent funding and resources.
