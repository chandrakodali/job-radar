Benefits
At WyeWorks, we believe that when people feel supported, they do their best work. We’ve designed our benefits to nurture your wellbeing, growth, and joy — inside and outside of work.
Lunches
We have two providers who make sure you receive the lunch of your choice every day.
Remote Work
We prioritize in-person work for more genuine, ongoing connection — but you can also work from home some days for a better work-life balance.
Flexible Hours
Manage your schedule to fit your needs, and take time during the workday for personal errands — as long as it doesn’t affect the team.
Snacks
Enjoy breakfast, afternoon snacks, and breaks at the office without needing to step out.
Team Bonding
Once a month, the bonding team organizes an outing or get-together — fully covered by WyeWorks.
English Classes
Keep improving your skills and prepare for any exams you’re interested in.
CUTI Partnership
Access exclusive benefits through our partnership with CUTI (cuti.org.uy/beneficios).
Study Days
For full-time employees, WyeWorks grants 4 extra days — giving you 12 days of study leave.
