What's Sharetribe
Sharetribe makes the best marketplace software solution in the world. Our mission is to democratize platform ownership by making it possible for anyone to build a successful marketplace business.
As a part of our team, you’ll have a real impact. Marketplaces powered by our software are used by millions of people in more than 100 countries. The majority of our 1,000+ customers are solo founders or small teams building platforms that make better use of existing resources, like reselling, rentals, and services. Our steward-owned company structure ensures that Sharetribe will always remain fully controlled by its working team. We're fully funded by the revenue from our customers and plan to keep it that way.
We value integrity, candor, kindness, purpose before profit, and balance. At Sharetribe, you won’t have to sacrifice your well-being to make a real difference in the world; you can have both.

Perks that matter
How we help our team thrive on and off the clock.
We determine our salaries through an open process that considers the market rate for corresponding roles in Finland, your individual contribution, and our strategy to reduce income inequality. All salaries are adjusted annually, and everyone at Sharetribe knows each other’s salary.
According to the Finnish work legislation, every full-time team member gets five weeks of paid time off after the first year of employment. As a starting bonus, you get 10 additional paid holiday days on top of the holiday you accumulate.
We extend the paid parental leave dictated by the collective bargaining agreement for the Finnish IT sector by 10 days for both parents. This means that currently, the total paid parental leave at Sharetribe is 80 days for the birthing parent and 40 days for the non-birthing parent.
You have full flexibility to decide if you work remotely or from our office. We have a really nice office in downtown Helsinki and we often organise activities like company-paid team lunches and gatherings there, but you're free to be remote whenever you prefer that.
Some highlights from our comprehensive health care package include short-term psychotherapy, gynecologist appointments without referral, and an annual dental health care budget of 250 euros. (If you want to know everything that’s included, ours is the Terveystalo Tuottava package .)
Set up your ideal workstation at the Sharetribe office or at home with your chosen gear: desk, chair, laptop, screen, and accessories. In addition, each teammate has a personal device budget which can be used for additional gear, such as a phone, tablet, additional screen, ergonomics improvements, etc.
Every teammate has a learning budget that can be used for courses, learning material, subscriptions, or allotting work hours for learning.
We get together every month for dinner and something fun: watch movies, make glass beads, arrange flowers, try indoor surfing… your ideas on team activities are welcome! (Joining is totally optional, so no pressure if that’s not your thing.) Twice per year, the entire team goes on a three-day cottage trip to talk strategy, do workshops, and enjoy each other’s company.
Convert a part of your salary to a lunch benefit, transportation benefit, bicycle benefit, housing benefit, and/or parking benefit.
Our office is located centrally in downtown Helsinki, and you can use the space off-hours to host free-time activities. Enjoy quality coffee, fresh fruit, and the occasional home-baked cake while at the office. Every other Friday is a company-paid team lunch day and a good occasion to meet several of your colleagues at the office.

Careers FAQ
At the moment, we don’t accept open applications for data protection reasons. If you’d like to stay updated on future openings, please follow us on LinkedIn or sign up for our career mailing list (no spam, we promise!).
Each opening is unique and has specific requirements outlined in the job ad. However, we often look for individual contributors with strong self-management skills. Given the nature of our customer base, fluency in English—both written and spoken—is also typically required. We work asynchronously, so great writing skills are helpful for every role.
Our hiring process varies depending on the role and is determined by the corresponding hiring team. For specific details, please refer to the job ad—you’ll find the process described at the bottom of the page.
We currently hire quite rarely—usually only once or twice a year. If you’d like to stay updated on open positions, follow us on LinkedIn or sign up for our career mailing list (no spam, we promise!).
We’re a small team and often receive a large number of applications. To ensure every application gets the attention it deserves, we review each one manually. You can expect to hear from us within about 2–3 weeks—we always get back to everyone who applied.
If you have any questions about your application, feel free to email our PeopleOps Manager, Kinga, at kinga@sharetribe.com.
