The Ideal Candidate
Who we’re looking for
We’re looking for kind and ambitious people who want to do the best work of their career. The work we do across hardware, software, and operations is hard and long-term oriented. We’re looking for colleagues who:
View fast-paced and changing environments as an opportunity -not a bug - and find the agency to move things forward.
Have a proclivity to not just learn, but also teach—those with an innate drive to help others and enable them to succeed.
Show deep thoughtfulness in their writing and thinking.
Always assume good faith and communicate with a deep sense of empathy.
