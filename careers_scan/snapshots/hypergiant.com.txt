Company Information
Why Join Hypergiant?
At Hypergiant, we are redefining what’s possible. Our team is made up of a diverse group of individuals who are implementing solutions to build a better world.
Hypergiant creates AI-enabled decision-making software that streamlines and accelerates the user journey from data to decision. In addition to our work with space, defense and critical infrastructure, we also partner with well known consumer facing tech companies to bring the best and brightest talent to their workforce.
Working at Hypergiant means you collaborate with passionate and focused teammates, on technology that impacts & changes the world.

Benefits
Employee Benefits
Medical, Dental & Vision Benefits
We provide top-notch medical, dental, and vision coverage, where we cover 100% of premiums for individual employees. We contribute a fixed amount per pay period for your dependents and offer flexible HSA and FSA plans tailored to your needs
Life Insurance
Life is unpredictable, but your future doesn't have to be uncertain. We offer a complimentary $50,000 life insurance policy with the flexibility to enhance your coverage at a reduced rate, ensuring that you and your loved ones have the peace of mind and financial security you deserve.
Short Term & Long Term Disability Insurance
We've got you covered with 100% coverage for both short-term and long-term disability insurance, ensuring you have the financial protection you need.
Personal & Professional Development
Unleash your potential using our $1200 per year Personal & Professional Development fund, designed to empower you with resources that enhance your mental, physical, and professional well-being.
Remote-Friendly Environment
Experience the freedom to work your way. Whether you prefer collaborating in our vibrant Austin office or embracing the comfort of your home anywhere in the US, our flexible work environment empowers you to thrive wherever you are.
Unlimited Paid Time Off & Holidays
We encourage a healthy work-life balance with unlimited paid time off, coupled with 14 paid company holidays, giving you the freedom to recharge and celebrate important moments.
Maternity/Paternity Leave
We proudly provide 8 weeks of paid maternity/paternity leave, ensuring you have the support you need during this special time in your life.
Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
Our Employee Assistance Program offers confidential counseling and support services, reinforcing your well-being and resilience both at work and in your personal life.
401k and Financial Advisory Services
At Hypergiant, we help you unlock your full financial potential. We offer access to a robust 401k plan with contribution matching up to 3%, and a complimentary financial advisor for all of our team members.
Employee Recognition
We recognize and reward excellence on our team through quarterly employee awards, birthday treats, free swag, and exciting giveaways. At Hypergiant, we celebrate you!
Monthly Lunch & Learns
Hypergiant's monthly Lunch & Learns offer a dynamic opportunity to expand your knowledge while enjoying complimentary lunches, creating a perfect blend of growth and gastronomy.
Remote Office Setup
We want you to feel comfortable and productive from day one. All new hires receive a $550 stipend to create a home office setup that works for them.
