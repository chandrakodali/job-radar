How We Work Together
A few principles that shape our culture, plus the benefits that keep the team focused and healthy.
Clinicians first
Every decision starts with the people delivering care. If it doesn't make their working life better, we don't ship it.
Move with urgency
Hospitals can't wait weeks for a fix. We ship small, ship often, and measure what actually changed.
Radical transparency
Pay, shift details, and internal roadmaps — we default to open. No hidden terms, inside or out.
Small teams, big scope
You'll own an area end to end and work directly with the people using it, not through five layers of process.
