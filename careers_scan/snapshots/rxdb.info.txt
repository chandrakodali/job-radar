Careers at RxDB
RxDB is a database that runs in the browser and on the phone. Apps built with it keep working without a network connection and sync once the connection is back. RxDB is an open source project used by developers all over the world, and we are looking for people who want to build things, not just collect credit points. The working student positions are in Stuttgart, Germany 🇩🇪. The sales position is fully remote.
