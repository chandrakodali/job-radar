A Note from Our Founders
Dear Tradex Community,
We’ve discontinued Tradex, our prediction market platform. This was a difficult decision. Given the changes to India’s online gaming rules, we decided to wind down the service.
To everyone who made a prediction, took part in our markets, or believed in what we were building, thank you. You were the reason we showed up every day.
What Comes Next
B2B Game Development
We’re building a free-to-play prediction market game that any developer can integrate into their own game. We’re also bringing our experience building interactive products to studios and publishers around the world.
AI Avatars and Video Production
We’re launching a production house focused on creating AI avatars and producing videos.
We’ll share more as these projects take shape. Thank you for being part of the Tradex story.
