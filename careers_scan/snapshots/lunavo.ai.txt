04 · The workforce
Meet the agents running your back‑office.
Each agent owns one job end‑to‑end. They share context, escalate cleanly, and
don't take PTO. Turn them on one at a time, or light them all up.
DISPATCH
Dispatches by margin, not by gut.
For every load, Lunavo compares running it on your own fleet, buying freight, and re-selling, calculates the contribution margin in real time, and recommends the best option. You decide with one click instead of five load boards open in parallel.
TMS · LOAD BOARDS · FLEET · CONTRACTS
ORDER INTAKE
Automated order intake.
Lunavo reads every request from email, PDF, Excel, extracts the data, applies your customer-specific rules, and creates the load in your TMS. Your first reply ships in seconds, not hours.
TMS · EMAIL · EDI · PDF
QUOTING
Quotes every request the moment it lands.
Lunavo pulls market rates from load boards, your historical lanes and your customer contracts, calculates the optimal margin, and drafts the quote. You approve. The customer gets an answer in minutes.
