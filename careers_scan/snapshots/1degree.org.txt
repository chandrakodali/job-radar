We build tech for good at One Degree
One Degree brings life-changing social services within reach for millions of low-income people seeking help every day. Through easy-to-use technology and deep community partnership, we remove the formidable barriers that prevent people from getting everything from housing to healthcare and beyond. We are a low-ego, humble group of people who love what we do and love bringing out the best in each other.
You’ll be a great fit for this team if you…
Enjoy fostering engaged collaboration
Believe it’s time to reimagine how technology can bring agency to empower communities
Connect families and individuals to life-changing opportunities
One Degree is committed to diversity
We want to especially encourage those from underrepresented communities to apply to any open positions listed. Our goal is to build a diverse team of talented people from all walks of life, and we want each employee who come aboard to love what they do, feel valued and grow with the team, and do their best work.
