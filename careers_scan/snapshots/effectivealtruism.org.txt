What makes a career high-impact?
A high-impact career involves using your job or income to help solve important problems where more people, funding, or research could make a significant difference. When thinking about where to focus your career, these three factors can help you find where you can do the most good:
Importance
How big and how severe is the problem? How much does the problem affect people, animals, and future generations?
Neglectedness
How many people are already working on the problem? Contributing to an overlooked problem often has more impact than working on an issue that already receives a lot of support.
Tractability
How likely is it that we can solve the problem? Is there a realistic path to progress through research, funding, advocacy, or other interventions?

High-impact career paths
You may not need to start from scratch to move into high-impact work. Many organizations working on important problems need professional skills, from software, policy, and research to operations, communications, fundraising, and product
Whatever your background, there are many ways to use your career to improve the world.
Direct
Direct work
Join an organization where you can use your current skillset to work directly on solving important problems.
Earn to give
Pursue a job that allows you to earn a high income so that you can donate to the most effective charities.
Research
Investigate some of the world’s most pressing problems to help identify better solutions.
Organization building
Start or help build an initiative to solve a neglected problem. High-impact organizations need founders, as well as people who can manage finances, hiring, marketing, and day-to-day operations.
Policy and advocacy
Connect evidence-based recommendations to decision-makers, and help shape policy that benefits the world both now and in the future.
Communications and fundraising
Help people understand complex problems and act to solve them. Support promising solutions through writing, campaigns, partnerships, and/or fundraising.
