How We Work
Quality Over Quantity
We believe database companies win on engineering excellence. Correctness, performance, and reliability matter. We invest heavily in testing and focus on building systems we're proud to have our names attached to.
Everyone Talks to Customers
Startups fail when they don't build something people want. We believe the best way to avoid this is to ensure engineers regularly engage with customers, tighten feedback loops into the development cycle, and help shape both product and technical direction.
Intellectual Honesty
We believe humility and curiosity are prerequisites for great engineering. We care more about finding the right answer than being right. Strong opinions are welcome, but they should be backed by evidence, curiosity, and a willingness to change your mind.
High Trust
We're a small team built on trust and initiative. We keep processes to a minimum, invest in people, and give engineers ownership over their work.
Open Source First
The world is built on open-source software, and we're no exception. We contribute upstream, engage directly with our community, and strive to be good stewards of the projects we depend on.
