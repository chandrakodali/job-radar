Hello Incoming Sheyk!
Here is our guide to joining one of the fastest-growing teams ever!
Our culture reflects who we are and how we interact with each other, as well as with everyone else outside the team. At Shekel Mobility, we identify as the “Sheyks,” a cool name if we do say so ourselves.
Sheyk is coined from the word, “Sheikh”, which means an Arab Leader, particularly the chief or head of an Arab tribe, family or village.
At Shekel Mobility, We identify as the “Sheyks” As a result, Sheyks are characterized by our exemplary leadership qualities, both as individuals and in the industry.
We believe in everyone on the team and trust each other to spread the word about Shekel Mobility wherever we find ourselves.
How many puns can you come up with using the word, “Sheyk?” Here is one. We hope that if you do join us, you get to really sheyk things up around here!
