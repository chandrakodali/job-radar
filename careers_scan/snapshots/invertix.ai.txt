01 What we build
The work.
Our agents triage alarms across monitoring portals, bring in the right technician and check the fix, inside the systems energy companies already use.
We are an early-stage startup: real ownership from day one, room to explore, and a low-ego team that holds a high bar and ships fast. Our engineers deploy the agents with the operations teams of the energy companies we work with.
Demo fleet. Figures are illustrative. See the product →
In detail
How we work. Our forward-deployed engineers work inside a customer’s operations team: they map the job, connect the systems and build the agents with that team, then run and improve them alongside it.
Who we work with. Altenia, Terna and TotalEnergies On. The energy companies we work with manage 50+ GW.

AI/LLM Engineer
Munich Full-time / Intern
Apply , AI/LLM Engineer (opens in a new tab)
Own the agent stack: tool design, routing, evals, prompt and context engineering, and multi-agent coordination.
In detail : AI/LLM Engineer
Your responsibilities
The stack grows as we add more specialised agents, and you own how they are built, evaluated and coordinated. You’ll work with years of noisy time-series telemetry, technical manuals that need modern OCR to parse, and portfolios that are each wired differently. The role rewards engineers who build systems that actually run in production, not demos.
Your background
3+ years of software engineering experience, with at least 1 year building and shipping LLM-powered systems in production.
Strong Python. Comfortable with async, FastAPI-style backends, and PostgreSQL.
Hands-on experience with agent orchestration concepts: tool design, routing, context management, and the middleware that keeps long-running agents stable (prompt caching, summarisation, checkpointing).
Familiarity with the mechanics of RAG: chunking strategies, embeddings, retrieval quality, and the evals that tell you whether any of it works.
Even better
Experience with industrial, sensor, or time-series data.
Background with eval frameworks for non-deterministic systems.
Have shipped multi-agent or multi-provider architectures.
Open-source contributions to agent or RAG tooling.
