Deliver with Bentocart, Earn $22 - $26 per hour plus bonus and tips
Work 2 hours a day, Make $1100+ a month
Do you have a car and like authentic ethnic food? Bentocart (www.bentocart.com) is a new and upcoming lunch
concept that brings authentic ethnic food from the Inner Richmond/Sunset area to Downtown San Francisco.
This is a great part-time opportunity for someone who is looking for supplemental income.
Interested? Keep reading for more details.
Why drive with Bentocart?
Short and stable hours (10:40am-12:40pm, Monday-Friday) so you have your afternoons/evenings free
Guranteed hourly rate start from $22 per hour, increase $1 every month, up to $26
$1100/month if you work 5 days per week at $26/hr
Bonus for bad weather days and busy days
Low gas costs and car wear/tear . Only ~ 8 miles of driving per shift
No need to look for parking in downtown . Customers come to you!
All weekday shifts, no need to work on weekend
What do you need?
MUST live in San Francisco!
Be reliable, prompt, and personable . If you are often late to things, this isn't the gig for you
Have a 4 door car . SUV/van preferred. No pets and smokers (we are transporting food)!
Comfortable navigating around downtown SF during the lunch rush (parallel parking skills are important)
Comfortable working with a smartphone and safely driving
Current vehicle registration and insurance
3+ years driving experience and clean DMV record
Can legally work in the US
21+ years old
Current ride-share or food delivery driver preferred
We are a small team with big dreams! We'd really like to find someone who is friendly, has a good vibe, and provides exceptional customer service. We are seeking someone who enjoys meeting our customers and chatting with them (they are all well-established and working at good companies). If you fulfill these requirements, we'd love to hear from you!
