Ledger at a glance
Ledger is a specialized broking and advisory firm exclusively focused on casualty ILS. We come from diverse backgrounds in (re)insurance, investment banking, capital markets, and actuarial science. We are driven by our shared goal of excellence and efficiency in all that we do.
We are a market leader, securitizing over $2.5B of casualty premium and pioneering the secondary market for casualty ILS. It is an exciting time at Ledger.
Benefits
Fully remote team
We believe you should work wherever you're happiest and most productive. Whether that's at home or in a co-working space, we give you the flexibility to design your ideal work environment. Our remote culture is built on trust, clear communication, and collaboration across time zones. If you enjoy working from an office, our NYC office provides common space to gather and also options for employees in the metro NYC area to work from the office as needed.
Flexible time off
We trust you to know when you need a break. Our flexible paid time off policy empowers you to take the time you need—whether it's for a vacation, a personal day, or simply to recharge. We believe rest is essential to doing great work, and we encourage you to step away regularly so you can come back refreshed, focused, and inspired.
Company retreats
Even though we’re a remote team, we believe in the power of face-to-face connections. That’s why we organize company retreats where we come together to celebrate our wins, strengthen our relationships, and have fun. These gatherings give us the chance to collaborate in person, spark new ideas, and build a strong, connected team culture. Whether it’s a scenic aquatic getaway, an exciting city adventure, or a relaxing retreat, our goal is to create memorable experiences that bring us closer as a team.
Personal development
We believe that learning never stops. That’s why we encourage and support your continuous growth through professional development opportunities and access to courses, books, and conferences. Whether you're looking to deepen your expertise, explore new skills, or take on new challenges, we provide the resources to help you thrive. Your growth is our growth, and we’re here to support you every step of the way.
