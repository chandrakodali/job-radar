Job Openings Dataset
Information about job openings sourced directly from company websites for highest accuracy and freshness. Access historical data since 2016 with over 300 million records with full global coverage.
Each job opening is refreshed once every 36 hours for highest accuracy. The dataset includes an average of 9.8 million active jobs at any given time, with historical data dating back to 2016. All job openings are categorized using industry-standard O*NET codes for accurate classification.
The dataset includes detailed information such as job title, URL, location, category, seniority level, full job description, salary information, contract type, O*NET job category codes and more. Each record tracks when a job was first seen and last seen, providing valuable insights into hiring trends and patterns.
Job openings data is continuously updated and verified, ensuring you have access to the most current and accurate information. With coverage of over 3 million companies, you can track hiring activity across industries, locations, and job categories to identify growth signals and market trends.
Whether you're analyzing hiring patterns, identifying expansion opportunities, or tracking competitive intelligence, our job openings dataset provides the reliable, detailed data you need to make informed decisions.
