Our values
Start with the customer
Whether internal or external, customers are our top priority. Every decision, no matter how big or small, starts with them in mind.
Bring our 'A' game
Excellence is a mindset we all champion. We’re driven and motivated. We experiment and learn. We celebrate not only what we do, but the impact we have.
Solve problems, seize opportunities
We roll up our sleeves and take ownership of issues both within and beyond our roles, and we embrace opportunities for personal growth.
Enable everyone to thrive
Our team’s diverse ideas, strengths, interests, and backgrounds help us succeed. We value each other’s opinions and differences.
Empower others through collaboration
We are one team, rowing in one direction. We work together. We’re helpful, supportive and positive. We are all about enabling each other’s success.
Breed trust through transparency
Transparency is our default. Knowing that trust builds a better business, we’re honest and candid about our successes and failures.
Benefits of working at Snowplow
Equity for everyone
Stock options, available to all employees globally. You're not just working here — you're building something you own.
Great work deserves great pay
We set salaries to attract exceptional people — and keep them.
Time off that works for you
Generous holiday allowance and enhanced parental leave for all parents, wherever you are in the world.
Quality healthcare coverage
Medical, dental, and vision, so you can focus on what matters.
401k / Pensions
Your future matters. Pension and 401k plans to help you get there.
Make an impact beyond Snowplow
Every employee gets five days of paid volunteering per year. Use it to contribute meaningfully to a cause close to your heart.
Extra perks & benefits
Whether you're in the office or working from home, we make sure you're set up to do your best work. We offer a home office budget, team events, and the kit you need to hit the ground running.
