The Snowplow Way
High agency
You have the power to make a difference here directly. When you see a problem or a missed opportunity, you do something about it, and you tell us what’s holding you back so we can remove it. Managers build alongside their teams.
“That’s not my job” isn’t something we say.
Make it count
We’re a data business, and we run on data ourselves. Every piece of work points at a goal that matters, with a number that shows whether it’s working. How you get there is up to you.
Take ownership, and make your efforts count.
Move fast
AI has changed how fast work can move, and we work at that speed. Get it out, learn from it, keep improving. The exception is anything customers trust us with: their data, security and contracts. Those we get right first time.
Try the thing before you write the process.
Challenge, and be open to challenge
Challenge the product, the plan or a leader’s decision, openly, with evidence and a suggestion. Expect the same back, and a straight answer. You can disagree and still treat people with respect.
A good argument from anyone can change where we go.
Make it compound
AI makes each of us faster. That only makes Snowplow faster if what one person works out reaches everyone else. So when you crack something, don’t keep it to yourself. Turn it into a skill, a prompt, a dataset or a tool that others can pick up, and tell people it exists.
Solve it once, for everyone.
Benefits of working at Snowplow
Equity for everyone
Stock options, available to all employees globally. You're not just working here — you're building something you own.
Great work deserves great pay
We set salaries to attract exceptional people — and keep them.
Time off that works for you
Generous holiday allowance and enhanced parental leave for all parents, wherever you are in the world.
Quality healthcare coverage
Medical, dental, and vision, so you can focus on what matters.
401k / Pensions
Your future matters. Pension and 401k plans to help you get there.
Make an impact beyond Snowplow
Every employee gets five days of paid volunteering per year. Use it to contribute meaningfully to a cause close to your heart.
Hybrid working
Hybrid that works for you and for us: come into our London or Boston office when it's useful, with the flexibility to work from home the rest of the time. Some roles are fully remote.
Extra perks & benefits
Whether you're in the office or working from home, we make sure you're set up to do your best work. We offer a home office budget, team events, and the kit you need to hit the ground running.
