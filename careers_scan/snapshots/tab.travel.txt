We’re building the payments and booking platform for tourism businesses
Tab helps hotels, hostels, retreats, tour operators and other tourism businesses get paid by guests from around the world. Our products make it easier to take payments in advance, in person and online, so they spend less time on admin and more time with their guests.
We work with thousands of businesses across Latin America, Africa and Asia, from independent operators to growing hospitality groups. Together, they’ve used Tab to take millions of payments from travellers around the world.
