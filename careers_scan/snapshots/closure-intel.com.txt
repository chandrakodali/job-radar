About Us
We started Closure with one goal: to enable more efficient, effective investigations and prosecution.

How Closure Helps
Closure securely accelerates the review of evidence using state of the art intelligence.
How we build

Why it matters
When justice is delayed, justice is denied. Closure helps combat evidence overload and support an effective justice system.
