Avo exists to inspire better data cultures so teams can build great user experiences.
Data is the backbone of every decision, strategy, and innovation. But what happens when that data is unreliable? Expensive data quality issues aren’t just inconvenient—they’re silently eroding trust, delaying progress, and undermining your ability to deliver results. Most importantly, data quality is increasingly important as part of companies' AI strategy. Because, as highlighted by OpenAI engineer James Betker , "[generative AI] model behavior is not determined by architecture, hyperparameters, or optimizer choices. It’s determined by your dataset, nothing else."
Avo is the leading data governance platform for event based analytics. Data and product teams at large enterprises like Delivery Hero, Fender, IKEA and Wolt use Avo to guarantee their event data quality upstream and get good data, fast. We are backed by world class investors we admire, including Y Combinator, Notable Capital and Heavybit, as well as data, product and engineering leaders at companies such as Google, Shopify and Optimizely.
Our mission has brought together a group of incredibly talented, driven, kind and fun individuals that we are proud of to call our team.
