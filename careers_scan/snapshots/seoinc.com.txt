Culture

A fun, energetic team balanced with focused, strategic hard work to earn top rankings that turn into sales for clients.

Yes. It is a varsity team: hard work is visible and makes a difference for clients.

Yes. SEO Inc. hires from entry-level to senior roles, for people willing to learn and adapt.

Common questions from candidates.
How do I apply for a job at SEO Inc.?
Email your resume, cover letter and case studies to hr@seoinc.com. Mention the position you are applying for.
What positions are open at SEO Inc.?
The careers page lists two openings in Account Management based in Carlsbad, California: SEO Project Manager, and General Inquiry and Career Opportunities.
Where are SEO Inc.’s offices?
SEO Inc. has offices in Nashville, Tennessee; Carlsbad, California; New York City; and Houston, Texas. Current listings are based in Carlsbad.
What kinds of roles does SEO Inc. hire for?
Roles in search engine optimization, social media management, copywriting and PPC management, from entry-level to senior positions.
What experience helps an application?
HTML, management or web experience, plus case studies that show results you have delivered.
Is SEO Inc. an equal opportunity employer?
Yes. SEO Inc. maintains a policy of nondiscrimination and equal employment opportunity for all qualified individuals regardless of race, color, religion, national origin, gender, age, disability, veteran status or any other protected status.

Since 1997. The company was founded by Garry Grant and has 29 years of work in search.

Yes. SEO Inc. is a Google Partner, and its work now covers AI search and GEO alongside SEO and paid search on Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising.
