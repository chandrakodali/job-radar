CAREER AT SARDINA SYSTEMS
How We Hire
We build technology so that others can build more technology.
Sardina Systems builds software to run infrastructure. We are not a consultancy firm that charges you for audits or migration plans. We provide a ready-to-use product that fits every environment, supporting research institutions, institutions, regional cloud hosters, and multinational telecom and GPU-as-a-service providers.
Our flagship product that backs our mission is FishOS, a platform for running cloud infrastructure at scale, built on OpenStack, Kubernetes, and Ceph, along with the automation that ties them together.
This page explains how we hire, who we look for, and what you can expect from us.
Our philosophy
OUR APPROACH
Our Hiring Philosophy
Bring your expertise. Take ownership. See your work through
We do not hire to fill seats. We wait for exceptional people. When the right person shows up, we hire.
We run an evergreen recruitment process. We are always open to meeting strong engineers, even when we are not actively hiring. If you are the right person, we will find a way to work together. If it is not the right time for us, we will tell you straight.
THE PEOPLE WE SEEK
What We Look For
Technical depth
We value deep, defensible expertise in a relevant domain. We look for people who understand the underlying principles of their work, not just the tools and technologies they use. We appreciate those who can explain why something works, challenge existing approaches, and solve complex technical problems.
Initiative & Ownership
We value people who take initiative, identify opportunities, and move things forward without waiting for instructions. We look for individuals who take responsibility for outcomes, improve processes, and are willing to own problems from start to finish.
Long-Term Commitment
We want to build a team that grows together. We value people who are interested in developing their expertise, taking on increasing responsibility, and contributing to the company over the long term.
Evidence of Expertise
We value tangible evidence of expertise: competitive programming (ICPC, Codeforces), Olympiads, hackathons, peer-reviewed publications, strong academic results, relevant certifications, and meaningful open-source contributions. As a commercial company, we value open source, though not all our work can be shared publicly.
Character & Mindset
We value honesty, transparency, intellectual curiosity, and resilience. We want people who are motivated by impact, professional growth, and mastery of their craft. We appreciate constructive disagreement, openness to different perspectives, and the ability to give and receive direct feedback. Above all, we look for people who genuinely want to build something meaningful.
Academic background
We appreciate the depth of technical training that comes with a Master’s degree or PhD, particularly in computer science and related disciplines. However, academic qualifications are considered alongside practical experience and the ability to solve challenging problems. Whether you developed a new algorithm for your thesis or tackled a difficult engineering challenge in a production environment, we’d like to hear about it.
OUR STANDARDS
What We Do Not Hire
We do not hire mercenaries. We do not hire people with contractor history, job-hopping patterns, or a salary-first mindset. We do not hire passive people who wait for instructions or hide behind managers. We do not hire people who are uncoachable: defensive, easily offended, unable to take feedback. We do not hire people who are untrustworthy: evasive, bending the truth, making self-claims without evidence. We do not hire generalists who are broad but shallow, with no deep expertise in any domain. And we do not hire people with no signals: no ICPC, no Codeforces, no papers, no awards, no top-tier GPA.
DAY TO DAY
How We Work
We are a remote-first company with a global team. We hire internationally and collaborate across time zones, with colleagues currently working from GMT-6 to GMT+8.
We value high autonomy. People are expected to identify priorities, define their tasks, and take ownership of their work.
We work across functions and encourage direct communication between teams. We focus on short development cycles, practical outcomes, and collaboration rather than unnecessary bureaucracy.
We encourage people to challenge ideas, share their opinions, and question existing approaches. Once a decision is made, we align and move forward together.
We also support professional development through financial assistance for learning, attending conferences, and speaking at industry events.
We share our progress, priorities, challenges, and plans. We believe that understanding the bigger picture helps everyone make better decisions.
Technology never stands still, and neither do we. We continuously explore new technologies, evaluate their practical value, and incorporate them into our work when they make sense.
GROWTH AT SARDINA
How We Develop People
We believe professional growth comes from doing meaningful work, solving challenging problems, and taking on increasing responsibility. We don’t expect people to have all the answers from day one. We expect them to keep growing.
We hire for potential. And we develop it
