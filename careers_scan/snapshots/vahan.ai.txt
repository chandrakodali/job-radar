Why We Exist
During a visit home to Delhi, Madhav was helping his parents serve food to people on the street when a
young boy approached. After a meal of daal and rice, the boy smiled, hugged him, and walked away. Madhav
couldn’t stop thinking about how much of a person’s future can be shaped simply by where they are born.
That moment sparked Vahan.
In 2016, Madhav started Vahan as an education platform, helping low-income Indians learn English and unlock
better opportunities. But he soon saw a bigger need: helping people find work, faster and simpler. Vahan
evolved from teaching to employment, using technology to connect people with jobs at scale. Today, Vahan
uses AI to make recruitment more human, calling candidates, speaking their language, and guiding them from
opportunity to their first day of work.
Because opportunity shouldn’t depend on where you’re born.
Madhav Krishna,
Founder and CEO, Vahan.
The Journey
2016
Founded in Bengaluru. The first product is Laxmi, a virtual English teacher
you could call on any phone.
2019
Y Combinator (S19), and the pivot to jobs. Among the first companies on the
WhatsApp API. Airtel invests through its Startup Accelerator, opening a reach of 30 crore subscribers.
2022
Named a Technology Pioneer by the World Economic Forum.
2024
$10M Series B led by Khosla Ventures, with Y Combinator and Vijay Shekhar
Sharma participating.
2025
OpenAI partners with Vahan. Persol Group and Temasek’s LemmaTree invest. The voice AI
recruiter goes to work at national scale.
2026
Vahan turns 10. 20 lakh+ workers placed across 900+ cities, with a 5 crore+
worker database.
