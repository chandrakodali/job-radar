How we work
How we think, and what we hire for. Engineering, design, research and commercial alike.
First principles thinking
We always start from ground truths, not “that's what's done”. Regardless of if we’re working on a data schema, a design system, or a pricing model, we always think from first principles.
Bias to Speed
We ship in days not months. When faced with a tough problem, we work out how to build it and then do it. If you like long process documents and planning meetings, you'll hate it here.
No Lanes
Your job title is a rough guess at what you'll actually do. You'll be out of your depth more often than not. Some people find that terrifying. The right people find it fun.
Extreme Ownership
We built the company around people, not process. There are no layers, no approvals, and nobody to escalate to. Which means when you spot a problem, it's your job to fix it.
