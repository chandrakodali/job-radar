What the work is actually like
Four things shape every role here
Outdoor sites, not datacentres
Cameras on masts, perimeter fences, mud and weather. What you ship gets installed somewhere with nobody around.
The device is not on your desk
Once an edge box is in a cabinet in a field, every fix has to land remotely, and land first time.
Bandwidth is a budget
Some links are thin and metered. Where connectivity calls for it, an on-site gateway processes video locally.
A missed event is the failure
Green dashboards are easy. Catching the one intrusion at 03:00 is the bar the whole system is judged on.
