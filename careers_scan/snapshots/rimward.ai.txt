Why Rimward
From wildfires to solar sites
I co-founded Pyronear : open-source wildfire detection on cameras already in place. Some of the sites we watched were solar plants, and their owners kept raising another problem: theft, on sites where nobody is around.
Rimward starts there. First, detection and anticipation that hold up on remote sites. Next, faster response when something happens.
You’d be the first hire: a whole part of the system to own, and direct work with me.
