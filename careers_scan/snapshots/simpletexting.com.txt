Join the SimpleTexting Journey
We’re a global team of over one hundred innovators, creatives, and engineers working together to connect organizations with their audiences using SMS.
Sinch, the Customer Communications Cloud, acquired SimpleTexting in 2021 and has continued to invest in SimpleTexting under the Sinch umbrella. Our aim is to bring businesses and customers closer together with our technology under the Global Sinch SaaS Business Unit.
Sinch powers meaningful conversations at scale across messaging, voice, and email to help businesses deliver unified, personalized experiences that truly revolve around their customers — no matter the channels they use. Over 150,000 businesses, including 8 of the 10 largest tech companies in the world, rely on us for their customer communication needs, with over 700 billion customer engagements each year.
We dream big — for our company, our customers, and our employees — and we hire the best talent worldwide to help us bring our vision to life. We have a local presence in more than 60 countries — probably somewhere near you!

Core Values
Our core values guide everything we do and push the company forward toward its goals.
Dream big
Great things happen when we dare to challenge the status quo and find creative new ways to wow our customers. Driven by curiosity, we believe there is no such thing as failure, only learning.
Win together
We are one global team. We embrace diversity, meritocracy and care about every single individual. We pride ourselves on being inclusive, respectful and sharing our knowledge to help each other grow.
Keep it simple
When our customers think of us, we want them to think simple and easy (the clue is in the name). We like to keep things uncomplicated, down-to-earth and useful. It’s easy to make things complicated; we do the hard work of keeping them simple.
Make it happen
We make things happen and make them matter. We are ‘doers’ who value getting results and avoid overthinking. We stay accountable for our actions and apply the highest ethical standards to everything we do.

Be excited to come to work every day
We’re building SimpleTexting to be a place people love to work. It’s a job that never ends, but we like to think we’ve done pretty well so far. It’s why we’re fully remote, give you ownership over your work, and use flexible vacation to create a work-life “fit”.
Build connections
We’re all about making connections that lead to great things. We care about connecting with our customers, that our customers can connect with their audience, and that our team connects with one another. We’d love to get to know you and see how we can work together.
