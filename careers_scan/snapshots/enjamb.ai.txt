Intelligence is earned.
If you've worked next to someone with decades in their field, you know this. They look at a design and know it's wrong before they can tell you why. They learned it over years of getting it wrong.
General models haven't learned it yet. Their work impresses anyone outside the field, but an expert spots the mistakes in minutes. To that expert, a model with supposedly endless knowledge looks like a well read intern. It turns out knowledge and intuition are different things.
Code was the first field where experts truly marveled at what models could do, and it makes sense that it came first. Code is the basic primitive of computers, the models themselves are made of it, and it has always lived in organized repositories, much of it on GitHub.
In almost every other field, the experience of how people mastered their craft sits locked inside the companies where they did the work, lost to old hardware and external drives, in formats nobody reads anymore, and sometimes even on paper.
We're Enjamb, and we build Earned Intelligence.
We recover the expert work buried inside companies and use it to give foundation models the intuition they can't learn from the open web. For companies, we train custom models on your own history and deploy agents that work alongside your team. These models are owned by you, learn from your own record, and keep improving as your experts work with them.
We're already seeing this with 13 AI and robotics labs. Together we are building the data, environments and evals that give their models intuition for the physical world.
