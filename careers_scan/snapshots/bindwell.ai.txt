The bitter lesson is coming for biology
Throughout history, humanity has run experiments, observed their outputs, and pieced together a representation of the physical world in order to explain what they saw, e.g. protein structure, quantum mechanics, etc. Teaching models these representations doesn't scale.
We train a single model on all raw inputs and outputs of humanity's experiments at scale, allowing it to directly learn its own rich representation that can be used to design new molecules orders of magnitude faster. This is the bitter lesson applied to molecular discovery.
