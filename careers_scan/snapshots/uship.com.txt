uShip was founded in 2004 and promptly became the model for startup culture and community involvement in Austin and Central Texas. Now a mature and successful shipping tech company, uShip has the unique offering of being a small private company that’s maintained what is loved about startup life, but also able to offer the stability, career growth, and benefits of a larger company. Participation is encouraged in our social clubs, DEI and uShip Gives committees, and company events, like our yearly crawfish boil or an afternoon on the lake. We value the authenticity of our employees, and encourage everyone to find and follow their passions while experiencing one of the Top Workplaces in Austin.

Perks and Benefits
HYBRID WORK ENVIRONMENT
Stay remote or come into our downtown office. Reimbursements available to set up and maintain your home office.
Food Delivery Pass & Credit
Enjoy company paid delivery pass plus a monthly credit. Use for lunch or any other delivery available in your area.
Health & Wellness
Competitive health benefits, 100% paid employee option available, FSA, HSA (with company contribution), and monthly wellness reimbursements.
401(k) Matching & Stock Options
No vesting required for 401(k) company matching, equity available to all full-time employees.
Flexible PTO
No need to accrue time to take time off! Company offers 8 paid holidays in addition to your vacation/sick time.
Professional Development
Professional development opportunities available to all. Mentorship programs, book clubs, and access to a world class online learning platform.
