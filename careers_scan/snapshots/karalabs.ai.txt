What it makes possible.
AI & compute
Dense processors concentrate heat in a small area. Diamond heat spreaders offer a material path away from those hot spots.
Thermal management
Spread concentrated heat from active components through diamond plates, submounts and integrated thermal interfaces.
Power electronics
An ultra-wide bandgap and exceptional heat transport make diamond a compelling material for research into high-power devices.
Photonics & optics
Broad optical transmission and heat transport support windows, laser components and photonic structures.
Quantum sensing
Precisely controlled defects, including nitrogen-vacancy centers, enable optical detection of magnetic fields.
