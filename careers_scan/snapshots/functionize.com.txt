Road to full autonomy
Since 2014, Functionize has been pioneering advancements in enterprise automation with AI-driven, cloud-scale innovation.
We’re not just an AI wrapper around a popular LLM like ChatGPT. By taking our own, independent AI-first approach to QA so many years ago, we amassed vast volumes of web application data, allowing us to extensively train our proprietary models. This foundation in AI for QA has given us a unique edge—now, we’re able to apply agentic technology across all web applications.
With years of expertise and a bedrock of proprietary data, we’re equipped to automate even the most complex business processes. Our approach stands apart, built from the ground up to meet today’s automation challenges.
