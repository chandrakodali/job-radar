Questions
How is Wideframe different from AI video generators like Sora or Runway?
Video generators make new footage from a prompt. Wideframe works on the footage you already shot. It watches every clip and hands you bins, selects and Premiere Pro sequences built from your own media.
Does Wideframe replace Premiere Pro?
No. Wideframe writes Premiere Pro projects (.prproj) with your original media linked. You open them in Premiere and do the creative edit, color and export the way you always have.
