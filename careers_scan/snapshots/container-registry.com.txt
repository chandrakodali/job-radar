Join Our Team
Shape the future of container registries with Harbor. Work with cutting-edge technology, contribute to open source, and build solutions that power enterprise infrastructure worldwide.
Why Join Container Registry?
Container Registry offers Harbor-powered container registry as a service for teams and organizations, cloud providers, software vendors, and industrial computing. We are part of the Harbor maintainers team and regularly contribute to project Harbor. Join us in building enterprise-grade container infrastructure.
Our Values
Engineering-Led
We are an engineering-led team with technical founders who love building software and services for software engineers.
End-to-End Ownership
We love to take end-to-end ownership for every task — from concept to delivery.
Perfection & Pride
We strive for perfection and take pride in the solutions we build — no shortcuts, no compromises.
Multidisciplinary Team
As a small and versatile team of multidisciplinary engineers, we bring diverse skills to every challenge.
How We Work
We enjoy high freedom and accept the high responsibility that comes with making your own decisions for better software.
Independent — We have the freedom to elaborate and define work out of concepts and brainstorming sessions with clients and peers.
Asynchronously — We structure our work in such a way that it does not stop while waiting for others to provide us with deliverables or clarification.
We embrace Agile software development principles — without worshiping SCRUM.
