how we hire
A short loop, run by the people you'll work with.
A 30-minute intro. Founder, not a recruiter. We talk about what you're working on now and why.
A real problem. We send you a small piece of an actual codebase or research question. Paid, time-boxed, async. No whiteboard riddles.
A working day. You spend one day with us — remote or in person — pairing on something live. You decide as much as we do.
An offer, fast. Decisions within 48 hours. Cash + meaningful equity + product profit-share. Transparent bands published before the offer.
who we look for
Three patterns we consistently bet on.
pattern 0 1
The shipper-researcher.
You read papers and you write production code. You know what a CUDA kernel costs and what a unit test costs, and you treat both with the same seriousness.
pattern 0 2
The dangerously productive generalist.
You move across the stack — model, infra, product, distribution — with disproportionate output. Title is irrelevant; throughput is everything.
pattern 0 3
The founder-in-residence.
You want to start a company on top of the agent stack and you'd like the runway, technical leverage, and design discipline of a small lab while you do it.
open roles
No live requisitions — but the inbound is real.
We're not running open requisitions right now. We're a small team executing against a long roadmap, and we'd rather wait six months for the right person than ship a mediocre hire this quarter.
That said, if you're already an exceptional engineer, researcher, or founder, and you'd want to work here even though there's no listing — that's exactly the kind of email we want to receive. Use the subject line inbound so it gets routed correctly.

what you'll get
What hiring actually means here.
Cash competitive with senior FAANG, plus meaningful equity. Transparent bands published before any offer.
Profit share on the product you work on. If Mercury makes money, Mercury's team makes money.
Remote-first, async-default. Two synchronous touchpoints per week, max. Optional quarterly off-sites.
A real research budget. Conferences, papers, hardware, model credits — funded without a form.
No internal politics. We are too small for it and the founder is allergic to it.
