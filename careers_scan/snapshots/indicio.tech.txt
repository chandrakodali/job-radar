Our Leadership Principles
Trust & Transparency 🔎
We trust each other to do what’s right and we trust the decisions being made at all levels of the company. We are transparent and share company information with teams regularly.
Respect & Empowerment 🤝
We are each empowered to make decisions and we respect those made by others. When compromises need to be made, we consider all opinions and do so with kindness. We believe in the ability of all our teammates to get their work done how, when, and where they feel most productive. We treat each other and our customers with the utmost respect.
Learning & Mentorship 📚
We spend time and resources for the personal growth and education of all team members, our customers, and the decentralized identity community. Through continuous learning, mentorship, and feedback, we all improve together.
Celebrate 🎉
We are a joyful and happy company. We love where we work and celebrate our accomplishments! We are tenacious and positive in our work together and with customers–even during challenging times. We celebrate our differences and take the time to get to know each other and all the aspects that make us special.
Impact 🏆
Every teammate is a leader, directly influences the success of Indicio, and understands the importance of this technology for humanity. We recognize we have power and responsibility as we develop this new technology. We can’t anticipate all its uses, but we seek to reduce unintended consequences.
Inclusion 🫂
We are agents of change, working together to ensure both Indicio and our industry are safe places to work where every person belongs. As a Public Benefit Corporation, we serve worldwide communities in positive and inclusive ways.
Open Roles
We highly encourage candidates of all backgrounds to apply – we recruit based on more than just official qualifications, including non-technical experience, initiative, and curiosity. We create a welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and equitable environment for all.
