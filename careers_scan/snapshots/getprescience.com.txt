prescience, inc.
Hippocrates believed that human health was a predictable phenomenon. In Epidemics , he wrote “Declare the past , diagnose the present , foretell the future .”
The modern business of healthcare has been built on the opposite premise. Insurance was designed for catastrophes; payment was structured around reimbursement; physicians intervene after the damage is done. Our healthcare system surrendered to the belief that health and disease is unpredictable. We believe that abundant intelligence will bring forth prescience . We can now model human behavior, decode systems biology, and anticipate risk with a precision that would have been impossible a generation ago.
Prescience is building the Foundation of a prescient healthcare system.
