Founding Engineer
The Role:
As a Founding Engineer at Denki, you'll help architect and build our core learning platform from the ground up. You'll work directly with our technical founders to implement crucial features, establish engineering best practices, and create scalable systems that power our AI-driven educational tools.
What You'll Do:
Design and implement core features of our AI-powered learning platform
Collaborate with AI researchers to integrate advanced machine learning models
Build robust, scalable backend services and intuitive frontend experiences
Help establish engineering culture, coding standards, and technical decision-making processes
Work across the full stack to deliver exceptional user experiences
What We're Looking For:
3+ years of experience in software engineering, preferably in startups or fast-paced environments
Strong proficiency in modern web technologies (React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js)
Experience building and deploying production systems at scale
Knowledge of machine learning and AI integration is a plus
Passion for education and technology's potential to transform learning
Ability to thrive in ambiguity and rapidly evolving environments
