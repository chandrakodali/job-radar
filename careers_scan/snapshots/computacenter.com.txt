Our business is about technology. But first of all, it’s about people.
We are a trusted, global provider of technology with a success story that spans over 40 years. Of our 21,000 people worldwide, over 4,000 are in the UK, sourcing, transforming and managing the latest digital technology to some of the world's greatest organisations. We have built powerful partnerships with some of the world's leading technology providers, such as Microsoft, Apple, IBM, HP Enterprise, Cisco and Intel. Together, we deliver solutions across the domains of Workplace, Applications & Data, Cloud & Data Center, Networking and Security.
The secret of our success is down to our people. People with a winning mindset and diverse experience, skills and ways of thinking enables them to understand and reflect our customers and provide them with the best possible service. Join us and you will receive the support when you need it and the autonomy when you don't. You will join an environment that offers endless opportunities for you to achieve your ambitions if you put your mind to it. Be part of the Computacenter family (and it does feel like a family) and be inspired by innovation, recognised for your contribution and be constantly challenged to try something new.
If this sounds like the perfect environment for you, join the team and realise your full potential!

A note on recruitment fraud
Computacenter does not use messaging platforms such as WhatsApp, Telegram, or Snapchat to advertise roles or approach candidates. We do not ask candidates to pay any fees or complete paid tasks at any point in the recruitment process, and we do not make offers of employment without conducting a proper selection process.
All genuine Computacenter vacancies are listed on this website. If you are unsure whether a communication is legitimate, please contact our recruitment team directly before responding.
