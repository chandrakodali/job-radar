What is xPay?
xPay is a cross-border payments company. We help B2B SaaS businesses move money globally without the usual friction. We're YC-backed, started in 2024, and we're already live in 180+ countries and 30+ currencies, processing over a million transactions every month. Cross-border payments is a $200 trillion market, and companies are already switching to us from billion-dollar incumbents.

Why join xPay?
We're bringing Valley vibes to Bangalore: singular focus and high agency, with room to keep growing. You can go deep as a specialist in your domain while still getting real exposure across every other part of the business — because here, learning is limitless, and nobody's boxed into one silo.
Right now, xPay is small enough that you're not one of many — you're one of the first. No hierarchy to climb, no red tape, no layers between you and any team or vertical: you'll work directly with everyone, from week one. We're going after something huge — processing $2 billion a day — and if you join now, you'll have helped build the engine, not just ridden it.
