We continuously grow our benefits roster to help our team enriching lives as part of the GBattizan team.
Minimum paid vacation time
We provide a minimum of two weeks of paid time off. We strongly encourage our team to at least take two weeks off each year, the rest is up to you.
100% insurance coverage
We cover your insurance needs, specifically health, dental, vision, and life, for you and your dependents.
No counting of sick days
A happy and health team is our priority. We encourage taking time off when you’re not feeling your best.
Company closure for the holidays
The holidays are a great time to spend time with loved ones so we pause our operations over the holidays so we can enjoy some well-deserved time off.
Offsites and outings
Every year we get together to celebrate our achievements and get some down time as a team in a relaxing setting. Pre COVID-19 our last outing was at a retreat at Mont Tremblant.
Choose your own adventure

Office setup budget
