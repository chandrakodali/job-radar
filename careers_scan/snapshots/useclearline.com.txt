Mission
Fix the customer work dealerships lose every day
Calls come in after hours. Conversations cross sales and service. Follow-ups get missed, context gets lost, and revenue disappears without anyone seeing exactly where it went.
Clearline gives dealerships one AI-native system that understands customer intent and takes action across calls, messages, appointments, and follow-up. It helps great teams respond faster and keep work from falling through the cracks.
