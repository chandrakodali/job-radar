Own Golpo AI’s revenue engine across direct sales, partnerships, and customer expansion.
The mission
Build qualified pipeline, close real B2B SaaS business, and create a partner channel that produces repeatable revenue—not just meetings or signed agreements.
What you’ll own
Generate pipeline through targeted outbound outreach, inbound qualification, referrals, events, and partner channels.
Own the complete sales lifecycle: account research, discovery, tailored demos, trials, proposals, negotiation, closing, and initial customer handoff.
Build Golpo’s partner ecosystem across agencies, consultants, resellers, education partners, and other strategic channels.
Enable partners, develop joint opportunities, support co-selling, and turn partner relationships into measurable revenue.
Expand and retain high-potential accounts through upgrades, wider organizational adoption, and recurring usage.
Maintain disciplined pipeline records, next actions, close dates, and forecasts; every active opportunity must have a clear next step.
Bring customer objections, competitive intelligence, and product feedback back to the founders and product team.
What we’re looking for
A proven record of personally sourcing and closing B2B SaaS, AI, or technology-product deals—not only managing accounts or influencing pipeline.
Strong prospecting, discovery, product-demo, commercial negotiation, and closing skills.
Experience selling into global markets; startup or emerging-product experience is strongly preferred.
The ability to build productive partner relationships and create opportunities from scratch.
High ownership, urgency, organization, coachability, and accountability for targets and commitments.
Comfort working hands-on without depending on a separate SDR, presales, or partnership-support team for every step.
How success is measured
Direct new revenue against target
Partner-sourced pipeline and revenue
Qualified pipeline coverage and stage movement
Conversion, retention, and account expansion
Forecast accuracy and follow-up discipline
We measure outcomes—not vanity metrics, activity for activity’s sake, or impressive-looking reports without business impact.
