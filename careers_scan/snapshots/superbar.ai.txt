How we work
Four operating principles.
Creativity is the job .
AI handles the rote work — taste, instinct, and original thinking are what's left. Everyone here is expected to bring all three, every day.
Merchant-first, always .
Every roadmap call starts with a real brand we work with. If it doesn't move their numbers, it waits.
Uncomfortably fast .
Ideas ship the same week, sometimes the same day. If a decision takes more than one meeting, we're already moving too slow.
Craft as a default .
Motion, type, copy, code — we sweat the details because shoppers feel them, even when they can't name them.
