About Univention
With our open source solutions, we make central and effective Identity & Access Management (IAM) and the integration of IT applications for the education sector, public administration and companies secure and compliant with data protection regulations. Openness, scalability and the simple use of IT are just as important to us as digital sovereignty and control over your own data and processes.
