I . Why we build
We live in an age of infinite leverage.
We believe humans will direct hundreds of agents each.
That makes human judgment more important than it has ever been.
Yet no platform today balances the power of agents with a clear view of what they actually did. We’re building the one that does: where judging the work is as simple as it can be.
II . What we're building
The agentic experience, with a human in the loop.
Vesence is one desktop for documents, spreadsheets, decks and email. Agents work across all of it in parallel, and everything they do comes back as a change you can read, check, and accept or reject.
III . Who uses it
Used by thousands of people at some of the world’s best firms.
Investors, law firms and enterprises do their most important work in Vesence, from the managing partner to the newest associate.
