05 / The economics One disruption. Multiple consequences.
Economics
The cost of acting late.
A single disruption triggers costs across multiple lines. They compound fast:
Detention & demurrage per container, per day
Equipment premiums surcharges on scarce capacity
Rebooking & reallocation new carrier, new window, new rate
Downstream delays warehouse, last-mile, production
Customer impact SLA penalties, service recovery
On PO-4471, equipment surcharge alone ran $150–500/TEU . Ekho helps teams make the call earlier, before these costs stack.
