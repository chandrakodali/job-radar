"I left my corporate career at Bain to teach personal finance after seeing the impact of the 2008 financial recession on my community, family, and friends. SmartPath was founded to help everyday people and families get objective financial guidance with personalized support and accountability.
Still today, our mission is to educate people about money, without selling any financial products, in an approachable and authentic way that meets them where they are on their financial journey.
SmartPath continues to help individuals and families gain confidence and alleviate the financial stress that comes from lack of access or times of uncertainty. It’s been a dream come true helping people move from chaos to confidence with their money."
Alok Deshpande
SmartPath CEO and Co-Founder

Our Core Values
Authenticity
We know who we are.
We won’t try to be someone we’re not - with our clients or with our colleagues. This means we treat others with respect, assume the best, and share direct feedback. Honesty makes our team stronger and our product better.
Simplicity
We teach finances (without the fanfare).
We’re not here to overcomplicate anything or outsmart anyone. We deal with complex ideas and topics, but when you actually know something, you should be able to easily explain it to a middle schooler. No big words, no posturing - just the facts.
Purpose
We love our work.
Nothing corny here. We enjoy helping others succeed. We are proud of the way our work changes lives and we celebrate these achievements every day.
Autonomy
Everyone drives (but no one drives alone).
We are a community of leaders. We own our work and empower others to own theirs. We’re focused on solutions, delineate responsibilities clearly, and control the clock.
Adaptability
We don’t think curiosity actually kills the cat.
We value curiosity and are unafraid to fail. We try to stay nimble and not waste time on what-ifs - we are decisive but can pivot with ease.
Tenacity
If there is no wind, we row.
We value hard work but accept setbacks. We’re focused on results, learning, and improving next time - we don’t cry over spilled milk.
Mastery
We’re all learning to fish here.
We’ve all heard it: “If you teach a man to fish…” We’re committed to learning new skills and pushing our limits even if (especially if) it’s uncomfortable.
