EQUALITY, DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION
We at BIOS respect and believe in the power of diversity. It is our differing backgrounds, personalities, skills, experiences, thoughts and perspectives that help us generate creative approaches and innovative solutions to our hard and challenging work. We embrace a culture of diversity which recognises that unique individuals are what deliver the ingenuity and strength of our team. We therefore want to attract a diverse talent pool and welcome applications from those who are currently underrepresented in the sector, including women, people from minority ethnic backgrounds, and people with disabilities. We encourage all candidates to reflect on their strengths and experience in the broadest sense, including transferable skills, when considering their suitability for a position.
BIOS is committed to equal employment opportunities. All qualified applicants will receive equal treatment regardless of age, disability, gender reassignment, marital or civil partner status, pregnancy or maternity, race, nationality, ethnic or national origin, religion or belief, gender or sexual orientation.
OUR INTERVIEW
PROCESS
This is very much a two-way process – a chance for you to find out about BIOS and the role, and for us to find out about you and your skills and experience. Here is an overview of our interview process:
INTRO CHAT
(We are able to offer interviews in-person or via Zoom)
A 30-minute call for us to get to know each other a bit and see if both parties think there is a good fit for the role.
CHALLENGE & PRESENTATION
Whether you are applying for a technical or non-technical position, we like to see your core capabilities first-hand. Every candidate is asked to complete and submit an at-home challenge after which you may be invited to meet with a group of us for an in-depth interview and a small group presentation (Usually 2-3 Hours).
MEET OUR CEO/FOUNDERS
Our founders interview everyone coming into the company at every level. If both sides feel there is a great fit and we are considering making you an offer, we will arrange for you to have a call or meeting with Oliver and Emil.
OFFER!
If it’s still all systems go, a written offer will be made. After that it’s just paperwork and agreeing a start date. We’ll be really excited to welcome you to the team by this point!

OUR INTERNSHIP PROGRAM
Each summer we offer a paid intern programme. These are designed to enable current students in relevant fields to translate their skills and expertise into an industry setting. Interns will have a dedicated supervisor and work on actual company projects designed to make the best use of their background and skills whilst developing their knowledge and experience. We will support all interns to produce a concrete output by the end of the internship which culminates in a presentation to the wider company.
