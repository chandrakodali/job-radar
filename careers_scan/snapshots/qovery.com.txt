01 Our values
What we stand for .
We mean every word of this. It's how we hire, work, and make choices every day. If you feel the same way, you'll fit right in.
Customer-obsessed
Our customers are more important than anything else. They are at the core of why we build Qovery. When a user requests help, we react as soon as we can and do all we can to make them successful. Taking care of them is as important as building an outstanding product.
Getting shit done
Focus on work and impact. Ask yourself if what you're doing makes a huge impact on Qovery. Under-promise and over-deliver. Plan and execute. Done is 100x better than perfect. Few meetings - only when necessary with real actions at the end.
Collaboration & respect
Be positive and friendly. Be a team player. Empower your colleagues. We believe the best ideas come from diverse perspectives working together in a culture of mutual respect.
Resilience
You never give up. You fight until you achieve your goals. Your mindset is that there are no impossible situations - but you stay realistic. Setbacks are learning opportunities, not stop signs.
Strive for excellence
Never settle for "good." Good is not enough. Hire awesome but humble people. Never stop learning and sharing with others. We push each other to raise the bar - every day.
Creativity: be a hustler
Sometimes there is no direct path to success. Workarounds are much better than being stuck in limbo. Use your creative mindset to achieve your goals and share your hustler energy with your colleagues.
Having fun
This is most of your waking life - do it well and enjoy it. We run hackathons, Beer & Tech sessions, conferences, open-source projects, innovation time, and internal mobility programs. You drive things bottom-up.
