Bangalore Hybrid · Full-time
Company Description
Atrisa (YC P26) builds AI agents to accelerate, debug, and automate analog circuit design. Our AI copilot helps engineers design circuits, run simulations, and debug failures, shortening the path from a specification to a working design.
Role Description
This is a full-time, hybrid role in Bangalore. You'll design, analyze, and verify analog and mixed-signal circuits, and work with our software and AI engineers to bring that work into Atrisa.
Design and simulate analog blocks, balancing performance, power, and area.
Investigate circuit behavior, debug simulation failures, and check designs across operating conditions.
Review schematics and layouts, and contribute to silicon validation.
Turn practical design problems into examples and tests that help our AI tools improve.
Qualifications
At least 3 years of experience in analog or mixed-signal circuit design.
A strong foundation in analog and mixed-signal circuit design.
Hands-on experience with industry EDA tools and SPICE simulation.
The ability to reason through circuit tradeoffs and explain what is causing a design to fail.
A bachelor's or master's degree in electrical engineering or a closely related field.
An interest in using AI and automation in circuit design.
Experience with silicon bring-up and lab validation is a plus. We're open to visa sponsorship.
