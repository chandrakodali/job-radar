Life at NextPay
A Few Things About Working Here
We’re a small, remote team, so good ideas can become real work quickly. We share context early, ask questions when something does not add up, and stay close to the businesses we build for.
Ship, Learn, Repeat
Turn ideas into useful work, get feedback from real users, and keep improving. See a better way to do something? Say so. We value people who spot a problem, suggest a fix, and help see it through.
A Little Help From AI
At NextPay, AI helps with the repetitive parts of work so you can spend more time thinking, creating, and solving. Every team member gets access to AI tools, whether they work in tech or not.
Built for Real Work
Our customers run businesses, pay employees and suppliers, and follow up on invoices. We build around the problems they face every day, not vanity metrics.
Sweat the Small Stuff
Payments affect people’s workdays and livelihoods. We check the details, raise concerns early, and look out for one another when the work gets tricky.

Perks That Have Your Back
Good work takes focus, flexibility, and a little breathing room. These are the practical ways we support the people doing it.
Fully Remote Work Setup
Do your best work from where you work best. We are remote-first and keep it practical with clear documentation, regular check-ins, and handoffs that do not leave you guessing. We care about outcomes, not office attendance, so you can skip Metro Manila traffic and get things done in your pambahay.
Unlimited PTO + HMO
Work matters, but so do doctor’s appointments, proper breaks, and the occasional reset. You get HMO coverage and unlimited paid time off, so you can take care of yourself and recharge when you need to.
Goodie Fridays
Fridays come with a small pick-me-up through Giftaway. You choose what you will enjoy, because one person’s comfort food is another person’s book, coffee, or game credit.
Equipment Support
We support the tools and setup you need to stay focused and comfortable, whether your desk is at home, in a café, or somewhere in between.
