We were insomniacs, tired of waiting for solutions that came too late. Stellar Sleep was born from that frustration.
We worked with sleep experts, converting our shared experiences into a digital solution. In 2021, we started as a venture at Harvard Innovation Labs to make our solution available to the world. Today we operate the largest network of insomnia clinicians in the US.*
