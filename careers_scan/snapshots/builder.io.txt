Our founders
Steve Sewell and Brent Locks met at UC Berkeley in 2011, bonding over having plenty of ideas and no way to code them yet.
A decade later, Steve is a visionary technologist (Brent’s words) and Brent still tops out at HTML and CSS. The shared goal hasn’t changed: let anyone bring an idea to life.

Actually remote. We are (mostly) headquartered in the San Francisco Bay Area and some roles are hybrid there, but the company operates digital-first with team members distributed across the world. Remote team members are not second-class participants.
We hire across multiple locations, and each posting lists the regions it is open to. If a role looks like a fit but the location is unclear, ask in the first conversation — we will tell you plainly whether we can hire where you are.
Every posting carries a range. Compensation may vary based on location and is discussed early in the hiring process, so nobody wastes weeks before finding a mismatch. Final offers are based on experience, role-related knowledge, skills, and qualifications demonstrated through the interview process.
Real equity, on a four year vesting schedule with a one year cliff. We also have a ten year exercise window, which means leaving Builder does not force you into an immediate decision about exercising your options.
Typically a few weeks end to end, depending on your availability. The process is five stages: application review, an intro conversation, a craft deep dive, meeting the team, and an offer with references. We move quickly on decisions and will tell you where you stand.
