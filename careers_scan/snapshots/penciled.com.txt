Partnership Program
Clinical Partner Program
Looking for a role outside of patient care? You understand how therapists think, evaluate software, and what frustrates them about EMRs. We need that perspective. Our Clinical Partner Program lets you leverage your clinical experience in the world of healthcare technology—while earning competitive commissions.
As a partner, you'll work closely with our team to introduce Penciled to clinics in your network and region. We'll train you on field sales and door-to-door operations, and give you access to clean, pre-qualified go-to-market data on target clinics. You bring the clinical credibility and connections—we bring the product, data, and support.
Top performers earn full-time positions
This isn't just a side gig. Partners who demonstrate strong results and alignment with our mission will be offered full-time roles on our growing team. It's a real pathway out of treating and into healthcare tech.
How you can contribute
• Outreach to clinics and demos from the clinician perspective
• Bridge between our product team and real-world clinical needs
• Connect us with classmates and colleagues across outpatient, hospital, and private practice settings
• Provide feedback on EMR pain points and clinic workflows
What you get
Earn commission on closed deals
Comprehensive field sales training program
Access to pre-qualified clinic data in your territory
Direct mentorship from our leadership team
Flexible hours—work around your clinical or class schedule
Pathway to full-time employment for top performers
Deep insight into PT practice operations, cash flow, and business models
Relationships with clinic owners and decision-makers
Opportunities for in-person ride-alongs with our team
Who should apply
• Licensed PTs, OTs, and PTAs looking to explore opportunities beyond treating
• DPT, OTD, or PTA students who want to expand their skillset
• Therapists with experience across outpatient, hospital, home health, or private practice
• Interest in healthcare technology, business, and software
• Strong network of classmates or colleagues in the rehab industry
Applications reviewed on a rolling basis
