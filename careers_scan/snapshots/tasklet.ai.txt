About Tasklet
Better models are only the beginning.
We believe every company will have AI employees. Not another chatbot to prompt or a workflow to babysit, but teammates with real responsibilities: closing the books, keeping a sales pipeline moving, helping customers, and following through on the work between messages. Tasklet is building the product that makes those employees possible.
Our vision is a team of virtual teammates that learn your business, collaborate with your people, and follow through around the clock. Customers already build custom agents, connect their business tools, and work with them in Tasklet, Slack, email, and text. We’re making it easier for an entire company to work with those agents—and for the people responsible to understand and control what they do.
The hard part is everything between a capable model and a colleague you can depend on. We combine models from multiple labs, swarms of parallel workers, cloud computers, and connections to business systems in one agent harness. Then we make that power understandable: what an agent owns, what it did, when it needs help, and what it is allowed to do next.
The AI labs are racing to build better models. We’re racing to turn that intelligence into a workforce businesses can actually rely on. We can use the best technology from any lab and focus our entire company on that problem. This is a chance to help define a new category of software—not add one more feature to the old one.
A small team with a proven track record.
Our team of 14 is based in San Francisco and is led by founders Andrew Lee (formerly founded Firebase & sold it to Google) and Jonny Dimond and includes Will Chambers , Hiranya Jayathilaka , Michael Lehenbauer , Michael Latman , Parker Chun , Christiana Stubblefield , Tyler Rockwood , Mark Robinson , Geoff Hamilton , Austin Haag , Brennan Murphy , and Dan Sundberg .
We’ve built the kinds of systems this moment demands: agent systems, cloud infrastructure, and productivity tools. That experience comes from building Shortwave , Firebase , and critical infrastructure at Google, Amazon, OpenAI, Salesforce, and elsewhere. Here, you’ll work directly with the people who built them—and own a meaningful piece of what comes next.
We run the company on our own AI employees. They handle real work in recruiting, support, and operations, and engineers direct parallel agents every day. If work can be delegated to an agent, we delegate it.
Backed with $42M from world-class investors.
We’ve raised over $42M from Union Square Ventures , Lightspeed Venture Partners , Y Combinator , Jeff Dean , Patrick & John Collison and others. Our most recent funding round was raised with a valuation cap of $500M.
Culture
A high-performance, results-focused culture.
Tasklet is a fast-paced, engineering-driven, results-oriented company. We aim to build a truly world-class team & an amazing work environment. These are the values we aspire to:
Understand our users & solve their A+ problems
You focus on the problems that really matter — not the ones you already know how to solve. You take responsibility for delivering experiences that our users love — end-to-end. This requires a deep understanding of our users, their workflows, and how AI agents can transform them.
Relentlessly pursue a vision for excellence, but ship today
In the world of AI agents, things change rapidly, and we intend to set the pace. You iterate with extreme urgency, shipping improvements daily that compound into transformative capabilities over time. You aim high, know where you're going, and don't stop until it's amazing.
Lead
You accomplish far more through teamwork than you could individually. You enable teamwork by being a leader, and you lead by doing, not just by talking.
Take initiative
You act like an owner and make great things happen without being asked. You try to be the change you want to see in the world. You believe nothing is someone else's problem.
Be honest & truth seeking
You’re committed to finding & communicating the truth. You follow evidence, not your ego. You speak your mind and let others speak theirs.
Deliver business results
You know that results — not effort — are what counts, and you take responsibility for our hardest & most important work: figuring out what needs to be done to get the right results. You own the results you produce, including your failures.
