Benefits
Not your average software company
Work with React Native contributors and open-source tool builders on product development and agentic delivery projects that stretch your skills. Collaborate with teammates who share knowledge and own what they ship. And that’s only the beginning of a long list of perks of working at Callstack.
Remote-first by design
Work from wherever you do your best work. Our team spans 10+ countries, and remote is the default, not a perk. Some roles may require hybrid or in-office work, but unless it’s in the job description, you won’t be asked to return just because.
Workations and team events worth showing up for
From team-wide workations to internal meetups, we make space for deep work, real connection, and a little adventure. Each Callstacker gets a one-week workation in a villa somewhere in southern Europe.
Wellbeing & Development Budget
Use this flexible budget to invest in your growth, learning, and overall well-being, from courses to physiotherapy.
We’ve got your back, at work and beyond
Fully covered life insurance
We care about your future, which includes support for you and your family when it matters most.
Time off that actually supports you
You get 24 paid days off per year, plus up to 30 days of paid sick leave. No games, no stress-we want you to rest when you need to.
Private healthcare
We co-finance private healthcare plans for you and your family or provide an equivalent if you're outside Poland.
Sports allowance
Whether it’s a Multisport card or local equivalent, we help cover the cost of staying active.
Mental health support
Free access to the Helping Hand platform, including webinars and one-on-one sessions with licensed specialists.
Milestone Five Anniversary Program
Stay with us long-term and we’ll celebrate it with a special benefit made just for you.
Accounting cost refund
We help cover your accounting costs if you're working with us on a B2B contract.
Agent Conf participation
You’ll have the chance to take part in Agent Conf and learn how engineers build and ship software with AI agents.
Top-notch hardware
We’ll set you up with Apple gear so you can work comfortably and efficiently from day one.
